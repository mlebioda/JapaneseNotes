# **Phrases and expressions – station / metro**

#w 切符を買う（きっぷをかう）- to buy a ticket | The action of purchasing a train or bus ticket.  
#wc 乗り換える（のりかえる）- to transfer (change lines/trains) | To switch from one train or line to another during a journey.  
#wc 乗る（のる）- to get on (train, bus) | To board a vehicle such as a train or bus.  
#wc 降りる（おりる）- to get off | To exit a vehicle such as a train or bus.  
#wc 遅れる（おくれる）- to be late / delayed | To arrive later than scheduled.  
#wc 聞く（きく）- to ask / to listen | To pose a question, or to listen to something.  
#wc 探す（さがす）- to look for / search | To try to find something.  
#wc 行く（いく）- to go | To move toward a destination.  
#wc 来る（くる）- to come | To move toward the speaker or a specified place.  
#wc 歩く（あるく）- to walk | To move on foot.  
#wc 待つ（まつ）- to wait | To remain in place until something happens.  
#wc 見つける（みつける）- to find | To locate something that was being searched for.  
#wc 迷う（まよう）- to get lost / be confused (direction) | To lose one's way or be unsure which direction to take.

# **Nouns (used in station questions)**

#w 出口（でぐち）- exit | The way out of a station or building.  
#w 入口（いりぐち）- entrance | The way into a station or building.  
#w 乗り場（のりば）- boarding area | The designated spot where passengers board a train or bus.  
#w 改札（かいさつ）- ticket gate | The gate where tickets are checked when entering or leaving a station.  
#w ホーム（ほーむ）- platform | The area alongside the tracks where passengers wait for and board trains.  
#w 切符売り場（きっぷうりば）- ticket counter | The counter or machine area where tickets are purchased.  
#w 駅員（えきいん）- station staff | An employee who works at a train station.  
#w 時刻表（じこくひょう）- timetable | A schedule showing departure and arrival times.  
#w 方面（ほうめん）- direction (e.g. 渋谷方面 – toward Shibuya) | Indicates the general direction or destination area a train is headed.  
#w 先（さき）- ahead / next | Refers to a point further along in space or time.  
#w 階段（かいだん）- stairs | A series of steps connecting different levels of a station.  
#w エレベーター（えれべーたー）- elevator | A machine that carries people between floors.  
#w エスカレーター（えすかれーたー）- escalator | A moving staircase that carries people between floors.

# **Adjectives (#wp)**

#wp 近い（ちかい）- near | Close in distance.  
#wp 遠い（とおい）- far | Distant.  
#wp 速い（はやい）- fast | Happening or moving quickly.  
#wp 遅い（おそい）- slow / late | Happening slowly, or arriving later than expected.  
#wp 分かりやすい（わかりやすい）- easy to understand | Clear and simple to grasp.  
#wp 便利（べんり）- convenient | Easy and useful to use.  
#wp 不便（ふべん）- inconvenient | Difficult or troublesome to use.  
#wp 安い（やすい）- cheap | Costing little money.  
#wp 高い（たかい）- expensive | Costing a lot of money.

# **Useful questions – typical station situations**

#w この電車は（しぶや）行きですか？ - Does this train go to (Shibuya)? | Asked to confirm a train's destination.  
#w どのホームから乗りますか？ - From which platform do I get on? | Asked when unsure which platform to board from.  
#w 乗り換えはどこですか？ - Where do I transfer? | Asked when looking for the place to change trains.  
#w 次の電車は何時ですか？ - What time is the next train? | Asked when checking the next departure time.  
#w 切符はどこで買えますか？ - Where can I buy a ticket? | Asked when looking for where to purchase a ticket.  
#w どっちの方向ですか？ - Which direction is it? | Asked when unsure which way to go.  
#w 出口はどこですか？ - Where is the exit? | Asked when looking for the way out.  
#w トイレはどこですか？ - Where is the toilet? | Asked when looking for a restroom.  
#w この線で（しんじゅく）に行けますか？ - Can I get to Shinjuku on this line? | Asked to confirm whether a line reaches a given destination.  
#w この電車は急行ですか？ - Is this an express train? | Asked to confirm whether a train is an express service.  
#w 次の駅はどこですか？ - What's the next station? | Asked when checking the name of the upcoming stop.

---

# **Useful answers – reactions / information**

#w はい、行けます。- Yes, you can. | An affirmative reply confirming that something is possible.  
#w いいえ、乗り換えが必要です。- No, you need to transfer. | A reply explaining that a transfer is required.  
#w あのホームです。- That platform (over there). | A reply pointing out a specific platform.  
#w 改札を出て右です。- After the ticket gate, it's to the right. | A reply giving directions relative to the ticket gate.  
#w 二つ目の駅です。- It's the second station. | A reply indicating how many stops away a destination is.  
#w 今来ます。- It's coming now. | A reply indicating that a train is arriving imminently.  
#w もうすぐです。- It's soon / almost here. | A reply indicating that something will happen very shortly.

TARGET DECK: Japanese-Vocabulary::Post-Vocabulary

#w 郵便局（ゆうびんきょく）- post office | The building or institution where mail and parcels are handled.  
#w 郵便（ゆうびん）- mail / postal service | The system for sending and delivering letters and parcels.  
#w 郵便物（ゆうびんぶつ）- postal item / mail | Any letter or parcel sent through the postal system.  
#w 郵便番号（ゆうびんばんごう）- postal code / ZIP code | The numeric code identifying a specific delivery area.  
#w 配達（はいたつ）- delivery | The act of bringing mail or a parcel to its destination.  
#w 速達（そくたつ）- express mail | A faster mail service for quicker delivery, at extra cost.  
#w 書留（かきとめ）- registered mail | A mail service that tracks and guarantees delivery of an item.  
#w 航空便（こうくうびん）- airmail | Mail sent by airplane, typically faster than surface mail.  
#w 船便（ふなびん）- surface mail (by ship) | Mail sent by sea, slower but cheaper than airmail.  
#w 小包（こづつみ）- parcel / package | A wrapped item sent through the mail.  
#w 宅配便（たくはいびん）- home delivery service (courier) | A courier service that delivers parcels directly to homes.  
#w EMS（いーえむえす）- international express mail | Japan Post's fast international shipping service.  
#w 送り状（おくりじょう）- shipping label / invoice | The label attached to a parcel listing sender, recipient, and contents.  
#w 受取人（うけとりにん）- recipient | The person who receives a mailed item.  
#w 差出人（さしだしにん）- sender | The person who sends a mailed item.  
#w 宛名（あてな）- address (of recipient) | The recipient's name and address written on an item being sent.  
#w 切手（きって）- postage stamp | A small adhesive label showing that postage has been paid.  
#w はがき - postcard | A card sent by mail without an envelope, often with an image on one side.  
#w 封筒（ふうとう）- envelope | A paper covering used to enclose and mail a letter.  
#w 料金（りょうきん）- fee / postage cost | The cost charged for sending mail or a parcel.  
#w 窓口（まどぐち）- service counter | The window where customers are served by staff in person.  
#w 住所（じゅうしょ）- address | The location where a person lives or where mail should be delivered.  
#w 荷物（にもつ）- package / luggage | An item being shipped, or personal baggage.  
#w 再配達（さいはいたつ）- redelivery | A second delivery attempt after a missed one.  
#w 不在票（ふざいひょう）- missed delivery notice | A slip left when a delivery could not be made because no one was home.  
#w 受け取り（うけとり）- receipt (of a parcel) | The act of accepting a delivered item.  
#w 追跡番号（ついせきばんごう）- tracking number | A unique code used to track the location of a shipped item.  
#w お届け先（おとどけさき）- delivery destination | The address or location a parcel is being sent to.  
#w 集荷（しゅうか）- pickup service | A service where a courier collects a parcel from the sender's location.  
#w 郵便ポスト（ゆうびんぽすと）- mailbox | A public box where mail is deposited for pickup.  
#w 窓口係（まどぐちがかり）- counter clerk | The staff member working at a service counter.  
#w 重さ（おもさ）- weight | How heavy an item is, often used to calculate postage.  
#w 国際郵便（こくさいゆうびん）- international mail | Mail sent to or from another country.  
#w 郵便局員（ゆうびんきょくいん）- post office worker | An employee who works at a post office.  
#w 局長（きょくちょう）- postmaster | The person in charge of a post office branch.

## **Verbs – postal actions (#wc)**

#wc 送る（おくる）- to send / mail | To dispatch a letter or parcel to someone.  
#wc 届ける（とどける）- to deliver / send to | To bring something to its destination.  
#wc 受け取る（うけとる）- to receive | To accept something that has been delivered or given.  
#wc 申し込む（もうしこむ）- to apply / request (e.g. redelivery) | To formally ask for a service, such as a redelivery.  
#wc 支払う（しはらう）- to pay (fee) | To give money to cover a cost, such as postage.  
#wc 渡す（わたす）- to hand over / give | To physically pass something to another person.  
#wc 書く（かく）- to write (address, name) | To write information by hand, such as an address.  
#wc 貼る（はる）- to stick / attach (stamp, label) | To affix something adhesive, such as a stamp.  
#wc 確認する（かくにんする）- to confirm / check | To verify that something is correct.  
#wc 聞く（きく）- to ask / inquire | To request information from someone.  
#wc 説明する（せつめいする）- to explain | To describe something so it is understood.  
#wc 集荷する（しゅうかする）- to collect / pick up (parcel) | For a courier to gather a parcel from the sender.  
#wc 追跡する（ついせきする）- to track (parcel) | To monitor the location or status of a shipped item.  
#wc 返送する（へんそうする）- to return (package) | To send an item back to its sender.  
#wc 届く（とどく）- to arrive / be delivered | For a mailed item to reach its destination.  
#wc 並ぶ（ならぶ）- to line up / queue | To stand in a line waiting for service.  
#wc 書き直す（かきなおす）- to rewrite | To write something again, usually to correct an error.  
#wc 入れる（いれる）- to put in (mailbox) | To place an item into a container, such as a mailbox.  
#wc 届け出る（とどけでる）- to file / report (change of address) | To formally notify an office of a change, such as a new address.

## **Adjectives (#wp)**

#wp 速い（はやい）- fast / quick | Happening or moving quickly.  
#wp 遅い（おそい）- slow / delayed | Happening slowly or arriving later than expected.  
#wp 便利（べんり）- convenient | Easy and useful to use.  
#wp 不便（ふべん）- inconvenient | Difficult or troublesome to use.  
#wp 安い（やすい）- cheap (fee) | Costing little money.  
#wp 高い（たかい）- expensive | Costing a lot of money.  
#wp 簡単（かんたん）- simple / easy | Not difficult; straightforward.  
#wp 複雑（ふくざつ）- complicated | Difficult to understand due to many parts or steps.  
#wp 正しい（ただしい）- correct / proper | Accurate or done in the right way.  
#wp 安全（あんぜん）- safe | Free from danger or risk.  
#wp 危ない（あぶない）- risky / dangerous | Likely to cause harm or loss.

## **Common questions and phrases (#w)**

#w この荷物を送りたいです。- I want to send this package. | Said when requesting to ship a package at the counter.  
#w 日本からポーランドへ送れますか？- Can I send this to Poland from Japan? | Asked when confirming whether international shipping to a specific country is possible.  
#w どのくらいかかりますか？- How long will it take? | Asked when inquiring about delivery time.  
#w 料金はいくらですか？- How much is the fee? | Asked when inquiring about the cost of postage.  
#w EMSで送れますか？- Can I send it by EMS? | Asked when confirming whether a fast international shipping option is available.  
#w 書留にしたいです。- I'd like to send it as registered mail. | Said when requesting a tracked, guaranteed delivery service.  
#w 追跡番号はありますか？- Do you have a tracking number? | Asked when checking whether a shipment can be tracked.  
#w 再配達を申し込みたいです。- I'd like to request redelivery. | Said when arranging a second delivery attempt after a missed one.  
#w 不在票を受け取りました。- I received a missed-delivery notice. | Said when explaining that a delivery attempt was missed.  
#w この住所に届けてください。- Please deliver it to this address. | Said when specifying the delivery destination.  
#w 郵便番号を教えてください。- Could you tell me the postal code? | Asked when requesting the postal code for an address.  
#w 切手を買いたいです。- I'd like to buy stamps. | Said when requesting to purchase postage stamps.  
#w ポストはどこですか？- Where is the mailbox? | Asked when looking for a public mailbox.  
#w 窓口はどちらですか？- Where is the service counter? | Asked when looking for the counter to be served.  
#w この書類を提出したいです。- I'd like to submit this document. | Said when handing in required paperwork.

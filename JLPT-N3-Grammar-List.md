# JLPT N3 Grammar — Master List

Compiled and cross-checked from multiple sources (there is no single official JLPT grammar list, so this list merges several study resources to avoid gaps):
- [JLPTsensei.com N3 Grammar List](https://jlptsensei.com/jlpt-n3-grammar-list/) (182 points)
- [Japanesetest4you.com N3 Grammar List](https://japanesetest4you.com/jlpt-n3-grammar-list/) (121 points)
- [Coto Japanese Academy — 20 Must-Know JLPT N3 Grammar Points](https://cotoacademy.com/20-must-know-jlpt-n3-grammar-points-master/)

Organized by category for study purposes. Nothing from the source lists was intentionally omitted — overlapping/variant entries across sources were merged into one line.

## grammar-index/ coverage check

🟢 = a file exists somewhere under `grammar-index/grammar/` that covers this point, **regardless of what JLPT level it's tagged with**. 🔴 = not covered by any file at any level.

**Result: 9 / 172 individual points covered (≈5%).** Unlike N5 and N4, the vault currently has **zero files tagged N3** — every green mark below is a case where the vault happens to already cover the same word/pattern at N4 or N5 (usually because it's a word that's genuinely useful earlier, e.g. なかなか, まるで, とおり, 中, ように, ため). This tracks with the vault's actual lesson history (git log shows lessons only through early N4/N5 so far — no N3 lessons have been extracted yet). Essentially all of N3-specific grammar — the reference particles (について, にとって, に対して, において, によって...), contrast/concession (くせに, ところが, どころか, は別として...), hearsay/inference (ということだ, に違いない, まさか...), the adjective-forming suffixes (っぽい, 気味, だらけ, がち, がたい, 的), completion-of-action verbs (切る, 込む, 通す, っぱなし...), and the whole discourse-marker section (ところで, それとも, つまり, さらに, すでに...) — is unwritten in this vault.

## 1. Degree, Extent, and Comparison

- 🔴 ほど (hodo) — degree, extent, "the more... the more..."
- 🔴 ほど～ない (hodo~nai) — "not as... as..."
- 🔴 ば～ほど (ba~hodo) — "the more [A], the more [B]"
- 🔴 くらい／ぐらい (kurai/gurai) — "to the extent that", "about", "approximately"
- 🔴 あまり (amari) — "so much that...", excessive degree (only あまり~ない's negative sense exists, at N5 — a different function)
- 🔴 あまりに／あまりにも (amari ni / amari ni mo) — "too much", "excessively"
- 🔴 どんなに～ても (donna ni~temo) — "no matter how much..."
- 🔴 いくら～ても (ikura~temo) — "no matter how much..."
- 🔴 とても～ない (totemo~nai) — "cannot possibly...", "hardly"
- 🟢 より (yori) — "than" — covered (N4/N5)
- 🔴 さえ (sae) — "even", "so much as"
- 🔴 さえ～ば (sae~ba) — "if only... then", "as long as"
- 🔴 せいぜい (seizei) — "at the most", "at best"
- 🔴 だけ (dake) — "as much as..." (extent use — only the N5 restrictive "only" sense exists, a different function)
- 🟢 なかなか (nakanaka) — "very", "quite" — covered (tagged N4, and the file explicitly covers both this positive sense and the negative なかなか～ない sense)
- 🔴 めったに～ない (metta ni~nai) — "hardly", "rarely", "seldom"
- 🔴 一度に (ichido ni) — "all at once"
- 🔴 数量＋は (suuryou + wa) — "at least [quantity]"
- 🔴 ずつ (zutsu) — "apiece", "each", "at a time"

## 2. Conditionals and Hypotheticals

- 🔴 ばいい (ba ii) — "should", "it'd be good if..."
- 🔴 ばよかった (ba yokatta) — "should have...", "it would have been better if..."
- 🔴 ば～のに (ba~noni) — "would have...", "if only..."
- 🔴 もしも～たら (moshimo~tara) — "if...", "supposing..."
- 🔴 としたら／とすれば／とすると (to shitara / to sureba / to suru to) — "if it were the case that...", "assuming..."
- 🔴 たらいい／といい (tara ii / to ii) — "it would be nice if..." — only the question form たらいいですか ("what should I do?") is covered, a different function
- 🔴 とみえる／とみえて (to mieru / to miete) — "it seems that..."

## 3. Obligation, Necessity, and Choice

- 🔴 べき／べきだ (beki/beki da) — "should do", "must do"
- 🔴 べきではない (beki dewa nai) — "should not do", "must not do"
- 🔴 ないと (naito) — "must do...", "unless you..."
- 🔴 ことだ (koto da) — "should do..." (advice)
- 🔴 ことはない (koto wa nai) — "there is no need to...", "never happens"
- 🔴 ないことはない (nai koto wa nai) — "it's not that you can't...", "is not impossible"
- 🔴 しかない (shika nai) — "have no choice but to..."
- 🔴 わけにはいかない (wake niwa ikanai) — "cannot afford to...", "must not"
- 🔴 ずにはいられない (zu niwa irarenai) — "can't help but...", "must do"
- 🔴 ようがない (you ga nai) — "there is no way to...", "it's impossible to"
- 🟢 ことになっている (koto ni natteiru) — covered (tagged N4: V「dict」ことになっています — "it is supposed to")

## 4. Reason and Cause

- 🟢 ため／ために (tame/tame ni) — the "for, for the sake of, in order to" sense is covered (tagged N4); the "because of, as a result of" sense is not
- 🔴 せいで (sei de) — "because of..." (negative outcome)
- 🔴 おかげで (okage de) — "thanks to...", "because of..." (positive outcome)
- 🔴 なぜなら (nazenara) — "because...", "the reason is..."
- 🔴 ことから (koto kara) — "from the fact that..."
- 🔴 そのために (sono tame ni) — "hence", "for that reason"
- 🔴 その結果／た結果／の結果 (sono kekka / ta kekka / no kekka) — "as a result of..."
- 🔴 もの／ものだから (mono/mono dakara) — "because..." (giving a reason/excuse)
- 🔴 けっきょく／結局 (kekkyoku) — "after all", "eventually", "in the end"

## 5. Hearsay, Inference, and Appearance

- 🔴 ということだ (to iu koto da) — "I've heard that...", "rumor has it that..."
- 🔴 というのは (to iu nowa) — "this means...", "because...", "that is to say..."
- 🔴 というと (to iu to) — "speaking of...", "if it were the case that..."
- 🔴 というより (to iu yori) — "rather than..."
- 🟡 と言えば (to ieba) — "speaking of...", "when you say..." — drafted in Leftovers-1 (as と言ったら／と言えば), pending extract-grammar
- 🔴 んだって (ndatte) — casual "I hear that..."
- 🔴 に違いない (ni chigai nai) — "I'm sure...", "no doubt that..."
- 🔴 まさか (masaka) — "there's no way...", "that's impossible"
- 🔴 もしかしたら (moshikashitara) — "perhaps", "maybe", "by any chance"
- 🔴 ような気がする (you na ki ga suru) — "have a feeling that...", "seems like..."
- 🔴 ように見える (you ni mieru) — "to look/seem/appear that..."
- 🔴 そうもない／そうにない (sou mo nai / sou ni nai) — "very unlikely to...", "showing no signs of..."
- 🔴 のでしょうか (no deshou ka) — polite way to ask a question

## 6. States and Appearance (Adjective-forming Suffixes)

- 🔴 っぽい (ppoi) — "-ish", "seems like", "easily does"
- 🔴 気味／ぎみ (gimi) — "-like", "-looking", "tending toward..."
- 🔴 だらけ (darake) — "covered all over in", "full of" (undesirable)
- 🔴 がち (gachi) — "apt to do", "tends to do", "often does"
- 🔴 がたい (gatai) — "hard to do", "difficult to"
- 🔴 的 (teki) — suffix turning a noun into a na-adjective/adverb ("-like", "-ic")

## 7. Manner and Way of Doing

- 🟢 ように (you ni) — "in order to", "so that" — covered (tagged N4; see also §14)
- 🟢 とおり／とおりに (toori/toori ni) — "in the way that...", "as..." — covered (tagged N4)
- 🟢 まるで (maru de) — "as if...", "just like..." — covered (tagged N4)
- 🔴 わけだ (wake da) — "for that reason", "no wonder", "as you'd expect"
- 🔴 わけではない (wake dewa nai) — "it doesn't mean that...", "it's not the case that..."
- 🔴 わけがない (wake ga nai) — "there is no way that...", "impossible"

## 8. Time Expressions

- 🟢 中 (chuu/juu) — "during", "throughout" — covered (tagged N4)
- 🔴 際に (sai ni) — "when...", "at the time of..."
- 🔴 最中に (saichuu ni) — "in the middle of...", "while..."
- 🔴 たとたん／とたん (ta totan) — "just as...", "the moment that..."
- 🔴 たびに (tabi ni) — "every time...", "whenever..."
- 🔴 途中で／途中に (tochuu de/ni) — "on the way", "in the middle of"
- 🔴 うちに (uchi ni) — "while...", "before... (changes)"
- 🔴 ぶりに (buri ni) — "for the first time in [period]"
- 🔴 てからでないと (te kara de nai to) — "must first do... before...", "cannot do without first doing"
- 🔴 たて (tate) — "just done", "freshly done"
- 🔴 てはじめて (te hajimete) — "not until...", "only after [X] did..."
- 🔴 ながらも (nagara mo) — "while", "although", "despite" (concurrent contrast)
- 🔴 一方だ (ippou da) — "more and more", "continuing to..."
- 🔴 一方で (ippou de) — "on one hand..., on the other hand..."

## 9. Contrast and Concession

- 🔴 くせに (kuse ni) — "even though...", "and yet...", "despite" (with a critical nuance)
- 🔴 ところが (tokoro ga) — "even so", "however", "even though"
- 🔴 どころか (dokoro ka) — "far from...", "anything but..."
- 🔴 たとえ～ても (tatoe~temo) — "even if...", "even though..."
- 🔴 たって (tatte) — casual "even if...", "no matter how..."
- 🔴 は別として (wa betsu toshite) — "aside from...", "apart from..."
- 🔴 とは限らない (towa kagiranai) — "not necessarily so", "isn't always true"
- 🔴 にしては (ni shite wa) — "for...", "considering that..."
- 🔴 にしても (ni shite mo) — "even if...", "regardless of..."
- 🔴 ことは～が (koto wa~ga) — "although...", "it's true that... but..."
- 🔴 むしろ (mushiro) — "rather", "instead", "if anything"

## 10. Emphasis and Focus

- 🔴 こそ (koso) — "precisely", "for sure" (emphasizes preceding word)
- 🔴 なんか／なんて／など (nanka/nante/nado) — "such as...", showing modesty or making light of something
- 🔴 は～で有名 (wa~de yuumei) — "famous for..."
- 🔴 はもちろん (wa mochiron) — "not to mention...", "not only... but also..."
- 🔴 を中心に (o chuushin ni) — "focused on...", "centered on..."
- 🔴 をはじめ (o hajime) — "starting with...", "for example..."
- 🔴 を込めて (o komete) — "filled with...", "put [feeling] into..."
- 🔴 一体 (ittai) — emphatic "what on earth...", "what in the world..."

## 11. Reference and Relational Particles

- 🔴 に関する／に関して (ni kansuru/ni kanshite) — "related to...", "regarding..."
- 🔴 について (ni tsuite) — "concerning...", "about..."
- 🔴 にとって (ni totte) — "to...", "for...", "as far as... is concerned"
- 🔴 に対して (ni taishite) — "towards...", "in contrast with...", "regarding..."
- 🔴 において／における (ni oite/ni okeru) — "in...", "at...", "regarding..."
- 🔴 によって／による (ni yotte/ni yoru) — "by means of...", "due to..."
- 🔴 によると／によれば (ni yoru to/ni yoreba) — "according to..."
- 🔴 につれて (ni tsurete) — "as... (then)...", "in proportion to..."
- 🔴 に比べて (ni kurabete) — "compared to..."
- 🔴 にかわって／にかわり (ni kawatte/ni kawari) — "instead of...", "on behalf of..."
- 🔴 に反して (ni hanshite) — "against...", "contrary to..."
- 🔴 から～にかけて (kara~ni kakete) — "through...", "from [A] to [B]"
- 🔴 にかけて (ni kakete) — "over (a period)", "concerning..."
- 🔴 を通じて／を通して (o tsuujite/o tooshite) — "through...", "via...", "throughout..."
- 🔴 にしたがって (ni shitagatte) — "as...", "in accordance with...", "consequently"
- 🔴 上で (ue de) — "upon...", "after...", "in order to..."
- 🔴 上に (ue ni) — "besides...", "in addition to...", "not only... but also..."
- 🔴 代わりに (kawari ni) — "instead of...", "in exchange for..."
- 🔴 に慣れる (ni nareru) — "to be/get used to..."
- 🔴 ごとに (goto ni) — "each...", "every...", "at intervals of..."

## 12. Completion and Extent of an Action

- 🔴 切る (kiru) — "to do something completely to the end"
- 🔴 切れない (kirenai) — "too much to finish/complete"
- 🔴 きり (kiri) — "only", "just"; also "since... (and nothing since)"
- 🔴 込む (komu) — "to do something for a long time" / "to move something inside"
- 🔴 直す (naosu) — "to do something over/again"
- 🔴 上げる (ageru, as ~あげる) — "to finish doing..." — only あげる "to give" is covered, an unrelated homonym
- 🔴 通す (toosu) — "to do until the end", "to continually do"
- 🔴 て済む (te sumu) — "sufficient by...", "resolved by..."
- 🔴 っぱなし (ppanashi) — "leaving something on/in use", "left as is"

## 13. Volition and Attempt

- 🔴 ようとする (you to suru) — "try to...", "be about to..."
- 🔴 ようとしない (you to shinai) — "not try to...", "not make an effort to..."
- 🔴 つもりだった (tsumori datta) — "I thought I would...", "was planning to..."
- 🔴 つもりで (tsumori de) — "with the intention of doing..."

## 14. Purpose and Prevention

- 🟢 ように (you ni) — "in order to...", "so that..." — covered (see §7)
- 🔴 には (ni wa) — "in order to...", "for the purpose of..."
- 🔴 てはいけないから (te wa ikenai kara) — "in order to not...", to prevent a negative situation

## 15. Requests, Suggestions, and Reactions to Situations

- 🔴 てごらん (te goran) — "(please) try to...", "(please) look..."
- 🔴 てばかりいる (te bakari iru) — "do nothing but...", "only..."
- 🔴 ている場合じゃない (te iru baai janai) — "this is no time to be doing..."
- 🔴 ても始まらない (temo hajimaranai) — "even if you..., it's no use", "there's no point to..."
- 🔴 ても構わない／てもかまわない (temo kamawanai) — "it doesn't matter if..."
- 🔴 てもしょうがない／ても仕方がない (temo shou ga nai / temo shikata ga nai) — "there's no point to...", "it can't be helped"
- 🔴 てしょうがない／てしかたがない (te shou ga nai / te shikata ga nai) — "can't help but...", "extremely..."

## 16. Conjunctions and Discourse Markers

- 🟡 ところで (tokoro de) — "by the way" (changing topic) — only ところです ("about to/in progress/just finished") is covered, an unrelated pattern — drafted in Leftovers-1, pending extract-grammar
- 🔴 それとも (soretomo) — "or", "or else"
- 🔴 つまり (tsumari) — "in other words", "in summary"
- 🔴 すなわち (sunawachi) — "in other words", "namely"
- 🔴 さらに (sara ni) — "furthermore", "again", "more and more"
- 🔴 さて (sate) — "well then", "now" (conjunction, changing topic)
- 🔴 すでに (sude ni) — "already", "something has already taken place"
- 🔴 しばらく (shibaraku) — "for a moment", "for a while"
- 🔴 ついでに (tsuide ni) — "while you're at it", "on the occasion of..."
- 🔴 ふと (futo) — "suddenly", "unexpectedly", "unintentionally"
- 🔴 つい (tsui) — "accidentally", "unintentionally", "by mistake"
- 🔴 ついに (tsui ni) — "finally", "at last", "in the end"
- 🔴 どうしても (doushitemo) — "no matter what", "at any cost", "after all"
- 🔴 わざと (wazato) — "on purpose", "intentionally"
- 🔴 わざわざ (wazawaza) — "to go to the trouble of...", "to go out of one's way to..."
- 🔴 決して～ない (kesshite~nai) — "never", "by no means"
- 🔴 別に～ない／べつに～ない (betsu ni~nai) — "not really", "not particularly"
- 🔴 割に／わりに (wari ni) — "considering...", "despite..."
- 🔴 っけ (kke) — casual sentence-ender: "what was it again...?"
- 🔴 じゃない (janai, as confirmation/surprise use) — "maybe...", "isn't it...?", expressing surprise — only the N4 んじゃない (seeking agreement) exists, a related but distinct point
- 🔴 か何か (ka nani ka) — "or something..."
- 🔴 も～ば～も (mo~ba~mo) — "both... and...", "either/or", "neither/nor"
- 🔴 だけでなく／ばかりでなく (dake de naku / bakari denaku) — "not only... but also..."
- 🔴 ふりをする (furi o suru) — "to pretend to...", "to act as if..."
- 🔴 かける／かけ (kakeru/kake) — "half-done", "not yet finished" — only the unrelated multi-use verb かける (hang/pour/lock/wear/sit/call/spend) is covered
- 🔴 だけど (dakedo) — "but", "however", "although"

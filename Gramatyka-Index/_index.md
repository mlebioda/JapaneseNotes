# Grammar Index

## Verbs

- [[verb-forms]] — The major conjugation forms of Japanese verbs: dictionary form (辞書形) and polite ます form, including affirmative, negative, and past variants.
- [[verb-te-iru]] — Use of ている to express ongoing states, current habits, and present continuous actions.
- [[verb-usage]] — Structural notes on specific verbs — which particles they take, how they combine with nouns, and their special usage rules.

## Adjectives

- [[adjectives-i]] — Conjugation of い-adjectives: non-past affirmative/negative and past affirmative/negative forms. Also covers conversion to adverb (い→く).
- [[adjectives-na]] — Conjugation of な-adjectives (non-past/past, affirmative/negative, polite/plain), use before nouns (な+N), and conversion to adverb (に).
- [[adjectives-special]] — Adjectives that behave differently from standard い- or な-adjective patterns, such as words that modify nouns directly without な.

## Particles

- [[particle-omission]] — How は, が, を, and に are dropped in casual conversation to sound more natural.
- [[particles-de]] — Uses of で: marking the means or method of an action ("by train"), and the location where an action takes place ("at the station").
- [[particles-direction]] — Use of へ and に to mark a travel destination or movement endpoint.
- [[particles-made]] — Use of まで to mark a time or place limit: "by (time)" (までに) and "up to / as far as (place)" (まで+V).
- [[particles-mo]] — Uses of も as an additive/inclusive particle: listing things, emphasizing unexpected inclusions, and expressing total affirmation or denial with interrogatives.
- [[particles-time]] — Use of に to mark a specific point in time when an action occurs, including which time words require に and which do not.
- [[particles-wa-ga]] — Contrast between は (topic marker) and が (subject marker / focus marker), including when each is used in questions and answers.
- [[particles-wo]] — Uses of を: marking the direct object of an action, a departure point, a transit route, and noun + をします constructions.

## Patterns

- [[ability-expressions]] — Expressing being good or bad at something using 上手/下手 with が.
- [[adverbs]] — How to form adverbs from adjectives (い→く, な→に) and standalone adverb vocabulary (いつも, たくさん, etc.).
- [[advice-suggestions]] — Patterns for giving advice or recommendations — ほうがいいです/ほうがいい for "you'd better / you should."
- [[alternative-questions]] — The か…か pattern for presenting two options ("is it A or B?"), including embedded use with 分かりません and 知らない.
- [[companion-expressions]] — Expressions using いっしょ to mean "together with" (に) or "the same as" (と/の).
- [[comparisons]] — Structures for comparing two items (より), choosing between them (どちら・の方が), and superlatives (の中で一番).
- [[conditional-patterns]] — Patterns expressing conditions, sequences, and "as soon as" relationships between clauses.
- [[conjecture-appearance]] — Expressing how something looks or seems — そうです for visual/immediate impression, でしょう for reasoned inference.
- [[conjunction-and]] — Chaining adjectives and clauses with "and": くて for い-adjectives, で for な-adjectives.
- [[conjunction-but]] — Using が mid-sentence to connect a positive clause with a contrasting or negative one.
- [[demonstratives]] — Ko-so-a-do words for pointing to things by proximity — as adjectives before nouns (この/その/あの/どの) and as standalone pronouns (これ/それ/あれ/どれ).
- [[desires-wants]] — Expressing what you want — ほしい for nouns and たい form for verbs — and asking about others' desires.
- [[directions-spatial]] — Words for cardinal directions and spatial sides: right, left, front, back, and compounds with 側 (side).
- [[existence-location]] — Patterns for stating that something/someone exists at a place, or that something is located somewhere, using あります and います.
- [[giving-receiving]] — Verbs for transferring objects between people — direction of give/receive and the particles に/から that mark giver and receiver.
- [[intentions-plans]] — Patterns for expressing personal intentions, resolutions, and fixed plans: と思っています (thinking of), つもりです (firm intention), 予定です (scheduled plan).
- [[likes-dislikes]] — Expressing liking and disliking with 好き/嫌い/大好き/大嫌い, their noun-modifying forms, and negation patterns.
- [[movement-verbs]] — Verbs for boarding, alighting, transferring, entering, leaving, and other directed movement, including their particle structures.
- [[obligation-must]] — Expressing obligation or necessity using the ない-form + なければなりません ("I have to…").
- [[opinion-expressions]] — Stating what you think with と思います and asking for opinions with どう思いますか.
- [[permission]] — Patterns for granting or asking permission (てもいいです/か) and expressing prohibition (てはいけません).
- [[personal-pronouns]] — Japanese first, second, and third person pronouns with their formality levels and plural forms (たち/ら).
- [[potential-ability]] — Expressing what one can or cannot do — N+が+できる for noun activities, and Vdict+こと+が+できる for verb actions.
- [[proposals-invitations]] — Polite verb endings for inviting, proposing, and offering to do something together.
- [[purpose-movement]] — Going, coming, or returning somewhere in order to do something: ます-stem + に + 行く/来る/帰る.
- [[quantity-expressions]] — Expressions that describe totals, amounts, or the entirety of something (e.g. 全部で — in total).
- [[question-words]] — Japanese interrogative words (どうやって, どうして, どんな, どうですか, etc.) and the sentence structures they appear in.
- [[reasons-causes]] — Using から to express "because" — both as a clause connector (P1 から P2) and as a sentence-final explanation.
- [[reported-speech]] — Direct and indirect quotation — reporting what someone said using 「」と言います (direct) or plain form + と言います (indirect).
- [[requests-commands]] — Patterns for making polite requests (Vてください) and negative requests (Vないでください) — telling someone to do or not do something.
- [[sentence-final-particles]] — Particles placed at the end of a sentence to convey attitude, seek agreement, soften statements, or add emphasis (ね, よ, よね, etc.).
- [[sentence-structure]] — Rules for ordering elements within a Japanese sentence (time, place, verb, etc.).

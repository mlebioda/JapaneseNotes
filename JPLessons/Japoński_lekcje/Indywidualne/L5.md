#g 

## **1. 〜てあげる– "to do something for someone"**

**Used when the speaker or someone in their group does a favor for someone else.**

> The giver is the subject; the receiver benefits from the action.

🔹 **Structure:**

✅ **Person A** は **Person B** に **Verb (て-form) + あげる**

🔹 **Example Sentences:**

- **私は友達に日本語を教えてあげた。**
    
    (_Watashi wa tomodachi ni Nihongo o oshiete ageta._)
    
    → "I taught my friend Japanese (as a favor)."
    
- **母は弟にご飯を作ってあげた。**
    
    (_Haha wa otouto ni gohan o tsukutte ageta._)
    
    → "Mom made food for my younger brother."
    

⚠️ **Note:** Be careful when using あげる with superiors (boss, teacher, elders). It may sound rude or arrogant.

## **2. 〜てくれる (te kureru) – "someone does something for me (or my group)"**

**Used when someone does a favor for the speaker or their in-group.**

> The receiver is the speaker; the giver does the action.

🔹 **Structure:**

✅ **Person A** が (or は) **Person B (me/my group)** に **Verb (て-form) + くれる**

🔹 **Example Sentences:**

- **友達が宿題を手伝ってくれた。**
    
    (_Tomodachi ga shukudai o tetsudatte kureta._)
    
    → **〜てくれる (te kureru) – "someone does something for me (or my group)"**
    
    **Used when someone does a favor for the speaker or their in-group.**
    
    > The receiver is the speaker; the giver does the action.
    
- **先生が日本語の発音を直してくれた。**
    
    (_Sensei ga Nihongo no hatsuon o naoshite kureta._)
    
    → "The teacher corrected my Japanese pronunciation (for me)."
    

👍 **くれる focuses on the kindness of the giver.**

---

## **3. 〜てもらう (te morau) – "to receive a favor from someone"**

**Used when the speaker (or their in-group) receives a favor from someone.**

> The receiver (subject) benefits from the giver's action.

🔹 **Structure:**

✅ **Person A** は **Person B** に **Verb (て-form) + もらう**

🔹 **Example Sentences:**

- **私は先生に作文をチェックしてもらった。**
    
    (_Watashi wa sensei ni sakubun o chekku shite moratta._)
    
    → "I had my teacher check my essay."
    
- **弟は友達にゲームを貸してもらった。**
    
    (_Otouto wa tomodachi ni geemu o kashite moratta._)
    
    → "My younger brother borrowed a game from his friend."
    

💡 **もらう focuses on the benefit the receiver gets.**

---

## **4. 〜てやる (te yaru) – "to do something for someone (lower than you)"**

**Used when the speaker does something for someone of lower status (kids, pets, plants, etc.).**

> Similar to あげる but with a sense of authority or superiority.

🔹 **Structure:**

✅ **Person A** は **Person B** に **Verb (て-form) + やる**

🔹 **Example Sentences:**

- **父は犬にえさをやった。**
    
    (_Chichi wa inu ni esa o yatta._)
    
    → "My dad fed the dog."
    
- **私は弟にゲームを買ってやった。**
    
    (_Watashi wa otouto ni geemu o katte yatta._)
    
    → "I bought a game for my younger brother."
    

⚠️ **やる can sound rude when used toward adults or equals. Use あげる instead.**

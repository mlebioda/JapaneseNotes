# 文法

## と + mimetic word (giongo/gitaigo) - manner adverbial

### 擬音語・擬態語（ぎおんご・ぎたいご）＋ と ＋ V - describes the manner an action happens in
- Structure
	- Mimetic word (giongo/gitaigo) + と + V
- Examples
	- ワンワンとほえますか - does it bark going "woof-woof"?
	- ニコニコと笑う（わら）- to smile beamingly
	- ぐっすりと眠る（ねむ）- to sleep soundly
	- ゆっくりと歩く（ある）- to walk slowly

### Register
- Register-neutral — this construction appears in both casual and polite speech (politeness comes from the verb ending, not from と). More common in spoken and descriptive Japanese than in formal business writing.

### Often confused with
- と (quotation marker, と言う／と思う) — test: is と marking a quoted thought or speech (と思う, と言う), or is it attached to a sound-symbolic/mimetic word describing HOW an action happens (mimetic word ＋ と ＋ V)? 「はい」と言いました (he said "yes") vs ニコニコと笑いました (he smiled beamingly) — same particle, entirely different function.
- と (listing/exhaustive "and", connecting two nouns) — also a different function; this と never connects two nouns, it always attaches to a single mimetic word directly before the verb it modifies.

## 欲しがる／を - 3rd-person "wants [a thing]" (が→を particle shift)

### Nが+ほしい → Nを+ほしがる／ほしがっています - shifts from 1st-person want to describing a 3rd person's observed want
- Structure
	- 1st person wants a thing: Nが + ほしいです
	- 3rd person (describing an outwardly-observed want, not the speaker's own): Nを + ほしがる／ほしがっています
- Examples
	- ロバートさんはパソコンをほしがっています - Robert wants a computer
	- 田中さんは新しいめがねをほしがっています - Tanaka wants new glasses
	- 田中さんはねこをほしがっています - Tanaka wants a cat
	- 彼はくろいスーツをほしがっています - He wants a black suit

### Register
- Neutral-polite as shown (ほしがっています); politeness comes from the verb ending as usual.

### Often confused with
- Plain N+が+ほしいです (1st person only, or asking the listener directly in a question) — test: is this the speaker's own want, or a want being described/observed about someone else? 私は車がほしいです (I want a car) vs 彼は車をほしがっています (he wants a car) — same base word ほしい, different particle and different grammatical subject-perspective.

## たがる - 3rd-person "wants to do [an action]"

### Vます-stem+たい → Vます-stem+たがる／たがっています - shifts from 1st-person want-to-do to describing a 3rd person's observed want-to-do
- Structure
	- 1st person wants to do an action: Vます-stem + たいです
	- 3rd person (describing an outwardly-observed want, not the speaker's own): Vます-stem + たがる／たがっています
- Examples
	- 子供(こども)たちは外(そと)であそびたがっています - the kids want to play outside
	- おとうとは新(あたら)しいゲームを買(か)いたがっています - my (younger) brother wants to buy a new game
	- すずきさんは新しいスマホに変(か)えたがっています - Suzuki wants to switch to a new smartphone
	- 友(とも)だちはそのカフェに行(い)きたがっています - my friend wants to go to that cafe
	- ねこがドアの外に出(で)たがっています - the cat wants to go out the door

### Register
- Neutral-polite as shown; politeness comes from the verb ending as usual.

### Often confused with
- Plain Vます-stem+たいです (1st person only, or asking the listener directly) — test: whose want is being expressed, the speaker's own (たい) or someone else's observed want (たがる)? 私は行きたいです (I want to go) vs 彼は行きたがっています (he wants to go).
- Structurally parallel to 欲しがる／を above — both are the "observed 3rd-person want" form of their respective 1st-person-only base pattern (ほしい, たい), documented in [[grammar-index/grammar/hoshii-i-want-n]] (not edited here — this is a separate standalone entry, just a cross-reference mention in prose).

# Vocabulary

## じゃあ - well then / so

#w じゃあ - well then / so | casual discourse marker introducing a conclusion or next step that follows from what was just said
#w そうですか。じゃあ、たけしさんにおみやげをかってきますよ - Is that so? Well then, I'll buy a souvenir for Takeshi

## どうしますか - what are you going to do (about it)?

#w どうしますか - what are you going to do (about it)? | asks about the manner of action / decision in a specific situation — how will you handle it, not which specific activity
#w 冬休(ふゆやす)み、どうしますか - Winter break — what are you going to do (about it)?

## 何(なに)をしますか - what will you do?

#w 何(なに)をしますか - what will you do? | asks about a concrete activity or plan, not the general approach

> **どうしますか** vs **何をしますか** — both ask "what will you do?", but not the same question. どうしますか asks about the *approach/decision* in a situation (how will you handle it) — 冬休み、どうしますか = "what are you going to do about winter break" (a decision to make). 何をしますか asks about the *concrete activity* itself — 週末、何をしますか = "what will you do this weekend" (naming an actual activity). どう ≈ "how/what approach", 何を ≈ "what [specific] thing".

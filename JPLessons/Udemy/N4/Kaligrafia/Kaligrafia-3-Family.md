## 事 - thing・こと、ごと・じ
[[事-abstractthing,matter]]

![[Pasted image 20250312205401.png]]
- **一 (いち)** – "One"
- **亅 (はねぼう)** – "Hook"
- **口 (くち)** – "Mouth"
- **彐 (けいがしら)** – "Snout" or "Hand-like shape"
**A thing (事)** is something that **one (一)** might say **with their mouth (口)**, but it can also be **hooked (亅)** into different meanings.

**(こと)**
#w 事（こと）- thing

**(ごと)**
#w 物事（ものごと）- matter, things
#w 仕事（しごと）- job
#w 兄は日曜日にも仕事があります(あに、しごと) - My elder brother works on Sunday too 
#w あの人の仕事はしりません（しごと、し）- I don't know that person's job

**(じ)**
#w 事業（じぎょう）- business

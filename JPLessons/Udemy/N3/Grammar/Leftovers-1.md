# 文法

## ところで - by the way (topic-change discourse marker)

### ところで - abruptly shifts the conversation to a new, often unrelated topic
- Structure
	- ところで + new topic/question
- Examples
	- そうですか。ところで、たけしさんはどこかに行きますか - I see. By the way, is Takeshi going somewhere?
	- ところで、明日（あした）のパーティーに行きますか - By the way, are you going to tomorrow's party?
	- ところで、あの映画（えいが）はもう見（み）ましたか - By the way, have you already seen that movie?

### Register
- Conversational — signals a deliberate change of subject in the middle of a conversation. Softer than abruptly changing topic with no marker at all: ところで tells the listener "I'm switching gears now," which makes the shift feel intentional and polite rather than jarring. Used in both casual and polite speech (politeness comes from sentence endings, not from ところで itself).

### Often confused with
- ところです family (about to/in progress/just finished — 「〜るところです」「〜ているところです」「〜たところです」, e.g. `[[grammar-index/grammar/tokoro-desu-dict-form]]`) — unrelated in meaning despite sharing the word ところ. The ところです family attaches to a verb to mark a moment in an action's timeline (about to happen / happening now / just finished); ところで is a standalone discourse marker at the start of a new sentence that changes the subject. Test: does ところ attach directly after a verb form and describe timing of an action, or does ところで stand alone at the top of a sentence introducing something new? 食（た）べるところです (about to eat) vs ところで、食べに行（い）きませんか (by the way, want to go eat?) — same string ところ, completely different grammar.

## と言ったら／と言えば - speaking of X, [it reminds me of] Y (word-association)

### X と言ったら／と言えば Y - free word-association triggered by mentioning a topic
- Structure
	- Topic X + と言ったら／と言えば + Y, where Y is the first thing that comes to mind when X is mentioned
- Examples (word-chain game — each answer becomes the next question's topic)
	1. すいかと言（い）ったら緑（みどり） - speaking of watermelon, [it makes you think of] green
	2. 鳥（とり）と言ったら羽（はね） - speaking of birds, wings [come to mind]
	3. 羽と言ったら虫（むし）です - speaking of wings/feathers, it's insects
	4. 空（そら）と言ったら雲（くも） - speaking of the sky, clouds
	5. 文字（もじ）と言ったら黒（くろ）い - speaking of characters/letters, black
	6. 黒いと言ったら真（ま）っ黒（くろ）です - speaking of black, it's pitch-black

### Register
- と言えば is the standard, neutral form used in normal conversation and writing. と言ったら is functionally the same pattern but carries a slightly more emphatic/exclamatory nuance (often used in more animated, casual speech, like this word-chain game). Both take a noun/topic directly before them.

### Often confused with
- と言う／という意味です (defining a term, e.g. `[[grammar-index/grammar/toiuimidesu-means]]`) — that pattern explains what a word *means*; と言ったら／と言えば doesn't define X at all, it triggers free association — whatever Y comes to mind when X is mentioned, not X's dictionary meaning. Test: is the sentence answering "what does X mean?" or "what does X remind you of?" 「ねこ」と言う意味です (it means "cat") vs ねこと言えば、こたつです (speaking of cats, [it reminds me of] kotatsu) — the second isn't a definition of ねこ at all.

#### この/その/あの　どの

- この - this (noun) close to speaker
- その - that (noun) close to listener
- あの - that (noun) far from both
- どの - which (noun)?

##### Structure
- この/その/あの, どの + Noun

#### これ/それ/あれ　どれ

- これ - this (noun) close to speaker
- それ - that (noun) close to listener
- あれ - that (noun) far from both
- どれ - which (noun)?

##### Structure
- これ/それ/あれ, どれ

### Mnemonic
The character combines a pestle in a mortar (午) with the radical for ten (十). The pestle positioned vertically in the mortar represents the sun at its highest point, symbolizing noon as the middle of the day.
Image of a pestle (handle at the top), that hits the middle of the mortar, like 'noon' is the middle of the day.

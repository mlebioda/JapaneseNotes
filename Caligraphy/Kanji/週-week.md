### Mnemonic
Left side shows movement (shinnyuu radical), right side displays surrounding/circumference (周). The 周 character combines an enclosed area (冂) with good luck (吉), where a samurai (士) with a full mouth (口) represents happiness.
The movement takes a round of a week.

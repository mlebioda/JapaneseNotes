# は

> は marks N1 as the sentence topic, with N2 describing or predicated of it.

## Structure

- N1は N2です - N2 describes N1 (topic marked by は)

## Examples

- ホテルはどれですか (podmiotem jest hotel) - Which one is the hotel? (subject is "hotel")
- 山田さんはだれですか - Who is Mr. Yamada?

## Register
Register: neutral
は is used at every formality tier; the sentence's politeness comes from its predicate (です／だ), not from は itself.

# この/その/あの　どの

> Demonstrative adjectives ("this/that/which") that always attach directly before a noun.

## Structure

この/その/あの/どの + Noun

- この - this (close to speaker)
- その - that (close to listener)
- あの - that (far from both)
- どの - which?

## Examples

- この本(ほん) - this book
- その資料(しりょう) - that document (near you)
- あの方(かた) - that person (over there)
- どの本(ほん)ですか - Which book is it?

## Notes

この／その／あの／どの always require a following noun and can never stand alone — contrast with これ／それ／あれ／どれ, which stand alone and can never directly precede a noun (これ本 is ungrammatical; must be この本).

Related but not covered in this lesson: こう／そう／ああ／どう (manner: "this way/that way") and ここ／そこ／あそこ／どこ (place: "here/there/where") complete the ko-so-a-do system.

## Register
Register: neutral
Used identically in casual and polite speech — no politeness variation on these demonstratives themselves.

## Often confused with

- [これ/それ/あれ　どれ](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/kore-sore-are-dore) — test: Does the demonstrative directly precede a noun (→ この／その／あの／どの), or stand alone as a pronoun (→ これ／それ／あれ／どれ)?

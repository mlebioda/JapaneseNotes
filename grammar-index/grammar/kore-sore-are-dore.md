# これ/それ/あれ　どれ

> Demonstrative pronouns ("this/that/which") that stand alone and can never directly precede a noun.

## Structure

これ/それ/あれ/どれ

- これ - this (close to speaker)
- それ - that (close to listener)
- あれ - that (far from both)
- どれ - which?

## Examples

- これは本(ほん)です - this is a book
- それは資料(しりょう)です - that is a document
- あれは何(なん)ですか - what is that (over there)?
- どれがあなたのですか - Which one is yours?

## Register
Register: neutral
Used identically in casual and polite speech — no politeness variation on these pronouns themselves.

## Often confused with

- [この/その/あの　どの](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/kono-sono-ano-dono) — test: Does the demonstrative stand alone as a pronoun (→ これ／それ／あれ／どれ), or directly precede a noun (→ この／その／あの／どの)?

# V dict とすぐです - As soon as ....

> As soon as you do V, [result follows].

## Structure

V-dictionary form + と + すぐです — the と-conditional (Vる + と, "when/as soon as X, Y follows naturally") combined with the adverb すぐ ("right away"); です stands in for an omitted result clause. Register: neutral — です keeps this fine for both casual and polite conversation.

## Examples

- 左へ曲がる　と　すぐです (ま) - As soon as you turn left, it is right here

## Register

Register: neutral
です on すぐ keeps this fine for both casual and polite conversation — no register shift is needed regardless of who you're speaking to.

## Often confused with

- [V dict + と - if](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/v-dict-to-if) — test: Is this a general natural-consequence conditional with any kind of result (plain と), or specifically emphasizing that the result happens immediately/right away (と + すぐです)?

# ...ようです

> Expresses that something appears to be the case — either a similarity/resemblance judgment, or an inferential assumption based on indirect evidence.

## 1. Similarity - It is like...

### Structure

1. V「dict」+ ようです
2. いadj + ようです
3. なadj + な + ようです
4. N + の + ようです

Speaker thinks something or some event looks similar to some other thing.

### Examples

- あの人(ひと)は英語(えいご)がとても上手(じょうず)です。まるでアメリカ人(じん)のようです。 - That person is so good at English. It's just as if he were American.
- 4月(しがつ)になったのに、まだ寒(さむ)くて、まるで冬(ふゆ)のようです。 - Although it's April, it's still cold — it's just like winter.
- あの難(むずか)しい試験(しけん)に合格(ごうかく)できたのが、本当(ほんとう)に夢(ゆめ)のようです。 - The fact that I passed that difficult exam really feels like a dream.

## 2. Assumption - it seems that

### Structure

1. V「dict」+ ようです
2. いadj + ようです
3. なadj + な + ようです
4. N + の + ようです

An inference from indirect evidence, reasoning, or general knowledge — not necessarily observed directly right now.

### Examples

- 雨(あめ)が降(ふ)るようです。 - It looks like it's going to rain (indirect inference).
- 雨(あめ)が降(ふ)りそうです。 - It looks like it's going to rain (direct visual impression).
- 外(そと)は雨(あめ)が降(ふ)っているようですね。 - It seems to be raining outside, doesn't it? | use ようだ, not そう(様態): based on indirect signs (sound, wet umbrellas), not watching the sky directly right now
- 山(やま)の上(うえ)は寒(さむ)いようです。 - The top of the mountain seems cold. | use ようだ, not そう(様態): a general/logical inference (altitude, common knowledge), not a direct sensory impression in this moment
- 誰(だれ)でも戦争(せんそう)は嫌(きら)いなようです。 - It seems that everyone dislikes war. | use ようだ, not そう(様態): a generalization about other people's feelings, not a directly observed reaction (そう(様態) would need a visible cue like someone's facial expression)

## Notes

ようです vs そうです（様態）:

| | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| ようです | An inference from indirect evidence, reasoning, or general knowledge — not necessarily observed directly right now. | 雨(あめ)が降(ふ)るようです - It looks like it's going to rain (e.g. based on a forecast or general reasoning, not necessarily the sky right now) |
| そうです（様態） | A direct, immediate impression from something the speaker is observing right now — not hearsay. | 雨(あめ)が降(ふ)りそうです - It looks like it's going to rain (the speaker looked at the sky and is speaking from that immediate impression) |

## Register

Register: neutral
です here is the standard polite copula; the plain-form equivalent ような／ように／ようだ (だ instead of です) is used identically in casual speech, with no change to the four attachment rules above. Formality is compositional, not fixed to the pattern itself.

## Often confused with

- [まるで - just like / not at all](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/marude) — test: Just stating a general impression of similarity, or emphasizing that the resemblance is total/complete?
- [N が します](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/n-ga-shimasu) — test: Is this an inference based on how something appears (ようです), or a direct sensory perception like smell/taste/sound (がする)?
- [。。。はずです - should do/ought (logically)](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/hazudesu-should-do-ought-logically) — test: Is the speaker deducing from known facts/reasoning (はずです) or judging from what they can see/sense (ようです)?
- [V「plain」そうです / そうだ - It is said that / I heard](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/sou-desu-hearsay) — test: Is this something the speaker heard from someone else (そうです), or an inference the speaker is making themself from indirect evidence (ようです)?
- [。。。らしいです - it seems](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/rashii-desu) — test: Is this based on hearsay or a typical/stereotypical impression (らしいです), or the speaker's own direct inference from evidence (ようです)?
- [そうです/そうだ - express an impression or inference based on direct observation - it looks](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/sou-desu-appearance-inference) — test: Is this an inference from indirect evidence, reasoning, or general knowledge (ようです), or a direct, immediate visual impression from what the speaker sees right now (そう様態)?

# でしょう - seems / right

> Expresses a confident guess ("it seems...") or seeks confirmation of a guess ("...right?").

## Structure

### Verb
- Past: V「た」+ でしょう → あなた朝(あさ)から晩(ばん)までずっと働(はたら)いて、疲(つか)れたでしょう。 - You worked from morning to night, so you must be very tired, right?

### い-adjective
- Present: Adj + でしょう → このかばん、高(たか)いでしょう。 - It seems that this bag is expensive.

### な-adjective
- Present: Adj + でしょう → 山田(やまだ)くんはカラオケが好(す)きでしょう。 - It seems that Mr. Yamada likes karaoke.

### Noun
- Present: N + でしょう → 明日(あした)は雨(あめ)でしょう。 - It seems that it will rain tomorrow.

Questions:
- でしょうか - more polite than ですか
  - こちらはお客様のお荷物でしょうか（きゃくさま、にもつ）- Is this your luggage, Sir?

## Examples

- 明日(あした)は雨(あめ)でしょう。 - It seems that it will rain tomorrow.
- 山田(やまだ)くんはカラオケが好(す)きでしょう。 - It seems that Mr. Yamada likes karaoke.
- このかばん、高(たか)いでしょう。 - It seems that this bag is expensive.
- あなた朝(あさ)から晩(ばん)までずっと働(はたら)いて、疲(つか)れたでしょう。 - You worked from morning to night, so you must be very tired, right?
- これはあなたのものでしょう。- This is yours, right?
- A: こちらはお客様(きゃくさま)のお荷物(にもつ)でしょうか。B: ええ、そうです - A: Is this your luggage, Sir? B: Yes, it is

## Use Cases

Two meanings:
- Seems — 明日は雨でしょう。(It seems that it will rain tomorrow.)
- Right? (confirming a guess is correct) — intonation is important here, it signals to the listener that you're confirming your guess: あなた朝から晩までずっと働いて、疲れたでしょう。(You worked from morning to night, so you must be very tired, right?)

## Notes

- ね particle (not yet in grammar-index) — test: is this an inferred guess based on evidence you're checking (でしょう, e.g. 疲れたでしょう - "you must be tired, right?"), or an assumed shared/obvious observation seeking empathy (ね, e.g. 疲れたね - "you're tired, aren't you")? They can combine: 明日は雨でしょうね - "it'll probably rain tomorrow, won't it"

## Register

Register: polite-context typical
でしょう is the polite/neutral register form; the plain/casual equivalent is だろう, used with friends and family or in casual speech.

## Often confused with

- [んじゃない - when someone asks for an opinion and the speaker is sure of their idea](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/njanai-seeking-agreement) — test: Is the speaker stating a confident guess (でしょう), or seeking the listener's agreement with a view they already hold (んじゃない)?
- [かもしれません・かもしれない - may/might](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/kamoshiremasen-kamoshirenai-may-might) — test: Is the speaker fairly confident in this guess (でしょう), or presenting it as an uncertain possibility (かもしれません)?

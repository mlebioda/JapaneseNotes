# に - time of occurrence of an action

> Marks the time at which an action occurs.

## Structure

- Time + に + V → ９:00(くじ)に　会社へ行きます (I go to the company at 9:00)
- Exception — these time words never take に: 今日、明日、明後日、昨日、一昨日、今朝、今晩、今、毎朝、毎日、毎週、今週、先週、来週、いつ、先月、今月、今年、来年、去年, and weekday names (曜日, e.g. 月曜日、日曜日). Underlying rule: relative time words anchored to "now" (this/next/last day, week, month, year) skip に; absolute, specific time expressions (clock times, calendar dates, weekday names) take it. → 明後日(あさって)学校に行きます (明後日 takes no に — exception word)
- Optional — に is optional (may be included or omitted) with 昼、夜、朝、晩 → 昼(ひる)（に）ゆうえんちに行きました (に optional with 昼)

## Examples

- ９:00(くじ)に　会社へ行きます - I go to the company at 9:00
- 8月(はちがつ)２１日(にじゅうににち)に　大阪へ行きました - I went to osaka on August 21
- 明後日(あさって)学校に行きます - i will go to school the day after tomorrow (明後日 takes no に — exception word)
- 来月　けっこんします - i will get married next month (来月 takes no に — exception word)
- 昼(ひる)（に）ゆうえんちに行きました - i went to amusement park in the afternoon (に optional with 昼)
- 夜(よる)（に）おにぎりを食べます - I will eat onigiri at night (に optional with 夜)

## Register

Register: neutral

に carries no politeness of its own — register comes from the verb ending.

## Often confused with

- [N(time) + まで +に + V - BY, until (do)](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/ntime-made-ni-v-by-until-do) — test: is に simply marking the time an action occurs (Time + に + V), or marking a deadline the action must be completed by (N(time) + までに + V)?

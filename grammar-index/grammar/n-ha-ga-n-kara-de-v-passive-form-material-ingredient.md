# N + は/が N + から/で + V 「passive form」- Material, ingredient

> Passive verbs of creation (作られる etc.) mark the raw material with から when it transforms completely, or で when it remains visible in the finished product.

## Structure

- N + は/が N + から/で + V「passive form」- Material, ingredient
    1. から - made from (material transforms completely)
        1. ワインは葡萄（ぶどう）**から** 作られました（つく） - wine is made **from** grapes
    2. で - made of (material is still visible)
        1. この家（いえ）は木（き）で作られました（つく）- this house is made **of** wood

## Examples

- ワインは葡萄（ぶどう）**から** 作られました（つく） - wine is made from grapes
- この家（いえ）は木（き）**で**作られました（つく）- this house is made of wood
- この椅子(いす)は木(き)でできている。 - This chair is made of wood.

## Notes

〜でできている ("is made of/from ~") is a common alternative to the passive 作られる, especially in casual speech.

## Register
Register: neutral
Used for descriptive statements about production or composition in both casual conversation and formal contexts (product descriptions, museum placards) — the register comes from the surrounding sentence, not this pattern itself.

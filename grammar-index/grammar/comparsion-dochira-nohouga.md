# comparsion どちら・ の方が (のほうが)

> Ask which of two things is more Adj, and answer with the one that is.

## Structure

- N1 と N2 と どちらが ADJ ですか — which one is more Adj, N1 or N2
- N1 と N2 と どちらが ADJ と思いますか — which one do you think is more Adj, N1 or N2
- N1/N2 の方が ADJ です — N1/N2 is more Adj
	- The "より N" comparison is normally dropped in the answer once it's contextually clear, e.g. みかんの方が好きです rather than みかんの方がリンゴより好きです
- どちらも ADJ です — both are Adj

## Examples

- A: リンゴと みかんとどちらが好きですか B: みかんの方が好きです (のほうが) - A: Which do you like more, apples or tangerine B: I like tangerine more
- A: キムさん と スミスさん と どちらが 日本語が上手ですか B: キムさんの方が上手です（のほうが） - A: Who is better at Japanese, Miss Kim or Smith? B: Miss Kim is better
- A: このざっしとそのざっしと どちらが面白いと思いますか B: どちらも面白いです (おもしろい、おも) - A: Which do you think is more interesting, this magazine or that magazine? B: Both are interesting
- どちらも - both

## Use Cases

どちら is the polite/neutral form; どっち is the casual spoken equivalent (どっちが好き？ vs どちらが好きですか). As with より, the actual politeness level comes from the copula/verb ending (だ vs です), not the question word itself.

## Register

Register: neutral
どちら itself is the neutral/polite question word; どっち is its casual spoken counterpart. As with より, actual politeness comes from the copula/verb ending, not the question word.

## Often confused with

- [より than - comparing 2 things](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/yori-than-comparing-2-things) — test: Are you asking/answering which of two things is more Adj (どちらが／のほうが), or directly stating the comparison (N1はN2よりAdjです)?
- [の中で一番 best among](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/node-best-among) — test: Are you comparing exactly two things (どちら／のほうが), or picking the best among three or more (一番)?

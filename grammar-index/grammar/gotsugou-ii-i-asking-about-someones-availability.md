# (ご)都合（つごう）+ いい／悪い - asking about someone's availability

> 都合 refers to a person's availability or schedule — not whether an object or situation is inherently convenient. Often paired with いい (available/convenient) or 悪い (unavailable/inconvenient), with the polite ご prefix used when asking about someone else's schedule.

## 1. Stating convenience / inconvenience

### Structure
- （ご）都合(つごう)が + いい／悪い → （ご）都合(つごう)がいい - to be convenient

### Examples
- （ご）都合(つごう)がいい - to be convenient
- （ご）都合(つごう)が悪(わる)い - to be inconvenient

## 2. Politely asking about someone's availability

### Structure
- ご都合(つごう)は + いかがでしょうか／よろしいでしょうか → ご都合(つごう)はいかがでしょうか - How is your availability? / Does that work for you?

### Examples
- ご都合(つごう)はいかがでしょうか - How is your availability? / Does that work for you? (polite, open-ended)
- ご都合(つごう)はよろしいでしょうか - Would that be convenient for you? (polite, confirming a specific time)

## Register
Register: polite-context typical
ご都合 (with the ご honorific prefix) and いかがでしょうか／よろしいでしょうか are formal/business register, typically used toward customers, superiors, or people outside one's in-group — not used when asking a close friend.

## Often confused with

- 便利（べんり）／不便（ふべん）- convenient/inconvenient (a thing's usefulness, not a person's schedule) — test: is the sentence about whether a place, tool, or app is practical in general, or about whether a specific proposed time fits a person's circumstances (都合)?
- 暇（ひま）／忙（いそが）しい - free/busy (a person's general state of being occupied or not) — test: is the sentence asking about someone's overall business level right now, or about whether one specific proposed time works given their existing commitments (都合)?

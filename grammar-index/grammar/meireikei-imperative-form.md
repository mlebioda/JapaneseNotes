# 命令形（めいれいけい）- imperative form

> A blunt, forceful command form used in emergencies, warnings, or casual encouragement.

## Structure

### Verb
- Group 1 (U-verb): dictionary form, う→え row → 書く → 書け (Write!)
- Group 2 (RU-verb): drop る, add ろ → 食べる → 食べろ (Eat!)
- Group 3 (irregular): する → しろ, くる → こい (Do it!/Come!)

**Full conjugation tables:**

|Plain|Imperative|
|---|---|
|書く|書け|
|飲む|飲め|
|話す|話せ|
|待つ|待て|
|乗る|乗れ|

|Plain|Imperative|
|---|---|
|食べる|食べろ|
|見る|見ろ|

|Plain|Imperative|
|---|---|
|する|しろ|
|くる|こい|

> する can also become せよ — more formal/literary/archaic (writing, military commands); しろ is the common conversational form.

**Negative imperative（禁止形 - きんしけい, prohibitive form）:** dictionary form + な — see [[grammar-index/grammar/vdictna-dont-do]] for the full pattern.
- 行くな — Don't go!
- 食べるな — Don't eat!

**よ softening:** imperative + よ softens the tone into casual encouragement/advice, mostly among close (often male) friends — 来(こ)いよ、頑張(がんば)れよ

**Softer alternatives:**
- 〜てください — polite request
- 〜なさい — soft imperative (parent/teacher to child)
- 〜てくれ — casual (masculine)

## Examples

- この川(かわ)は危(あぶ)ないから、注意(ちゅうい)しろ。 - This river is dangerous, be careful!
- がんばれ！がんばれ！ - Come on! Come on!
- 明日(あした)暇(ひま)だったら、うちへ来(こ)いよ。 - If you are free tomorrow, come to my house. (imperative)

## Use Cases

Used when strongly ordering someone in urgent situations — accidents, disasters, emergencies — or cheering/supporting during sports (typically by men), e.g. 止まれ (used on roads) or がんばれ. Blunt and masculine-coded — rude toward superiors, but acceptable casually among close (often male) friends, often softened by adding よ (来(こ)いよ).

## Register

Register: tiered
- Plain imperative / 禁止形 (書け、食べろ、しろ、こい、行くな) — blunt, masculine-coded; used toward subordinates, in emergencies, on signs, sports cheering; rude toward superiors; casual among close (usually male) friends, often softened with よ.
- なさい — softer, still top-down (parent→child, teacher→student); not used toward superiors.
- てください／てくれ — everyday register; てください is neutral-polite for anyone, てくれ is casual/masculine among equals.

## Often confused with

- [ます形 なさい - giving instructions](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/masu-nasai-giving-instructions) — test: Is this a blunt/emergency command among peers or inferiors (imperative), or a calmer authority-based instruction like teacher-to-student (なさい)?

# へ - e、に - direction of movement, travel

> Particle へ marks the destination of travel or movement; interchangeable with に in this use.

## Use Cases

Used with travel destinations and reaching points. へ and に can replace each other when indicating direction of movement.

## Structure

Place + へ + Verb (movement verb)

## Examples

- 会社へ戻ります — I go back to the company.
- 東京へ/に行く — go to Tokyo (both particles work for specific destinations)
- アメリカへ行く — go toward America (へ emphasises directionality of travel)

## Notes

へ and に are interchangeable in most travel/destination contexts. The subtle difference: に points to the arrival point specifically; へ emphasises the direction of travel. In practice, native speakers use both freely.

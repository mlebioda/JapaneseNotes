# 通(とお)りに。。。 - exactly as, as, just like

> To indicate doing something as planned or instructed, or confirming that something matched a prediction/expectation ("just as I thought").

## Structure

### Verb
- Dictionary (general/habitual): V「dict」+ とおりに → 先生(せんせい)が言(い)うとおりに書(か)いてください。 - Please write as the teacher says (in general).
- Past (one specific completed instance, including confirming a prediction): V「た」+ とおりに → わたしが書(か)いたとおりに、書(か)いてください。 - Please write exactly as I have written. (思(おも)ったとおりに = "just as I thought")

### Noun
- With の: N + の + とおりに → 予定(よてい)のとおり、9時(じ)に集合(しゅうごう)します。 - As planned, we will gather at 9 o'clock.
- Without の (sound changes to どおり): N + どおりに → 予定(よてい)どおり、9時(じ)に集合(しゅうごう)します。 - As planned, we will gather at 9 o'clock.

Standalone noun forms: このとおり (like this / in this way), そのとおり (exactly / that's right / like that), あのとおり (like that (over there) / as you can see) → このとおりに、やってください。 - Please do it exactly like this.

## Examples

- 先生(せんせい)が言(い)うとおりに書(か)いてください。 - Please write as the teacher says. (general/habitual — dict form)
- わたしが書(か)いたとおりに、書(か)いてください。 - Please write exactly as I have written. (one specific instance — た form)
- 「予定(よてい)どおり／予定(よてい)のとおり」、9時(じ)に集合(しゅうごう)します。 - As planned, we will gather at 9 o'clock. (2 readings)
- 昨日(きのう)のサッカーの試合(しあい)は、思(おも)ったとおりに、山田(やまだ)さんのチームが勝(か)った。 - As I thought, Mr. Yamada's team won the soccer game yesterday.
- 先生(せんせい)が言(い)ったとおりに、勉強(べんきょう)しました。 - I studied as the teacher said.
- 約束(やくそく)「のとおり・どおり」に、9時(じ)に駅(えき)に来(き)ました。 - I came to the station at 9:00 as promised.
- このとおりに、やってください。 - Please do it exactly like this.

## Register

Register: neutral
Used across all speech styles and registers; どおり (without の) is common in formal writing and official announcements, while のとおり is equally natural in spoken and written contexts — this is a stylistic/orthographic variant, not a politeness tier.

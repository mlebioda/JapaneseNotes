# 杯 (はい/ぱい/ばい) - number of cups/glasses of liquid

> How to count cups or glasses of liquid using 杯, one through ten.

## Structure

Number + 杯（はい／ぱい／ばい）

- １杯（いっぱい）- one cup/glass
- ２杯（にはい）- two cups/glasses
- ３杯（さんばい）- three cups/glasses
- ４杯（よんはい）- four cups/glasses
- ５杯（ごはい）- five cups/glasses
- ６杯（ろっぱい）- six cups/glasses
- ７杯（ななはい）- seven cups/glasses
- ８杯（はっぱい）- eight cups/glasses
- ９杯（きゅうはい）- nine cups/glasses
- １０杯（じゅっぱい）- ten cups/glasses
- 何杯（なんばい）- how many cups/glasses

## Examples

- コーヒーを２杯（にはい）飲（の）みました。- I drank two cups of coffee.

## Register

Register: neutral
Counter readings themselves don't carry politeness — they attach identically in polite and plain sentences; only the surrounding verb/copula changes with register.

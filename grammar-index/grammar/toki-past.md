# Vた＋とき＋V - do.... after doing something

> Marks a completed action (Vた形) that finishes before a later event — "after/when [A happened], [B happened]"; the two events are sequential, not simultaneous.

## Structure

B happens after the V-た action completes; the two events are sequential, not simultaneous.

### Verb
- Past: V-た + とき → アメリカへ行ったとき、ハンバーガーを食べました - I ate hamburgers after (when) I went to America

### い-adjective
- Past: Adj-かった + とき → 忙(いそが)しかったとき、電話に出られませんでした - When I was busy, I couldn't answer the phone.

### な-adjective / Noun
- Past: だった + とき → 学生だったとき、よく図書館で勉強しました - When I was a student, I often studied at the library.

## Examples

- アメリカへ**行ったとき**、ハンバーガーを食べました - I ate hamburgers **after** I went to America
- 家に帰ったとき、両親に会った (いえ・かえ・りょうしん・あ) - I met my parents when I came back home.
- 忙(いそが)しかったとき、電話に出られませんでした - When I was busy, I couldn't answer the phone.
- 学生だったとき、よく図書館で勉強しました - When I was a student, I often studied at the library.

## Register

Register: neutral
とき is neutral across every register regardless of whether the とき-clause itself is past or non-past; formality comes from the sentence's own copula/verb ending, not from とき.

## Often confused with

- [A....とき...B - while A, B](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/toki-non-past) — test: Does B happen around or before A completes, general "when" (Vとき), or specifically after A is complete (Vたとき)?

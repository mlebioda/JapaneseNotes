# い -> くて

> Joining 2+ い-adjectives (or an い-adjective with another clause) meaning "and", using くて for every adjective but the last.

## Structure

- い-adjective (drop い) + くて + ... + final adjective (keeps its own tense)
- Nは いadjくて....いadjです
- Nは いadjくて....いadjくて....adjです
- The tense of the last adjective in the chain determines the tense of the whole sentence.
- Negative chains the same way: い→くなくて (not just affirmative くて)

## Examples

- この部屋(へや)は新(あたら)しくて広(ひろ)いです - this room is new and wide
- 昼ご飯は安くて美味しいです(ひるごはん、やす,おい) - the lunch is cheap and delicious
- 天気(てんき)が悪(わる)くて、とても寒(さむ)かったです  - the weather was really bad and cold
- この部屋(へや)は高(たか)くなくて安(やす)いです - this room isn't expensive and is cheap

## Register
Register: neutral
くて-chaining works identically in casual and polite speech — only the final predicate's copula (です vs plain) carries the formality tier.

## Often confused with
- [5.  Nは + なadj で - AND](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/naadj-de-and-connecting-adjectives) — test: Is the word being chained an い-adjective (→ くて), or a な-adjective/Noun (→ で)?

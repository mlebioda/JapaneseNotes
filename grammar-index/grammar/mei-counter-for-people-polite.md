# 名 (めい) - counter for people (polite)

> A polite/formal counter for people, used in service and reservation contexts.

## Structure

Register: formal/service counter (restaurants, reservations, ceremonies) — contrast with the casual/neutral 人（にん）used in everyday conversation.

- 一名（いちめい） - one person (polite)
- 二名（にめい） - two people (polite)
- 三名（さんめい） - three people (polite)
- 四名（よんめい） - four people (polite)
- 五名（ごめい） - five people (polite)
- 六名（ろくめい） - six people (polite)
- 七名（ななめい） - seven people (polite)
- 八名（はちめい） - eight people (polite)
- 九名（きゅうめい） - nine people (polite)
- 十名（じゅうめい） - ten people (polite)
- 十五名（じゅうごめい） - fifteen people (polite)
- 二十三名（にじゅうさんめい） - twenty-three people (polite)
- 二十名（にじゅうめい） - twenty people (polite)
- 三十名（さんじゅうめい） - thirty people (polite)
- 四十名（よんじゅうめい） - forty people (polite)
- 五十名（ごじゅうめい） - fifty people (polite)
- 六十名（ろくじゅうめい） - sixty people (polite)
- 七十名（ななじゅうめい） - seventy people (polite)
- 八十名（はちじゅうめい） - eighty people (polite)
- 九十名（きゅうじゅうめい） - ninety people (polite)
- 百名（ひゃくめい） - one hundred people (polite)

## Examples

- 何名(なんめい)ですか - how many people? (polite)
- 何名様(なんめい)ですか - how many people? (very polite, used by service staff)

## Register
Register: polite-context typical
Used by restaurant/service staff, in reservations, and at ceremonies — the formal register counter for people, contrasted with the casual/neutral 人（にん）used in everyday conversation.

# ね - seeking approval

> Sentence-final particle seeking agreement or confirming shared knowledge — roughly "isn't it?" / "right?"

## Structure

[Sentence, plain or です／ます form] + ね

## Examples

- 明日(あした)の仕事(しごと)のしりょうですね - these are tomorrow's work materials, right?
- インドから来(き)ましたね？ - you're from India, right?
- KIMさんは韓国人(かんこくじん)ですね - Ms Kim is Korean, isn't she?

## Register
Register: neutral
ね attaches after plain or です／ます forms alike, so it's used in both casual and polite speech without changing formality.

## Often confused with

- [よ](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/yo) — test: Does the sentence present new information to the listener (→ よ), or seek agreement/confirm shared knowledge (→ ね)?

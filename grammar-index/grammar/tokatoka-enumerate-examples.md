# とか...とか - enumerate examples of things, behaviors or actions

> Lists example items from a set, implying there are more like them ("things like X and Y").

## Structure

とか...とか - and....and.... etc. Usually appears more than once, but a single とか is also common for a vague, non-committal suggestion. Attaches directly to the plain form of the item being listed.

### Noun
- N + とか → すし**とか**、そば**とか**を食べました - I ate sushi, soba and so on

### Verb (plain form)
- V-plain + とか → すしとか食べたい - I want something like sushi (single とか, vague suggestion)

### な-adjective / copula (plain だ)
- Plain-だ + とか → 好きだとか言っていた - he said something like he likes it

## Examples

- 昨日、日本料理を食べに行きました。すし**とか**、そば**とか**を食べました - Yesterday I went to a Japanese restaurant. I ate sushi, soba and so on
- A: 粗大ゴミは何ですか（そだい) B: 椅子とか、自転車とか、テレビとかは粗大ゴミです（いす、じてんしゃ、そだい) - A: What is bulky trash? B: Chairs, bicycles, TVs and so on
- A: 日本の文化は少し分かりますか。B:はい、歌舞伎とか、能とかを見たことがあります（ぶんか、かぶき、のう) - Do you know a little about Japanese culture? B: Yes, I have watched Kabuki and Noh etc....

## Use Cases

Casual/conversational — natural in speech but avoided in formal writing or business Japanese, where や...や(など) is used instead.

## Notes

Often confused with: や...や (softer, more neutral/written, also non-exhaustive), と (exhaustive listing - names every item), など (formal/written list-ending marker)

## Register
Register: neutral
Common in everyday spoken Japanese; avoided in formal writing or business contexts, where や...や(など) is preferred instead.

## Often confused with

- [Listing activities Vた り  ・・・します - two and more activities](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/tari) — test: Is this chaining full activities/states each in their own conjugated form, closed by します (たり), or picking a few illustrative example items from a set, often nouns (とか...とか)?

# N＋に＋します - speaker says what he/she decided on

> Expresses the speaker's decision — "I'll go with N" — when choosing among options.

## Structure

### Noun

- Present: N ＋ に ＋ します → 会議を3時にします。(かいぎ・じ) - I'll make the meeting 3 o'clock.

## Examples

- A: 彼女(かのじょ)へのプレゼント、何(なに)**にする**？ B: うーん、花(はな)かな。女(おんな)の子(こ)はみんな花(はな)が好(す)きでしょう。 - A: "What are you going to get/decide on for her present?" B: "Hmm, flowers I guess. All girls like flowers, right?"
- A: 部長(ぶちょう)、来週月曜日(らいしゅうげつようび)の会議(かいぎ)は何時(なんじ)**にしますか**。 B: 午後(ごご)3時(じ)**にしましょう**。 - A: General manager, what time will the meeting be next Monday? B: Let's make it 3:00 p.m.

## Notes

A separate pattern, ....します - a situation changed through a person's will, uses this same N+に+します surface form for a different function (causing a change of state, not choosing/deciding among options) — don't conflate the two.

## Register

Register: tiered
Ranges from casual 何にする？ up to polite 何になさいますか (honorific, using the sonkeigo verb なさる) at the top tier.

## Often confused with

- [V「dict」 こと ＋ に　＋ する - I decided (on my own will)](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/v-dict-koto-ni-suru-i-decided-on-my-own-will) — test: Is the decision about which verb/action to take (ことにする), or which noun/option to choose (にする)?

# そうです/そうだ - express an impression or inference based on direct observation - it looks

> Express a tentative guess based on what you can see — "it looks like...".

## Structure

### Verb
- V（ます stem）＋そうです → ふる→ふりそう、遅れる（おく）→おくれそう、買う→買いそう

### い-adjective
- い→そう＋です → うれしい→うれしそう、高い→高そう
- Exceptions: いい／よい→よさそう、ない→なさそう

### な-adjective
- ＋そうです → 便利（べんり）→便利そう、しずか→しずかそう

## Examples

- 雨(あめ)が降(ふ)りそうですね - it looks like it is going to rain
- あの子供は泣きそうです（な）- That child looks like he is going to cry
- 会議の資料、明日できそうですか - Does it look like the materials for the meeting will be finished tomorrow? (soft question, ok if the answer is no)
- 会議の資料、明日できますか - Will you finish the materials for the meeting tomorrow? (direct question, expects yes/no)
- あの犬は大きそうです - "That dog looks big." (a guess based on appearance, e.g. seen from a distance)
- あの犬は大きいです - "That dog is big." (a plain statement of fact, no guessing)

## Use Cases

そう（様態）expresses a tentative guess based on appearance — this is not a lexical restriction on specific adjectives. 大きい、長い、広い combine with そう normally (大きそう、長そう、広そう). The real point is that そう isn't used when the fact is already obvious/certain to the speaker — in that case use a plain statement (大きいです) or みたい instead. Not used with nouns.

## Notes

- Confusable: みたい ("seems, looks like") — similar meaning, but based on any kind of evidence, not just visual; N＋の／V plain＋みたい (see the formal comparison with ようです below for the closely related よう)

## Register

Register: neutral
そう（様態）attaches to the plain stem regardless of formality; the sentence-final そうだ (plain) / そうです (polite) choice follows the surrounding speech level rather than the pattern imposing one.

## Often confused with

- [V「plain」そうです / そうだ - It is said that / I heard](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/sou-desu-hearsay) — test: Is this a direct visual impression of how something looks right now, attaching to the stem (そう様態: 降りそう), or reported hearsay from another source, attaching to the plain form (そう伝聞: 降るそうだ)?
- [...ようです](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/you-desu) — test: Is this a direct, immediate visual impression from what the speaker sees right now (そう様態), or an inference from indirect evidence, reasoning, or general knowledge (ようです)?
- [。。。らしいです - it seems](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/rashii-desu) — test: Is this a direct visual impression from what the speaker can see right now (そう様態), or hearsay/a typical impression based on outside information (らしいです)?

# 名詞（めいし） + 中（ちゅう・じゅう）- during

> 中 attached to a noun expresses "during," "throughout," or "in the middle of," with the reading (ちゅう or じゅう) depending on the meaning.

## 1. Ongoing action or state (中 = ちゅう)

### Structure

N + 中（ちゅう） → 仕事中（しごとちゅう）- during work (= 仕事をしています)

### Examples

- 仕事**中**（しごとちゅう）- during work
- 電話**中**（でんわちゅう）- during phone call
- 部長(ぶちょう)は会議**中**(かいぎちゅう)です。 - The manager is in a meeting. (during)
- 先生(せんせい)は研究**中**(けんきゅうちゅう)です。 - The teacher is doing research. (during)
- あの留学生(りゅうがくせい)は勉強**中**(べんきょうちゅう)です。 - That international student is studying. (during)

## 2. Period of time (中 = ちゅう or じゅう, depending on the word)

### Structure

N + 中（ちゅう） → 三月中（さんがつちゅう） - during March
N + 中（じゅう） → 今日中（きょうじゅう）- during today; 今年中（ことしじゅう）- during this year

### Examples

- 三月中(さんがつちゅう) - during March
- 今日中(きょうじゅう) - during today
- 今年中(ことしじゅう) - during this year
- この仕事(しごと)は今月中(こんげつちゅう)にやらなければなりません。 - We have to finish this work within this month.
- 飛行機(ひこうき)は午前中(ごぜんちゅう)に着(つ)きます。 - The flight will arrive in the morning.
- 今日中(きょうじゅう)に申(もう)し込(こ)まないと、間(ま)に合(あ)いません。 - We will be late if we don't apply for it by the end of today.

## 3. Whole extent — place or area (中 = じゅう)

### Structure

N + 中（じゅう） → 日本中（にほんじゅう）- throughout Japan; 世界中（せかいじゅう）- whole planet

### Examples

- 日本中（にほん**じゅう**）- **throughout** Japan
- 世界中（せかい**じゅう**）- **whole** planet
- あの子(こ)はとても頭(あたま)が良(よ)いです。学校中(がっこうじゅう)の人(ひと)が知(し)っています。 - That child is really clever. All the people in school know that. (whole school)
- 万里(ばんり)の長城(ちょうじょう)は**世界中**(せかいじゅう)で有名(ゆうめい)です。 - The Great Wall is famous throughout the **whole** world.

## Register
Register: neutral
中 as a noun-suffix works the same way across casual and formal speech; it is a structural/temporal marker, not a politeness marker.

## Often confused with

- [2. V「て形」いるところです - action in progress right now](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/tokoro-desu-teiru-form) — test: Is this a specific action caught in progress right now, or a general/habitual timeframe like "during work" or "during this month"?
- [間（あいだ）- between, during, among](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/aida-between-during-among) — test: Is the action happening continuously throughout the entire span (中), or at some specific point within the span (間に)?
- [N(time) + まで +に + V - BY, until (do)](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/ntime-made-ni-v-by-until-do) — test: Is this expressing "by/within this period" with 中 (→ N中に), or a deadline for a one-time completed action (→ N(time) + までに + V)?

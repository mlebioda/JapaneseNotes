# Vないでください - please don't do it

> A polite request asking someone not to do something.

## Structure

Verb (ない-form) + でください

- V「ない」でください → 売らないでください（う）- please don't sell it

## Examples

- 売(う)らないでください - please don't sell it
- 忘れないでください（わす) - please don't forget
- 消(け)さないでください - please don't turn off
- 病院（びょういん）で 携帯電話（けいたいでんわ）を 使（つか）わないでください。 - Please don't use your cellphone in the hospital.
- これから テストを 始（はじ）めますので、本（ほん）は 見（み）ないでください。 - We're about to start the test now, so please don't look at your book.
- この部屋（へや）で タバコを 吸（す）わないでください。 - Please don't smoke in this room.

## Register

Register: polite-context typical
ください softens the negative request into a polite instruction; appropriate with teachers, service staff, on signs, and in formal spoken requests to people you're not close to. Dropping ください (plain Vないで alone) makes it casual — 見ないで ("don't look") — used between friends/family or in emotional speech.

## Often confused with

- [V「dict」＋な - Don't do...](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/vdictna-dont-do) — test: Is the request polite/asking (ください) or blunt/commanding (plain form + な)?
- [V「て形」はいけません - is forbidden to do something](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/v-tewa-ikemasen-forbidden) — test: Is this a polite request asking someone not to do something (Vないでください), or a blunter, rule-like prohibition (てはいけません)?

# **んです**/のです - express the situation to provide a reason, explaining something

> Frame a statement or question as explaining a reason or shared context, rather than a plain fact.

## Structure

### Verb
- V plain＋んです／のです → 電車が遅れたんです - The train was late

### い-adjective
- い-adjective＋んです／のです → 部屋がくらいんですが。。。 - The room is dark...

### Question / Softening forms
- んですか／のですか → どうして会社に遅れたんですか - Why were you late for work?
- んですが／のですが → すみません。渋谷駅（しぶやえき）に行きたいんですが、どう行きますか。 - Excuse me, I'd like to ask the way to Shibuya station.

## Examples

- バスが遅れたんですから、遅れました。（おく）- I was late because the bus was late. (explicit reason, spoken)
- バスが遅れたから、遅れました。（おく）- I was late because the bus was late. (plain statement of fact, no んですから framing)
- どうして会社に遅れたんですか（おく）- Why were you late for work? (spoken)
- 電車が遅れたんです(おく) - The train was late (spoken)
- どうして会社に遅れたのですか（おく）- Why were you late for work? (written/formal register)
- 電車が遅れたのです(おく) - The train was late (written/formal register)
- 今日、どうして学校に来なかったんですか。（きょう、こ）- Why didn't you come to school today? (probing, wants an explanation)
- すみません。秋葉原駅に行きたいんですが、どうやって行きますか（あきはばらえき) - Excuse me, I want to go to Akihabara station, how do I get there? (softly introducing the topic before asking)
- ちょっと、聞(き)きたいんですが、あんどうさんはどちらにいますか - Excuse me, may I ask where Miss Ando is?
- 部屋(へや)がくらいんですが。。。 - The room is dark... (the request "please turn on the light" is left unsaid — んですが makes it obvious)
- すみません。渋谷駅（しぶやえき）に行きたいんですが、どう行きますか。（い）- Excuse me, I'd like to ask the way to Shibuya station. (polite, soft, topic-introducing)
- すみません。渋谷駅（しぶやえき）に行きたいです。どう行きますか。（い）- Excuse me, I want to go to Shibuya station. How do I get there? (more direct, no softening)

## Use Cases

んです／のです applies to both explaining a reason for something, introducing a topic before asking about it, softening the opening of a question, and marking shared context (the speaker expects the listener to infer the rest). な-adjectives and nouns attach with な before んです／のです (な-adjective＋な＋んです／のです, N＋な＋んです／のです) — not directly exemplified in this lesson.

## Register

Register: tiered (んです, のです)
んです is spoken language, used across casual to polite speech. のです is written / formal-spoken language — same grammar and same use cases as んです, just swapped in for a more formal register.

## Often confused with

- [。。。ので - because](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/node-because) — test: Is this stating a direct cause-and-effect plainly (ので), or framing the situation as background explanation or shared context rather than a strict logical connector (んです)?

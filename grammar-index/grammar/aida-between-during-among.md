# 間（あいだ）- between, during, among

> 間 marks a range or span. Which meaning it takes depends on the particle that follows: から〜まで marks a distance/range, に marks a specific action happening within a span ("during"), and で marks a state holding across a group ("among").

## 1. Range (distance/time from N to N)

### Structure
- N から N までの間 → 駅(えき)から家(いえ)までの間(あいだ) - from the station to my home (distance)

### Examples
- 駅(えき)から家(いえ)までの間(あいだ) - from the station to my home (distance)
- １０時(じ)から11時(じ)までの間(あいだ) - in the range of 10:00 to 11:00

## 2. During (a specific action within the span)

### Structure
- N の間に + V → 休(やす)みの間(あいだ)に勉強(べんきょう)する - to study during the holiday

### Examples
- 休(やす)みの間(あいだ)に勉強(べんきょう)する - to study during the holiday

## 3. Among (a state holds across a group)

### Structure
- N の間で + V → 学生(がくせい)の間(あいだ)で人気(にんき)がある - popular among students

### Examples
- 学生(がくせい)の間(あいだ)で人気(にんき)がある - popular among students

## Register
Register: neutral
間 is a register-neutral structural noun — usable identically in casual, business, or written Japanese.

## Often confused with

- [名詞（めいし） + 中（ちゅう・じゅう）- during](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/meishi-chuu-juu-during) — test: Is the action happening at some specific point within the span (間に), or continuously throughout the entire span (中)?
- [ながら - While - 2 actions occuring at the same time](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/nagara) — test: Must both actions happen at the same time by the same subject (ながら), or does the event just happen at some point within a time span (間に)?
- [A....とき...B - while A, B](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/toki-non-past) — test: Does something happen at some point within a bounded span (間に), or is this a general point-in-time "when" (とき)?
- [まで](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/made) — test: Does the event happen at some specific point within a bounded span (間に), or does the action/state continue right up until an endpoint (まで)?
- [weeks](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/weeks) — test: Is 間 marking the span between two named points (AとBの間), or is it a bound counter suffix attached directly after a number of weeks (週間)?
- [Period of time](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/period-of-time) — test: Is 間 marking the interval between two named points (AとBの間), or a bound suffix directly forming a duration out of hours and minutes (時間、分)?

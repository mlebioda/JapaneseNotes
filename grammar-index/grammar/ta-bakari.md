# V「た」ばかり - action that just ended

> Attaches to the plain past (た-form) of a verb to mark an action as subjectively recent — it just happened.

## Structure

- V「た」ばかり → ご飯（はん）を食べ**た**ばかり - I just ate a meal
- 。。。ばかり＋の＋NOUN → 生まれた（う）**ばかりの赤**（あか）ちゃん - baby who was just born

## Examples

- 帰(かえ)ったばかり - I just came back
- ご飯(はん)を食べ**た**ばかり - I just ate a meal
- 生(う)まれた**ばかりの赤**(あか)ちゃん - baby who was just born
- 新(あたら)しい家(いえ)に引(ひ)っ越(こ)し**たばかり**なので、まだ片付(かたづ)けていません - I just moved to a new house, so I haven't tidied up yet.
- A: 金(きむ)さんはいますか。B: 出(で)かけ**た**ばかりです - A: Is Miss Kim here? B: She just went out.

## Notes

Don't confuse this with Noun + ばかり meaning "only/nothing but" (e.g. 甘(あま)いものばかり食(た)べる - only eats sweet things) — that is an unrelated, restrictive use of ばかり attached to a noun, not to a た-form verb.

## Register

Register: neutral
ばかり attaches to the plain past (た-form) regardless of formality; the sentence's own copula (です/だ) after it carries the politeness level, not ばかり itself.

## Often confused with

- [3. V「た形」ところです - action has just finished](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/tokoro-desu-ta-form) — test: Is this marking the exact instant an action finished (たところです), or that it happened somewhat recently, possibly a little while ago (たばかり)?
- [V「て」しまいます - Finish something completely/complete an action](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/vteshimaimasu-finish-something-completely-complete-an-action) — test: Does the sentence simply mark that something happened recently (たばかり), or emphasize that the action is fully/completely done, often with a nuance of regret (てしまいました)?
- [た形 + ことがあります - have you ever](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/ta-kotogaarimasu-have-you-ever) — test: does this describe an action that just finished a moment ago (た形＋ばかり), or a lifetime experience of ever having done something (た形＋ことがあります)?
- [行ったことがありました - I once did ~ (recalling a single past experience)](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/ttakotogaarimashita-i-once-did-recalling-a-single-past-experience) — test: does this mark an action that had just finished at a past moment (た形＋ばかり), or recall a specific one-time experience from further back (た形＋ことがありました)?

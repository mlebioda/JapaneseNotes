# を

> Particle を marks the direct object of an action (primary use), and also the starting point of departure, a transit location, or the noun in N+をします constructions.

## 1. Object of an action

The most common use. Marks what is directly acted upon by the verb.

### Structure

Noun + を + Verb

### Examples

- ご飯を食べる → to eat a meal

## 2. Starting point of departure

Marks the place one leaves from.

### Structure

Place + を + departure verb (出る, 離れる, etc.)

### Examples

- 会社を出る → to leave the company
- 東京を離れる → to leave Tokyo

## 3. Transit spot

Marks a place one moves through or along.

### Structure

Place + を + motion verb (走る, 歩く, etc.)

### Examples

- こうえんを走る → to run through the park

## 4. With する

N + をします to express performing an activity.

### Structure

Noun + をします

### Examples

- 会議をします → to hold a meeting
- 仕事をします → to do a job

## Examples

- ご飯を食べる — to eat a meal.
- 会社を出る — to leave the company.
- こうえんを走る — to run through the park.
- 会議をします — to hold a meeting.

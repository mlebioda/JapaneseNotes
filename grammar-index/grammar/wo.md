# を

> Marks the object of a transitive verb, a motion's starting/transit point, or combines with する — several distinct uses of the particle を.

## 1. Object marker

### Structure

- N + を + V(transitive) → ご飯を食べる (to eat a meal)

### Examples

- ご飯を食べる - to eat a meal

## 2. Departure point

### Structure

- N + を + V(motion, departure) → 会社を出る (leave the company)

### Examples

- 会社を出る - leave the company
- 東京をはなれる - leave Tokyo

## 3. Transit spot

### Structure

- N + を + V(motion, path) → こうえんを走(はし)る (run in the park)

### Examples

- こうえんを走(はし)る - run in the park

## 4. する collocation

### Structure

- N + を + します／しました → 会議(かいぎ)をします (to hold a meeting)

### Examples

- 会議(かいぎ)をします - to hold a meeting
- 仕事をします - do a job
- 何をしますか - what are you going to do?

## Notes

Often confused with から (departure point) — test: are you emphasizing the act of leaving/exiting a place (を), or the point something originates/starts from (から)? 家を出る (leave/exit the house) vs 家から出る (come out from the house, origin point). (No standalone grammar-index file for departure-point から yet — left as prose.)

## Register

Register: neutral

を carries no politeness of its own — register comes from the verb ending.

## Often confused with

- [で - where the action will take place](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/de-where-the-action-will-take-place) — test: does the verb describe movement across/along the space (を), or is the space just the static location where an action happens (で)? 公園を走る (run through/along the park) vs 公園で走る (run in the park, as the location).

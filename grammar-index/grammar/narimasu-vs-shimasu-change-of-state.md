# ....します - a situation changed through a person's will (compare なる, a change due to nature/circumstance, not a person's will)

> Marks a change of state deliberately caused by a person's will, in contrast to なります which marks a change happening naturally.

## 1. なります - change due to nature/circumstance

### Structure

The target of change is marked with を, whether followed by an い-adj stem+く or a な-adj/noun+に:

- い-adjective
	- Present: Adj「く」＋ なります → 最近(さいきん)、あたたか**くなりました** - it became warm recently

### Examples

- 最近(さいきん)、あたたか**くなりました** - it became warm recently

## 2. します - change caused by person's will

### Structure

The target of change is marked with を, whether followed by an い-adj stem+く or a な-adj/noun+に:

- い-adjective
	- Present: Adj「く」＋ します → エアコンをつけて、部屋を暖か**くしましょう** - Let's turn on the air conditioner and warm up the room
- な-adjective / Noun
	- Present: Adj/N ＋ に ＋ します → 部屋をきれいにしました。(へや) - I cleaned my room.

### Examples

- エアコンをつけて、部屋を暖か**くしましょう** - Let's turn on the air conditioner and warm up the room
- ラジオの音(おと)を大(おお)きくしてください。 - Please turn up the radio volume.
- 部屋(へや)をきれい**にしました**。 - I cleaned my room.
- パンフレットを半分(はんぶん)だけ**にして**、あとは箱(はこ)に何(なに)か軽(かる)いものを入(い)れてください。 - Please cut the pamphlets down to half, and put something light in the box for the rest

## Notes

A separate pattern, N＋に＋します - speaker says what he/she decided on, uses this same N+に+します surface form for a different function (choosing/deciding among options, not causing a change of state) — don't conflate the two.

## Register

Register: neutral
Both なります and します work in casual and polite speech alike; formality is carried by the conjugation of なる／する itself (plain vs です／ます), not by the construction.

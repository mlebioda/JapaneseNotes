# ADJ＋の - substitute of noun

> Attach の to an adjective to stand in for an omitted or already-mentioned noun ("the ~ one").

## Structure

- ADJ + の — the Adj one
	- 白いの — the white one

## Examples

- さくらは、とてもきれいで白いのとピンクのがありました - the cherry blossoms were beautiful, and there were white ones and pink ones
- A: 白いくつと 黒いくつと どちらが好きですか B: 白いのが好きです - A: Which shoes do you like, the white or the black? B: I like white ones.
- A: このアパートは 二つの 部屋が 空いています B: せまいのは 安いです。広いのは高いです（へや、あ、やす、ひろ）- A: There are two rooms available in this apartment. B: The small one is cheap. The big one is expensive

## Use Cases

This の stands in for an omitted or already-mentioned noun (白いの = "the white one") — different from possessive の (私の本) and from explanatory んです, all three of which look identical in writing. No register restriction of its own; politeness comes from the sentence-final copula as usual (白いのです vs 白いのだ).

## Register

Register: neutral
This の has no register restriction of its own; politeness comes from the sentence-final copula (白いのです vs 白いのだ).

## Often confused with

- [の 名詞化（めいしか） - nominaliser (when word is added after an adjective or verb)](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/no-meishika) — test: Does the の stand in for an omitted noun that was already mentioned (白いの = "the white one"), or turn a whole verb/adjective clause into a noun (歌うのが好きです)?

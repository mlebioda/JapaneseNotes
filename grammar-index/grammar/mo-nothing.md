# 何も - nothing

> 何も + negative verb = "nothing" — the negative-polarity counterpart of 何か.

## Structure

- 何も + V negative

## Examples

- 何(なに)もありません。 - There is nothing.

## Register
Register: neutral
Formality is carried entirely by the negative verb that follows; 何も itself does not change with politeness level.

## Often confused with

- [何か - something](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/ka-something) — test: Is the sentence negative (→ 何も), or affirmative (→ 何か)?

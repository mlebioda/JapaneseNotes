# 回 - times

> How to count "times" (frequency) using 回, one through ten.

## Structure

Number + 回（かい）

- １回（いっかい）- one time
- ２回（にかい）- two times
- ３回（さんかい）- three times
- ４回（よんかい）- four times
- ５回（ごかい）- five times
- ６回（ろっかい）- six times
- ７回（ななかい）- seven times
- ８回（はっかい）- eight times
- ９回（きゅうかい）- nine times
- １０回（じゅっかい）- ten times
- 何回（なんかい）- how many times

## Register

Register: neutral
Counter readings themselves don't carry politeness — they attach identically in polite and plain sentences; only the surrounding verb/copula changes with register.

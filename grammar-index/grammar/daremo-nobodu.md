# だれも - nobodu

> だれも + negative verb = "nobody" — the negative-polarity counterpart of だれか.

## Structure

- だれも + V negative

## Examples

- だれも来(き)ませんでした。 - Nobody came.

## Register
Register: neutral
Formality is carried entirely by the negative verb that follows; だれも itself does not change with politeness level.

## Often confused with

- [だれか - someone](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/dareka-someone) — test: Is the sentence negative (→ だれも), or affirmative (→ だれか)?

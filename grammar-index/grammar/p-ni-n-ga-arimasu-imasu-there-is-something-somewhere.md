# PにNがあります・います - there is something somewhere

> Introducing something/someone that exists at a place — あります for inanimate things, います for animate things.

## Structure

Place に Noun が あります・います

Rule: あります is for inanimate things (objects, plants) — e.g. 椅子, 机, トイレ. います is for animate things (people, animals) — e.g. だれ, 部長.

## Examples

- あそこに椅子(いす)と机(つくえ)があります - There is a chair and desk over there
- エレベーターの　となり に　トイレがあります - there is a restroom next to the elevator
- 会議室(かいぎしつ)にだれがいますか - who is in the meeting room?
- 会議室(かいぎしつ)に部長(ぶちょう)がいます - The general manager is in the meeting room

## Use Cases

あります・います are the polite (ます-style) forms; the plain/casual forms are ある／いる (not covered in this lesson).

## Register
Register: polite-context typical
あります／います are the ます-style (teineigo) forms taught here; the plain/casual equivalents ある／いる are used among friends and family but are a separate pattern not covered by this file.

## Often confused with

- [Something is somewhere](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/something-is-somewhere) — test: is the noun new information being introduced (が), or an already-known topic whose location is being stated (は)?
- [で - where the action will take place](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/de-where-the-action-will-take-place) — test: is the verb a static existence verb (あります／います, needs に), or a dynamic action verb (needs で)?

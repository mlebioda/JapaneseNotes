# を 過ぎる（す）- to pass somewhere and further go ahead/ time

> Cross past a place, time, or boundary and continue beyond it.

## 1. を過ぎる - passing a place/point

### Structure

- 場所／時間＋を＋過ぎる → 京都を過ぎる - to pass Kyoto

### Examples

- 京都を過ぎる（す）- to pass Kyoto
- 6時を過ぎる（す）- It's past 6 o'clock

## 2. が過ぎる - time elapsing

### Structure

- 時間＋が＋過ぎる → 時間がはやく過ぎる - time passes quickly

### Examples

- 時間がはやく過ぎる（す）- time passes quickly

## Notes

- 過ぎる always indicates crossing some kind of boundary (place, time, quantity, intensity).
- In casual speech it's sometimes shortened: 「食べすぎちゃった」= "I ate too much."

## Register

Register: neutral
過ぎる is a plain descriptive verb used identically across casual and polite speech; the casual contraction noted above is a colloquial shortening, not a separate register.

## Often confused with
- [まで](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/made) — test: Does the action cross past the point and continue beyond (を過ぎる), or does it stop at/extend up to the given point (まで)?

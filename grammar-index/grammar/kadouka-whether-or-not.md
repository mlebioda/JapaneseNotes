# かどうか - whether or not

> Express uncertainty about whether something is the case — "whether or not...".

## Structure

### Verb
- V plain形＋かどうか → 飲み会に参加するかどうか教えて下さい - Please tell me whether or not you will attend the drinking party

### い-adjective
- い-adjective＋かどうか → この料理がおいしいかどうかわかりません - I don't know whether this dish is delicious or not

### な-adjective
- な-adjective＋かどうか → 高橋さんが元気かどうか知りません - I don't know if Mr Takahashi is fine or not

### Noun
- N＋かどうか → 彼が学生かどうか知りません - I don't know whether or not he is a student

## Examples

- この料理がおいしいかどうかわかりません - I don't know whether this dish is delicious or not
- 彼が学生かどうか知りません - I don't know whether or not he is a student
- 飲み会に参加するかどうか教えて下さい（のみかい、さんか、おし）- Please tell me whether or not you will attend the drinking party
- 高橋さんが元気かどうか知りません - I don't know if Mr Takahashi is fine or not

## Use Cases

Use かどうか when you are not sure whether something is the case, usually with verbs like 知る、わかる、言う、教える.

## Notes

- Confusable: か alone (AかBか — a direct choice between alternatives) vs かどうか (embedded yes/no uncertainty, "whether or not")

## Register

Register: neutral
No restriction — used across all speech levels, from casual to polite.

## Often confused with

- [疑問詞（ぎもんし）...か ... - indirect question](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/gimonshika-indirect-question) — test: Is this a yes/no embedded question with no wh-word (かどうか), or a wh-word (だれ／いつ／どこ／いくら／何) embedded question (疑問詞...か)?

# 匹 (ひき) - small Animals / fish/insects

> How to count small animals, fish, and insects using 匹, one through ten.

## Structure

Number + 匹（ひき／びき／ぴき）

- １匹（いっぴき）- one (small animal)
- ２匹（にひき）- two (small animals)
- ３匹（さんびき）- three (small animals)
- ４匹（よんひき）- four (small animals)
- ５匹（ごひき）- five (small animals)
- ６匹（ろっぴき）- six (small animals)
- ７匹（ななひき）- seven (small animals)
- ８匹（はっぴき）- eight (small animals)
- ９匹（きゅうひき）- nine (small animals)
- １０匹（じゅっぴき）- ten (small animals)
- 何匹（なんびき）- how many (small animals)

## Register

Register: neutral
Counter readings themselves don't carry politeness — they attach identically in polite and plain sentences; only the surrounding verb/copula changes with register.

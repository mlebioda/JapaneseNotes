# A....とき...B - while A, B

> Marks a situation or point in time when the main clause happens — "when/while [A], [B]." Attaches to verbs (dictionary or ない form), い/な-adjectives, and nouns.

## Structure

B defines the tense of the sentence.

### Verb
- Present: V-dict + とき → 時間があるとき銀行に行きます - I will go to the bank when I have time.
- Negative: V-ない + とき → 鈴木さんがいないとき、山田さんはこの仕事をやります - When Mr. Suzuki is not here, Mr. Yamada will take over this job.

### い-adjective
- Present: Adj + とき → 若いとき、たくさんの国に行きました - I went to a lot of countries when I was young.

### な-adjective
- Present: Adj + な + とき → 暇なとき、インターネットで買い物をします - When I have time, I do shopping online.

### Noun
- Present: N + の + とき → 学生のとき、よく公園へ花を見に行きました - I often went to a park to see flowers when I was a student.

## Examples

- 時間があるとき銀行に行きます (じかん・ぎんこう・い) - I will go to the bank when I have time.
- 鈴木さんがいないとき、山田さんはこの仕事をやります (すずき・やまだ・しごと) - When Mr. Suzuki is not here, Mr. Yamada will take over this job.
- 若いとき、たくさんの国に行きました (わか・くに・い) - I went to a lot of countries when I was young.
- 暇なとき、インターネットで買い物をします (ひま・か・もの) - When I have time, I do shopping online.
- 学生のとき、よく公園へ花を見に行きました (がくせい・こうえん・はな・み・い) - I often went to a park to see flowers when I was a student.
- アメリカへ**行くとき**、ハンバーガーを食べました - I ate hamburgers **when** I went to America
- 家に帰るとき、両親に会った (いえ・かえ・りょうしん・あ) - I met my parents when I went home

## Notes

Comparison with the past-とき form (Vた形＋とき, covered separately — see the container's other sub-topic): 行ったとき ("after I went") vs 行くとき ("when I went / on the way"):
- アメリカへ**行ったとき**、ハンバーガーを食べました - I ate hamburgers **after** I went to America
- アメリカへ**行くとき**、ハンバーガーを食べました - I ate hamburgers **when** I went to America

Timelines:
- 行ったとき (past とき): 1. Arrive in America → 2. Eat hamburger (eating happens after arrival)
- 行くとき (non-past とき): 1. Eat hamburger → 2. Arrive in America (eating happens before arrival is complete, e.g. on the way)

Often confused with:
- ながら - simultaneous, same-subject only
- 間(あいだ) - a stretch/span of time, contrasts with とき's point-in-time framing
- たら - can also mean "when" for a conditional/one-time event, a very common confusion with とき
- 際(さい) - formal/written register variant of とき

## Register

Register: neutral
とき is used across every register. A more formal/written near-synonym is 際 (さい), common in announcements and business writing (e.g. ご利用の際は...).

## Often confused with

- [ながら - While - 2 actions occuring at the same time](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/nagara) — test: Is this a general "when" with no simultaneity or same-subject requirement (とき), or must both actions happen at the same time by the same subject (ながら)?
- [Vた＋とき＋V - do.... after doing something](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/toki-past) — test: Does B happen around or before A completes, general "when" (Vとき), or specifically after A is complete (Vたとき)?
- [間（あいだ）- between, during, among](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/aida-between-during-among) — test: Is this a general point in time when something happens (とき), or specifically marking that something happens at some point within a bounded span (間に)?

# 。。。が。。。 -

> The particle が used to mean "but" between two clauses, or to softly introduce a new topic.

## 1. But (contrast)

### Structure

- Clause1 + が + Clause2 → 昨日 山田さんは はじめてダンスしました が とても上手でした - Yamada danced for the first time yesterday but was very good

### Examples

- 昨日 山田さんは はじめてダンスしました が とても上手でした - Yamada danced for the first time yesterday but was very good

## 2. Topic introduction

### Structure

- Clause + が, ... → すみませんが.... - excuse me (with bring topic)

### Examples

- すみません**が**.... - excuse me (with bring topic)
- すみません**が**。。。すずきさんはいませんか - excuse me, is Suzuki here? (bring topic)
- 昨日あげた本ですが、もう読みましたか（きのう、よ) - Have you read the book I gave you yesterday? (bring topic)
- こんにちは、ABCグローバル株式会社（かぶしきがいしゃ）の安藤（あんどう）ですが、山田（やまだ）さんはこちらにいますか。 - Hello, I am Ando from ABC Global Co., Ltd. Is Mr. Yamada here? (bring topic, introduction first then question)
- 自転車（じてんしゃ）で来（き）ましたが、自転車（じてんしゃ）でそのレストランに行（い）ってもいいですか。 - I came by bicycle, can I go to that restaurant by bicycle? (bring topic, first introduced mean of transport, then question)

## Register

Register: neutral
が itself is register-flexible (works in both polite and plain sentences). Casual speech typically prefers けど instead; written/formal Japanese prefers けれども/けれど. The topic-introducing use (すみませんが...) is itself a common polite cushioning device, frequent in business Japanese.

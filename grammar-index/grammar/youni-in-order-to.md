# ように - act on purpose or goal in mind, in order to, so that

> Marks a goal or purpose one acts toward — "in order to / so that."

## Structure

### Verb

- Present (affirmative, non-volitional/potential): V「dict, often potential」＋ように → 日本語(にほんご)が上手(じょうず)に**なれ**る**ように**、毎日勉強(まいにちべんきょう)しています。 - I study Japanese every day so that I can become good at Japanese.
- Negative: V「nai」＋ように → ガラスが割(わ)れないように、新聞紙(しんぶんし)で包(つつ)みます - I will pack the glass with newspaper so that it won't break

The affirmative side is almost always a non-volitional/potential verb (見えるように, なれるように); plain volitional verbs don't take affirmative ように naturally.

## Examples

- 電車(でんしゃ)に間(ま)に合(あ)う**ように**、駅(えき)まで走(はし)って行(い)きます - In order to catch the train, I will run to the station
- ガラスが割(わ)れないように、新聞紙(しんぶんし)で包(つつ)みます - I will pack the glass with newspaper (in order to) so that it won't break
- 遅刻(ちこく)しない**ように**、一(ひと)つ前(まえ)の電車(でんしゃ)に乗(の)りました。 - In order not to be late, I took the earlier train.
- 日本語(にほんご)が上手(じょうず)に**なれ**る**ように**、毎日勉強(まいにちべんきょう)しています。 - I study Japanese every day **so that** I can become good at Japanese.
- 試合(しあい)に**勝(か)て**る**ように**、みんな毎日練習(まいにちれんしゅう)を頑張(がんば)っています。 - In order to win the game, everyone tries their best to practice every day.

## Register

Register: neutral
Works in both plain and polite speech; formality is carried by the surrounding verb/copula, not by ように itself.

## Often confused with

- [ために - for (purpose of)](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/tameni-for-purpose-of) — test: Is the action volitional and directly controllable by the subject reaching a goal (ために), or a non-volitional/potential outcome the subject hopes will happen (ように)?

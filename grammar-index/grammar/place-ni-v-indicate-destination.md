# Place + に ＋　V - indicate destination

> Uses に before a movement verb to indicate the specific destination.

## Structure

Place + に + V (movement verb)

## Examples

- ラジさんは4月から大学に行きます — Raj will go to university from April
- 会社に行く — to go to the company

## Notes

に can be replaced with へ in most cases, but they differ:
- に points to a specific destination (会社に行く — go TO the company)
- へ points toward the direction of an objective (アメリカへ行く — go toward America)

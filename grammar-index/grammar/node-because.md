# 。。。ので - because

> A neutral-to-polite causal connector, "because ~" — softer and more objective-sounding than から.

## Structure

### Verb
- Present/Past (plain) + ので → 風邪(かぜ)を引(ひ)いたので昨日(きのう)学校(がっこう)を休(やす)みました - Because I caught a cold, I was absent from school yesterday.

### い-adjective
- Plain + ので → 明日仕事(あしたしごと)が早(はや)いので、今晩(こんばん)早(はや)く寝(ね)るね。 - Because I will work early tomorrow, I will sleep early tonight.

### な-adjective
- Adj + な + ので → ここは静(しず)かなので、引越(ひっこ)ししたいと思(おも)います。 - Because it is quiet here, I think I want to move here.

### Noun
- N + な + ので → もう夏(なつ)なので、海(うみ)に行(い)きましょう。 - Because it's summer, let's go to the beach.

## Examples

- 風邪を引いたので昨日学校を休みました（かぜ、ひ) - Because I caught a cold, I was absent from school yesterday
- 今朝電車が遅れたので、学校に遅刻しました。(けさ・でんしゃ・おく・がっこう・ちこく) - Because the train was late this morning, I was late to school.
- 明日仕事が早いので、今晩早く寝るね。(あした・しごと・はや・こんばん・はや・ね) - Because I will work early tomorrow, I will sleep early tonight.
- ここは静かなので、引越ししたいと思います。(しず・ひっ・こ・おも) - Because it is quiet here, I think I want to move here.
- もう夏なので、海に行きましょう。(なつ・うみ・い) - Because it's summer, let's go to the beach.

## Use Cases

Often left dangling at the end of a sentence to soften a refusal or excuse, trailing off without finishing the thought - e.g. すみません、今日はちょっと忙しいので… (I'm a bit busy today, so…). In casual speech, often contracted to んで (今日は忙しいんで…).

## Notes

Often confused with: から (direct/subjective reason, can stand alone as an answer - なぜ？眠いから - unlike ので)

## Register
Register: tiered (casual: んで; standard: ので)
ので itself is neutral-to-polite — softer and more objective-sounding than から, preferred when avoiding a pushy/blaming tone in requests or apologies. In casual speech it's often contracted to んで.

## Often confused with
- [ために - for (purpose of)](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/tameni-for-purpose-of) — test: Is this stating a direct cause/reason (→ ので), or a goal/purpose being pursued (→ ために)?
- [。。。し、。。。し - conjunction](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/shishi-conjunction) — test: Is this one direct reason stated plainly (→ ので), or a non-exhaustive list of multiple reasons/qualities (→ し...し)?
- [**んです**/のです - express the situation to provide a reason, explaining something](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/ndesu-nodesu-explaining-reason) — test: Is this framing the situation as background explanation or shared context (→ んです), or stating a direct cause-and-effect plainly (→ ので)?
- [から - because](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/kara-because) — test: Is this a softer, more objective-sounding reason (→ ので), or a direct/subjective reason that could stand alone as an answer (→ から)?

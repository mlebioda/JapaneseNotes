# Vます形＋ませんか　Shall we? / Would you like? / Why don't you...?

> A polite way to propose or invite someone to do something, leaving the choice open to them.

## Structure

### Verb
- Vます形＋ませんか → いっしょに図書館に行きませんか (Would you like to go to the library with me?)

Three nuances of the same pattern:
- Shall we — いっしょにアルバイトをしませんか
- Would you like (often with いっしょに) — いっしょに図書館に行きませんか
- Suggesting something for the listener to do (not a request for a favor — that's てくれませんか／ていただけませんか) — 今日部屋をそうじしませんか

Often paired with いっしょに. Typical responses: いいですね／ちょっと.

## Examples

- 遊(あそ)びませんか - shall we play?
- しませんか - Shall we do?
- 行きませんか - Shall we go?
- いっしょにアルバイトをしませんか B:そうですね・ちょっと - Shall we do a part-time job together? B: Well... (a hesitant, indirect way of declining)
- A: いっしょに 図書館に行きませんか（としょかん）B: ええ、いいですね - Would you like to go to the library with me? B: Yeah, sounds great
- いっしょに歌を歌いませんか（うた）- Would you like to sing together?
- 来週(らいしゅう)の祝日(しゅくじつ)にいっしょに旅行(りょこう)しませんか - Would you like to travel with me on a holiday next week?
- ドアを閉(し)めませんか - Why don't you close the door?
- 図書館から本を借りませんか（としょかん、か）- Why don't you borrow a book from the library?
- 今日(きょう) 部屋(へや)をそうじしませんか - Why don't you clean your room today?

## Register

Register: tiered (polite ます-based vs. casual plain-form)
Polite (ます-based) — appropriate with colleagues, acquaintances, customers, teachers. Casual equivalent for close friends/family: plain-form V-ない？ (e.g. いっしょに行かない？)

## Often confused with

- [Vます形＋ましょう - Let's...](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/mashou) — test: does the sentence leave the choice open to the listener (ませんか) or assume they'll agree (ましょう)?
- [Vます形＋ましょうか - Shall I/We](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/mashouka) — test: is the speaker proposing a shared action open to refusal (ませんか) or offering to do something as a favor for the listener (ましょうか)?

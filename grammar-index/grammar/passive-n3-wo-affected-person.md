# N1 + は  + N2 + に N3 + を + V「passive form」- representing bad/good result of action on someone

> N1 is affected — usually negatively, occasionally positively — by an action N2 performed on N3, a part or possession of N1.

## Structure

- N1 + は + N2 + に N3 + を + V「passive form」- representing bad result of action
    - N1 - receives bad action (can be omitted when it's obvious)
    - N2 - initiate the action
    - N3 - is the thing that is harmed (body or possession)
    - Example:
        - 電車（でんしゃ）の中（なか）で私（わたし）**は**となりの人（ひと）**に**足（あし）**を**ふまれました - The person next to me stepped on my foot on train
    - Good action examples:
        - 私(わたし)は先生(せんせい)に作文(さくぶん)を褒(ほ)められた。 - I was praised by the teacher for my composition.

## Register
Register: neutral
Common in everyday narration of personal experience — works equally in casual speech (られた) and polite speech (られました).

## Examples

- 電車（でんしゃ）の中（なか）で私（わたし）**は**となりの人（ひと）**に**足（あし）**を**ふまれました - The person next to me stepped on my foot on train
- A: その手（て）、どうしたの？ B: 犬（いぬ）に手（て）をかまれちゃった - A: What's wrong with your hand? B: I had my hand bitten by a dog
- 弟(おとうと)は泥棒(どろぼう)にさいふを盗(ぬす)まれました。 - My younger brother had his wallet stolen by a thief.
- 妹(いもうと)**に**大好(だいす)きなケーキ**を**全部(ぜんぶ)食(た)べ**られた**。 - My favorite cake was eaten entirely by my younger sister.
- 私(わたし)は先生(せんせい)に作文(さくぶん)を褒(ほ)められた。 - I was praised by the teacher for my composition.

# 3. V「た形」ところです - action has just finished

> States that an action has just this instant finished — the た-form verb attached to ところです/ところでした.

## Structure

### Verb
- Present: V(た form) + ところです → たった今(いま)出(で)かけたところです - He just left.
- Past: V(た form) + ところでした → バスが出(で)たところでした - The bus had just left.

## Examples

- バス乗(の)り場(ば)についたら、バスが出(で)たところでした。 - When I arrived at the bus stop, the bus had just left.
- A: ただいま。 B: お帰(かえ)り。ちょうどご飯(はん)ができたところだよ。 - A: I'm home. B: Welcome back. Dinner's just ready.
- A: 山田(やまだ)さん、いらっしゃいますか。 B: たった今(いま)出(で)かけたところです。まだ会社(かいしゃ)の近(ちか)くにいると思(おも)います。 - A: Is Yamada-san here? B: He just left. I think he's still near the office.
- たった今(いま)出(で)かけたところです。まだ会社(かいしゃ)の近(ちか)くにいると思(おも)います。B: He/she just left just now. I think he/she is still near the office.

## Notes

Compares たところです to two other patterns often confused with it:

| Pattern | Nuance | Example |
|---|---|---|
| たところです | just finished — this instant, emphasizes the immediacy of completion | 出(で)かけたところです - He just left (this instant) |
| もう + た形 | "already" happened — a plain fact, no emphasis on recency | もう出(で)かけました - He has already left |
| たばかり | "just did" — recent, but looser than ところ; allows a bit more elapsed time | 出(で)かけたばかりです - He just left (recently) |

## Register

Register: neutral
Politeness comes only from the copula attached after it — だ (casual) vs です/でした (polite). Same shift applies to all three forms: 食べるところだ → 食べるところです, しているところだ → しているところです, 出たところだった → 出たところでした.

## Often confused with

- [1. V「dict」ところです - about to start](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/tokoro-desu-dict-form) — test: Has the action already just finished, or is it about to start?
- [2. V「て形」いるところです - action in progress right now](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/tokoro-desu-teiru-form) — test: Has the action just this instant ended, or is it still ongoing right now?
- [V「て-form」くる / きた / いく — action continuing over time](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/te-kuru-kita-iku) — test: has the action just this instant finished (たところです), or has it been gradually accumulating up to now (てきた)?
- [V「た」ばかり - action that just ended](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/ta-bakari) — test: Is this marking the exact instant an action finished (たところです), or that it happened somewhat recently, possibly a little while ago (たばかり)?

# が

> が marks N1 as the grammatical subject, with N2 predicated of it — often emphasizing or newly specifying which N1.

## Structure

- N1が N2です - N2 describes N1 (subject marked by が)

## Examples

- あの白(しろ)い建物(たてもの)がホテルです - that white building is the hotel (building is the subject)
- あの人が山田さんです - that person is Mr. Yamada (subject is Yamada)
- 山田さんは大阪に行きます - (Speaking of) Yamada, he's going to Osaka
- 山田さんが大阪に行きます(おおさか) - (Specifically) it is Yamada who is going to Osaka

## Register
Register: neutral
が is used at every formality tier; the sentence's politeness comes from its predicate (です／だ), not from が itself.

## Often confused with
- [N1 は N2 が ADJ です — N2 is a trait/feature of N1](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/n1-ha-n2-ga-adj-desu-n2-is-a-trait-feature-of-n1) — test: Does が mark the sole subject of a simple N1が N2です sentence, or a second/embedded subject describing a trait of an already-topic-marked N1 (N1は N2が ADJです)?

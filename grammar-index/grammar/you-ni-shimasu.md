# ようにします - working hard on / making an effort to

> The speaker makes an effort (or asks someone else to make an effort) to do — or not do — something.

## Structure

1. V「dict」+ ようにします - try to do something (positive effort)
2. V「ない」+ ようにします - try not to do something (negative effort)
3. V「dict/ない」+ ようにしています - an ongoing, standing commitment to (not) do something
4. V「dict/ない」+ ようにしてください - politer than てください; asks someone to be mindful/careful about an ongoing goal
   - Not for a direct one-time request or instruction: e.g. ケーキを食べるようにしてください is unnatural — eating one specific cake right now is a single concrete act, not an ongoing effort/habit goal; plain ケーキを食べてください fits better here.
   - It's rather for asking someone to keep something in mind going forward.

## Examples

- 最近(さいきん)運動(うんどう)が足(た)りないので、なるべくたくさん歩(ある)くようにしています。 - Recently, I haven't been getting enough exercise, so I try to walk as much as possible. | ongoing habitual effort — not the same as plain 歩いています, which would just state the action without emphasizing deliberate effort
- 登山(とざん)に行(い)くんだけど、日(ひ)が暮(く)れるまでに、帰(かえ)るようにするよ。心配(しんぱい)しないで。 - I'm going mountain climbing, but I'll make sure to be back before the sun sets. Don't worry. | casual register (よ, plain する) — a personal resolution, not a request directed at someone else
- 壁(かべ)を塗(ぬ)ったばかりだから、触(さわ)らないようにしてください。 - The wall was just painted, so please be careful not to touch it. | softer than 触らないでください — asks for care/mindfulness, not a blunt prohibition
- 先生(せんせい)は研究中(けんきゅうちゅう)ですので、邪魔(じゃま)をしないようにします。 - Since my teacher is in the middle of research, I'll make sure not to disturb them. | first-person resolution/effort, not a command to anyone else
- 遅刻(ちこく)しないようにしてください。 - Please make an effort not to be late. | soft caution, allows for the possibility of slipping — contrast with the next line
- 遅刻(ちこく)しないでください。 - Please don't be late. | direct, blunt prohibition — no effort-framing, expects strict compliance

## Notes

Comparison with related patterns:

| Pattern | Nuance | Don't confuse with |
|---|---|---|
| 〜ようにする | speaker's own deliberate, intentional effort/resolution ("I'll make sure to / I try to") | 〜ようになる — a change that happens as a result (gained ability, new habit), not an active decision. ようにする is agentive; ようになる is a resulting state. |
| 〜ようにしている | a standing, ongoing practice/habit maintained through effort | plain 〜ている — states an ongoing action/state with no implication of deliberate, maintained effort |
| 〜ようにしてください | polite request to be mindful/careful about a goal, softer, allows some slack | 〜てください (direct one-time request/instruction) and 〜ないでください (direct blunt prohibition) — both are stricter and don't carry the "make an effort" framing |

## Register

Register: neutral
ようにします itself carries no fixed politeness — register comes from the copula/verb that follows: する (casual) vs します (polite), してください (polite request) vs plain してくれ/して (casual). All examples above pattern this way.

## Often confused with

- [お/ご V「ます形」ください - Respectful request form (尊敬語)](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/o-go-v-masukudasai-respectful-request-form) — test: Asking for a specific one-time respectful action right now, or asking someone to keep making an effort toward an ongoing goal?
- [Vて形＋くださいませんか politer form of くれませんか - could you please (do smth for me)](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/kudasaimasenka-polite-request) — test: A direct one-time polite request, or asking someone to be mindful of an ongoing goal going forward?
- [V「dict」 こと ＋ に　＋ する - I decided (on my own will)](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/v-dict-koto-ni-suru-i-decided-on-my-own-will) — test: An ongoing effort/resolution kept up over time, or a firm one-time decision made once?

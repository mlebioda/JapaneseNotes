# 特別な動詞（とくべつなどうし）

> Special humble (謙譲語) verbs that replace the plain verb when speaking humbly about one's own actions

## Structure

| Plain (dictionary) form | Kenjougo (humble) form | Meaning                     |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------- | --------------------------- |
| 言う                      | 申(もう)す / 申し上げる         | to say                      |
| 聞く・尋(たず)ねる・訪(たず)ねる      | 伺(うかが)う                | to ask / to hear / to visit |
| 行く・来る                   | 参(まい)る                 | to go / to come             |
| いる                      | おる                     | to be                       |
| もらう                     | いただく                   | to receive                  |
| 見る                      | 拝見(はいけん)する             | to look / to see            |
| する                      | 致(いた)す                 | to do                       |
| 知る                      | 存(ぞん)じ上(あ)げる           | to know (someone)           |
| あげる・やる                  | 差(さ)し上(あ)げる            | to give                     |
| 会う                      | お目(め)にかかる              | to meet                     |
| 食(た)べる・飲(の)む            | いただく                   | to eat, drink               |

## Examples

昨日(きのう)、高橋(たかはし)先生(せんせい)にお目(め)にかかりました。 - I met Mr. Takahashi yesterday.
A: どうぞ、このケーキを召(め)し上(あ)がってください。 B: ありがとうございます。いただきます。 - A: Please, go ahead and have this cake.  B: Thank you. I will have some.
日曜日(にちようび)、午後(ごご)3時(じ)ごろ、社長(しゃちょう)のお宅(たく)にうかがってもよろしいでしょうか。 - May I visit your home around 3:00 p.m. on Sunday, Sir?
わたしはラジと申(もう)します。 - I am Raji. (literally "I am called Raji.")

## Notes

- 存(ぞん)じ上(あ)げる is the humble form of 知る only for **people** (knowing someone). For things and facts, use 存(ぞん)じる — typically 存じております in polite speech.

## Register
Register: polite-context typical
Used when speaking about your own actions to a superior, customer, or outsider — business settings, service industry, formal introductions. Pairs with polite ます endings; not used in casual speech.

## Often confused with
- [**2. 特別な動詞（とくべつなどうし）- special verbs**](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/tokubetsunadoushi-special-verbs) — test: Lowering your own action (→ kenjougo special verbs like 伺う／申す), or raising someone else's status (→ sonkeigo special verbs like いらっしゃる／おっしゃる)?
- [お・ご V『ます形」します](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/o-go-v-masushimasu) — test: Is there a special humble verb for this action (→ use it from the table), or none (→ fall back to お・ご V「ます形」します)?

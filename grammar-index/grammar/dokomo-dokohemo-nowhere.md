# どこも/どこへも - nowhere

> どこも/どこへも + negative verb = "nowhere" — どこへも emphasizes direction (not going anywhere), while どこも is used more generally for place.

## Structure

- どこ（へ）も + V negative

## Examples

- どこへも行(い)きません。 - I'm not going anywhere.
- いいえ、どこも行(い)きません。ホテルにいます。 - No, I'm not going anywhere. I'll be at the hotel.

## Register
Register: neutral
Works identically in plain and polite speech; only the verb ending changes formality.

## Often confused with

- [どこか - somewhere, anywhere](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/dokoka-somewhere-anywhere) — test: Is the sentence negative (→ どこも/どこへも), or affirmative (→ どこか)?

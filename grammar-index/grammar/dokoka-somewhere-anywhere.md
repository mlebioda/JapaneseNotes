# どこか - somewhere, anywhere

> どこか = "somewhere/anywhere" — an indefinite pronoun for places, used in affirmative statements/questions; its negative response uses どこへも.

## Structure

- どこか（へ） + V — affirmative/question
- Negative response: いいえ、どこへも + V negative

## Examples

- どこかへ行(い)きますか。 - Are you going somewhere?
- どこか（へ）旅行(りょこう)しますか。 - Will you go anywhere for a trip?
- 去年(きょねん)の冬(ふゆ)に、どこかへ行(い)きましたか。 - Did you go somewhere last winter?
- いいえ、どこへも行(い)きませんでした。 - No, I didn't go anywhere.
- 空港(くうこう)に行(い)く前(まえ)に、どこかへ行(い)きますか。 - Are you going somewhere before you go to the airport?

## Register
Register: neutral
Works identically in plain and polite speech; only the verb ending changes formality.

## Often confused with

- [どこも/どこへも - nowhere](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/dokomo-dokohemo-nowhere) — test: Is the sentence affirmative (→ どこか), or negative (→ どこも/どこへも)?

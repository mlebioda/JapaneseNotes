# で - where the action will take place

> Uses で to mark the location where an action takes place.

## Structure

N (Place) + で + V

## Examples

- 昨日、秋葉原で冷蔵庫を買った — Yesterday I bought a fridge in Akihabara
- 家で、朝ごはんを食べません — I don't eat breakfast at home
- 駅でざっしを買います — I'm going to buy a magazine at the station

## Notes

で (location of action) vs に (existence/destination):
- 公園で遊ぶ — play in the park (action happens there → で)
- 公園にいる — be in the park (existing at a location → に)

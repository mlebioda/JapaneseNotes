# で - where the action will take place

> Marks the place where an action happens.

## Structure

N(Place) ＋で＋V. Register: neutral — で itself carries no politeness; register comes from the verb.

## Examples

- 昨日、秋葉原で冷蔵庫を買った（あきはばら、れいぞうこ）- Yesterday, I bought a fridge in Akihabara
- 家で、朝ごはんを食べません - I don't eat breakfast at home
- 駅で ざっしを買います - I'm going to buy a magazine at the station

## Register

Register: neutral
で carries no politeness of its own; register comes from the verb.

## Often confused with

- [で - means or method](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/de-means-or-method) — test: Does で mark where the action physically takes place (で-location), or the tool/method used to perform it (で-means)?

# この/その/あの/どの

> Ko-so-a-do demonstrative adjectives placed before nouns to indicate the proximity of a thing to the speaker or listener.

## Structure

この / その / あの / どの + Noun

## Meaning

Demonstrative modifiers that must be followed by a noun:
- この — this [noun] (near speaker)
- その — that [noun] (near listener)
- あの — that [noun] (far from both)
- どの — which [noun]?

## Examples

- このかばん - this bag
- そのかばん - that bag (near you)
- あのかばん - that bag (over there)
- どのかばん - which bag?

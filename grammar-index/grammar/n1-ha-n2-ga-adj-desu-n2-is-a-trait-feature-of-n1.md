# N1 は N2 が ADJ です — N2 is a trait/feature of N1

> The double-subject (は/が) construction: N2, a trait or part of N1, is described by the adjective.

## Structure

- N1　は　N2 が ADJです (no N1のN2) - N1 is the topic/subject
- N1のN２は - N2 (of N1) is the subject

Negative / Past:
- 東京は道がせまくないです - Tokyo's streets are not narrow
- 東京は道がせまかったです - Tokyo's streets were narrow

N2 is a trait or part of N1, and the adjective describes that trait. English has no direct equivalent, which is why it trips learners up. Don't confuse it with a plain N が ADJ です sentence (single subject) — dropping the N1は clause changes what's being described. Neutral register, used in any context.

## Examples

- 東京は道がせまいです（みち）- Tokyo's streets are narrow
- 彼女(かのじょ)はかみが長(なが)いです - her hair is long
- この絵(え)は色(いろ)がきれいです - The picture's color is beautiful
- 彼(かれ)は背(せ)が高(たか)いです - He is tall
- にほんは漫画(まんが)が有名(ゆうめい)です - manga is popular in Japan

## Register
Register: neutral
Used across all speech styles and registers; formality is carried by the copula (plain だ／である vs polite です), not by the construction itself.

## Often confused with
- [は - marks a phrase (already carrying another particle) as a contrastive topic](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/ha-contrastive-topic-marker) — test: Does は mark N1 as topic while が still marks a second subject N2 in the same sentence (double-subject: N1は N2が ADJです), or does は stack onto/replace another particle entirely, with no second が-subject present?
- [が](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/ga-subject-marker) — test: Does が mark the sole subject of a simple N1が N2です sentence, or a second/embedded subject describing a trait of an already-topic-marked N1 (N1は N2が ADJです)?

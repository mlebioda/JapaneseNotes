# 全部で - in total

> Expresses a total count or amount — "in total" / "all together".

## Structure

全部（ぜんぶ）+ で + [quantity/statement]

## Meaning

全部 means "all" or "everything". Adding で marks the total as a sum or aggregate. Used to state how many there are altogether.

## Examples

- 全部で四人です（ぜんぶ、よにん）- There are 4 people in total.

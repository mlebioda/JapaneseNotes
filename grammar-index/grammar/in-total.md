# 全部で(ぜんぶ）in total

> Expresses a total count or amount — equivalent to "in total" or "altogether".

## Structure

全部（ぜんぶ）+ で + [number + counter] + です

## Examples

- 全部(ぜんぶ)で四人(よにん)です — There are four people in total.

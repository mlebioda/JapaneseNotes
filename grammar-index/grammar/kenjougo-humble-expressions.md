# 謙譲語（けんじょうご） - humble expressions

> Lowering the speaker's own action to indirectly show respect toward the listener/referent

## Notes

Categorized 謙譲語 vocabulary from this lesson:

Verbs
- 拝見(はいけん)する - to look / to view (humble); use when looking at something belonging to/from a superior — to watch, get acquainted with, glance at
- 伺(うかが)う - to hear / to ask / to visit (transitive); covers 聞く・尋ねる・訪問する — humbly receiving info or visiting someone above you

Pronouns
- わたくし - I; more formal/humble than 私, used in business or very formal speech

People or objects
- 弊社(へいしゃ) - our company (humble); used when referring to your own company to an outsider
- 粗品(そしな) - little gift (humble); downplays the value of what you're giving, standard business-gift phrasing

## Register
Register: polite-context typical
Kenjougo vocabulary lowers the speaker's own actions/status to indirectly show respect — used in business, service, and formal speech toward superiors and outsiders.

# interrogative + ても.... - no matter

> An interrogative word combined with ても forms "no matter what/who/where/when [verb happens]."

## Structure

Interrogatives used with this pattern:
- **何(なに/なん)** — what
- **誰(だれ)** — who
- **どこ** — where
- **いつ** — when
- **どんな** — what kind
- **なぜ/どうして** — why
- **どう/どのように** — how
- **どれ** — which (of many)
- **どちら** — which (of two) / where (polite)
- **いくつ** — how many
- **いくら** — how much (price)
- **どのくらい/どれぐらい** — how long / how much (extent)

- 何(なに) — no matter what / anything:
    - 何(なに)を言(い)われても気持(きも)ちは変(か)わらない。 - No matter what you say, my feelings won't change.
- 誰(だれ) — no matter who / anyone:
    - 誰(だれ)に聞(き)いても答(こた)えがわからない。 - No matter who I ask, nobody knows the answer.
- どこ — no matter where / anywhere:
    - どこに行(い)っても友達(ともだち)ができる。 - No matter where I go, I make friends.
- いつ — no matter when / anytime:
    - いつ電話(でんわ)しても出(で)ない。 - No matter when I call, he doesn't answer.
- どんな — no matter what kind / any kind:
    - どんな歌(うた)でも歌(うた)える。 - I can sing any song.
- どんなに + adjective/adverb + ても — no matter how [much/adjective]:
    - どんなに難(むずか)しくても諦(あきら)めない。 - No matter how hard it is, I won't give up.
- いくら — no matter how much:
    - いくら食(た)べても太(ふと)らない。 - No matter how much I eat, I don't gain weight.

## Examples

- 何(なに)を言(い)われても、わたしの気持(きも)ちは変(か)わりません。 - No matter what you say to me, my feelings won't change.
- 山田(やまだ)さんはどんな歌(うた)でも歌(うた)える。 - Mr. Yamada can sing any song.
- 何(なに)を言(い)われても気持(きも)ちは変(か)わらない。 - No matter what you say, my feelings won't change.
- 何(なに)でも食(た)べられる。 - I can eat anything.
- 誰(だれ)に聞(き)いても答(こた)えがわからない。 - No matter who I ask, nobody knows the answer.
- 誰(だれ)でも間違(まちが)える。 - Anyone can make mistakes.
- いつ電話(でんわ)しても出(で)ない。 - No matter when I call, he doesn't answer.
- いつでも来(き)てください。 - Please come anytime.
- どんな歌(うた)でも歌(うた)える。 - I can sing any song.
- どんなに難(むずか)しくても諦(あきら)めない。 - No matter how hard it is, I won't give up.
- いくら食(た)べても太(ふと)らない。 - No matter how much I eat, I don't gain weight.

## Notes

Not every interrogative above plugs directly into V+ても. なぜ/どうして (why) has no standard "no matter why" equivalent — this pattern doesn't cover it. どう/どのように (how) doesn't attach as plain どうても either; degree-based "no matter how [adjective/adverb]" instead uses どんなに + adjective/adverb + ても (see どんなに難しくても above) — a separate construction from bare どんな + でも ("any kind of ~").

Interrogative + でも alone (何でも, 誰でも, いつでも, どんな N でも) is a simpler pattern meaning "any/anything" — no verb clause attached. Interrogative + [verb]ても is the fuller concessive clause meaning "no matter if/when [verb happens]." Compare 何でも食べられる ("I can eat anything") with 何を言われても気持ちは変わらない ("no matter what you say, my feelings won't change").

## Register
Register: neutral
Used broadly across casual and polite speech — no inherent formality association.

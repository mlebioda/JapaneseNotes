# OR - か、か（２ options)

> か…か connects two alternatives meaning "A or B"; also used in embedded clauses to express uncertainty about which of two things is the case.

## Use Cases

Used with verbs, adjectives, adverbs, and nouns. Frequently appears with わかりません or 知らない to express "I don't know whether A or B."

## Structure

- ～か ～か

## Examples

- 今日は九月八日ですか、九日ですか（くがつ、ようか）— Is today September 8th or 9th?
- あそこは映画館ですか、美術館ですか（えいがかん、びじゅつかん）— Is that a movie theatre or an art museum?
- これはアンドさんのざっしですか、山田さんのざっしですか — Is this Mr. Ando's magazine or Mr. Yamada's?
- これは日本語か英語かわかりません（にほんご、えいご）— I don't know if this is in Japanese or English.
- この料理はおいしいかまずいかわかりません（りょうり）— I don't know if this dish is tasty or not.
- 行くか行かないか、まだ決めていません（い、い、き）— I haven't decided yet whether I'll go or not.
- 雨が降るか降らないか心配です（あめ、ふ、ふ、しんぱい）— I'm worried about whether it will rain or not.

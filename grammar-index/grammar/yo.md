# よ

> A sentence-final particle used to let the listener know information they didn't already know, or to emphasize it.

## Structure

Attaches to the end of a sentence (after です/plain forms).

## Examples

- この店(みせ)はとても有名(ゆうめい)ですよ - this restaurant is very famous, you know
- わたしの家(いえ)から駅(えき)までは遠(とお)いですよ - my house is far from the station, you know
- これはあなたのかさですよ - this is your umbrella, you know

## Use Cases

- Letting the listener know information
- Emphasizing information
- Information not known to the listener
- Caution: can sound assertive/pushy if overused with strangers or people of higher status — use sparingly outside close relationships

## Notes

Difference between よ and ね (ね has no standalone grammar file yet):
- 今日はあついですね - today is hot, isn't it? (ね seeks agreement/confirmation on shared knowledge)
- 明日はあついですよ - tomorrow will be hot, you know (よ presents new information to the listener)

## Register
Register: neutral
Used across all politeness levels — よ itself carries no formality tier, though overuse toward strangers or superiors can read as pushy (see Use Cases above).

## Often confused with

- [ね - seeking approval](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/ne-seeking-approval) — test: Does the sentence seek agreement/confirm shared knowledge (→ ね), or present new information to the listener (→ よ)?

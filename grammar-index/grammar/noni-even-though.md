# 。。。のに - even though

> Even though ~ — connects two clauses expressing a mismatch between expectation and reality, often with regret or surprise.

## Structure

### Verb
- V-plain + のに → 一生懸命(いっしょうけんめい)練習(れんしゅう)したのに、負(ま)けてしまった。(Even though we practiced hard, we lost.)

### い-adjective
- Present: Adj + のに → このパンは安(やす)いのに、大(おお)きいです。(Even though this bread is cheap, it's big.)
- Past: Adj-くなかった + のに → 安(やす)くなかったのに (even though it wasn't cheap)

### な-adjective
- Present: Adj + な + のに → 旅行(りょこう)が好(す)きなのに、お金(かね)がなくて…(Even though I like traveling...)
- Past: Adj + だった + のに → 好(す)きだったのに (even though (I) liked it)

### Noun
- N + な + のに → ご飯(はん)を食(た)べたばかりなのに、またお腹(なか)が空(す)いた。(Even though I just ate, I'm hungry again — ばかり behaves grammatically as a noun here)

## Examples

- 試合(しあい)のために一生懸命(いっしょうけんめい)練習(れんしゅう)したのに、負(ま)けてしまった。 - Even though we practiced very hard for the game, we lost. (feel regret)
- このパンは安(やす)いのに、大(おお)きいです。 - Even though this bread is cheap, it is big.
- 旅行(りょこう)が好(す)きなのに、お金(かね)がなくて、どこへもいけません。 - Even though I like traveling, I don't have money, so I can't go anywhere.
- ご飯(はん)を食(た)べたばかりなのに、またお腹(なか)が空(す)いた。 - Even though I just ate some food, I feel hungry again.

## Use Cases

Creates a complex sentence between conflicting contexts, revealing the speaker's dissatisfaction, sadness, or surprise. Cannot be followed by a question, command, request, suggestion, or volitional (let's) in the second clause — のに requires both clauses to be factual/real.

## Register

Register: neutral
- Used in both casual and polite speech — the surrounding grammar can be made formal — but the regret/complaint/surprise nuance persists regardless of register, so it can sound blunt or accusatory toward superiors unless softened by the rest of the sentence.

## Often confused with

- [ために - for (purpose of)](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/tameni-for-purpose-of) — test: does the clause express an unexpected contrast between two facts (のに) or a purpose/goal being pursued (ために)?

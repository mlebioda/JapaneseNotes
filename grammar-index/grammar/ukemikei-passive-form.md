# 受身形（うけみけい）- passive form

> The passive form describes an action received or suffered by the subject, formed by conjugating the verb to its passive/receptive form.

## Structure

**Group 1 (G1) — う verbs** Change the last kana to its あ-row equivalent + れる
- う → われる
    - 言う(いう) → 言われる(いわれる) — to say → to be said
- く → かれる
    - 書く(かく) → 書かれる(かかれる) — to write → to be written
- ぐ → がれる
    - 脱ぐ(ぬぐ) → 脱がれる(ぬがれる) — to take off → to be taken off
- す → される
    - 話す(はなす) → 話される(はなされる) — to speak → to be spoken / talked about
- つ → たれる
    - 打つ(うつ) → 打たれる(うたれる) — to hit → to be hit
- ぬ → なれる
    - 死ぬ(しぬ) → 死なれる(しなれる) — to die → (to suffer someone's death)
- ぶ → ばれる
    - 呼ぶ(よぶ) → 呼ばれる(よばれる) — to call → to be called
- む → まれる
    - 飲む(のむ) → 飲まれる(のまれる) — to drink → to be drunk
- る → られる
    - 取る(とる) → 取られる(とられる) — to take → to be taken

**Group 2 (G2) — る verbs** Drop る, add られる
- る → られる
    - 食べる(たべる) → 食べられる(たべられる) — to eat → to be eaten
    - 褒める(ほめる) → 褒められる(ほめられる) — to praise → to be praised

**Group 3 (G3) — irregular**
- する → される — to do → to be done
- くる → こられる — to come → (to be inconvenienced by someone's arrival)

## Examples

- 先生（せんせい）は学生（がくせい）を褒（ほ）める - The teacher praises students
- 学生（がくせい）は先生（せんせい）に褒め**られる**（ほ） - Students are praised by the teacher

## Use Cases

**迷惑(めいわく)の受身(うけみ) (suffering/indirect passive):** passivizing an intransitive verb (死(し)なれる, 来(こ)られる) implies the speaker was negatively affected by the event, even though there's no direct object "receiving" the action — e.g. 母(はは)に死(し)なれた ("my mother's death affected me badly"), 友達(ともだち)に来(こ)られた ("a friend came, to my inconvenience"). The same nuance extends to transitive verbs in the N1は N2に N3を V「passive」pattern (足(あし)をふまれた, ケーキを食(た)べられた) — the point isn't just what happened, but that it happened *to the speaker's detriment*.

## Register
Register: neutral
The passive conjugation itself carries no inherent politeness value — it combines freely with plain or ます-form endings and appears in casual conversation, business speech, and formal writing alike.

## Often confused with
- [1. **動詞(どうし)の尊敬形(そんけいけい) - respectful forms of verbs**](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/sonkeikei-passive-form) — test: Is the subject receiving the action from someone else (passive), or is the subject a superior whose own action is being described respectfully using the same られる shape (honorific)?

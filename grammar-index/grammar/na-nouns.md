# な形容詞 + Nouns

> A な-adjective modifies a following noun by inserting な between them.

## Structure

- な形容詞 + な + Noun
	- しずかな夜

## Examples

- しずかな夜 - quiet night

## Register
Register: neutral
This attachment rule (な-adjective + な + Noun) applies identically in casual and polite speech; formality is carried by the surrounding sentence, not by this construction.

## Often confused with

- [2. なadj + Noun](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/naadj-noun) — test: Is the な-adjective directly modifying a following noun (→ add な), or ending the sentence as a predicate before です/か (→ no な)?

# Interrogative V conditional form + 　いいですか - ask for opinion or advice

> Asks for advice or an opinion on what to do — an interrogative word + a verb's ば-conditional form + いいですか ("is it fine if...?").

## Structure

- Interrogative V conditional form「ば」+ いいですか - ask for opinion or advice

## Examples

- この書類(しょるい)に何(なに)を書(か)けばいいですか。 - What should I write in this document?
- コンサートのチケットを買(か)いたいんですが、どこで買(か)えばいいですか。 - I want to buy a concert ticket, but where should I buy it?
- どうしたらいいですか。 - What should I do?

**Casual (いいの？ instead of いいですか):**
- 明日(あした)パーティーに参加(さんか)するんだけど、どんな服(ふく)を着(き)ればいいの？ - I'm attending a party tomorrow, so what kind of clothes should I wear?

## Notes

**「たら」+ いいですか** — an equally common alternative to ば, often more natural in spoken Japanese (e.g. どうしたらいいですか). The only difference from ば here is naturalness/frequency — both are neutral conditionals that work fine with questions and advice-seeking.

**なら + いいですか** — なら reacts to an already-stated or assumed premise (行くなら, 買うなら); an open interrogative like 何/どう has no premise to react to, so this combination doesn't occur naturally.

## Register

Register: tiered (polite / casual)
いいですか is the neutral-polite form suitable for most conversations; the casual equivalent drops です for いいの (rising intonation, informal). Both keep the ば-conditional unchanged — only the final question tag shifts register.

## Often confused with

- [。。。なら。。。- if](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/nara-if) — test: can なら replace ば here for an open interrogative "what should I do" question (no — なら needs an already-assumed premise to react to), or must ば/たら be used?

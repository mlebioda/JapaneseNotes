# Years 年

> How to say a given year, ask what year it is, and count a duration in years.

## 1. Stating the year

### Structure

Number + 年（ねん）

| Number | Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 1999年 | せんきゅうひゃくきゅうじゅうきゅうねん | 1999 |
| 4年 | よねん | 4 years / year 4 (irregular — not よんねん) |
| 何年 | なんねん | what year? |

### Examples

- 今年は何年ですか（ことし、なんねん）- What year is it?
- 来年は何年ですか（らいねん、なんねん）- What year will next year be?

## 2. Counting a duration in years

### Structure

Number + 年（間）

| Number | Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 四年(間) | よねんかん | 4 years (period) |

### Examples

## Use Cases

です above (in "what year is it?") is the polite (teineigo) form. In casual/informal speech です is dropped: 今年何年？, 来年何年？

## Register
Register: tiered (casual, polite)
今年は何年ですか is the polite (teineigo) form, appropriate with teachers, strangers, or in formal writing. Dropping です for 今年何年？ is casual, used with friends or family. The counting-duration sense (四年間) attaches the same way regardless of register.

# Vない＋なければなりません- I have to do

> Vない + なければなりません — expresses obligation: "if you don't do V, it's not acceptable" = "you have to do V."

## Structure

Vない＋なければなりません
- ければ - "if" (conditional)
- Vない + ければ - "if I don't do V"
- なりません - negative of なる ("to become"); literally "it won't do / it's not acceptable [if you don't do V]"
→ combined: "if you don't do V, it won't do" = "you have to do V"

Register: neutral-to-formal, safe in speech and writing. In casual spoken Japanese, なきゃ(いけない) is a very common contraction with the same meaning (e.g. 行かなきゃ = 行かなければならない) — expect to hear it constantly even though this lesson drills the formal form.

Conjugation examples:
- 習う（なら）→ 習わなければなりません - I have to study (from someone, practice)
- 乗る（の）→ 乗らなければなりません - I need to ride
- 返す（かえ) → 返さなければなりません - I need to return (something)

Negative of this pattern (don't have to do V): Vなくてもいいです
- 習う（なら）→ 習わなくてもいいです - you don't have to study

## Examples

- 勉強しなければなりません - I have to study
- 習(なら)わなければなりません - I have to study (from someone, practice)
- 乗(の)らなければなりません - I need to ride
- 返(かえ)さなければなりません - I need to return (something)
- 明日朝６時に起きなければなりません（あしたあさ、お) - I have to get up at 6 tomorrow morning
- 金曜日(きんようび)までにDVDを返(かえ)さなければなりません - I have to return the DVD by Friday
- 習(なら)わなくてもいいです - you don't have to study

## Register
Register: tiered (neutral-to-formal: なければなりません; casual: なきゃ(いけない))
なければなりません is safe in both speech and writing across formal and neutral settings; なきゃ(いけない) is a very common casual contraction heard constantly in everyday spoken Japanese but avoided in formal writing.

## Often confused with
- [。。。はずです - should do/ought (logically)](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/hazudesu-should-do-ought-logically) — test: Is this a rule/obligation the subject must follow (→ なければなりません), or the speaker's confident logical expectation about a fact (→ はずです)?
- [V「て形」はいけません - is forbidden to do something](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/v-tewa-ikemasen-forbidden) — test: Is the subject obligated to do something (→ なければなりません), or forbidden from doing something (→ てはいけません)?

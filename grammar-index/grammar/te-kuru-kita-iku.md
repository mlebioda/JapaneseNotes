# V「て-form」くる / きた / いく — action continuing over time

> Marks an action or change building up continuously over time — てきた (built up to now), てくる (a change emerging as it nears now, or the literal "go and come back"), or ていく (will keep building from now into the future).

## Structure

1. **てきた** — from past up to now (the arrow goes → present)
2. **てくる** — a change becoming noticeable as it approaches now (still past → present, not future); also the literal "go do X and come back" (行ってきます)
3. **ていく** — this is the form that expresses future continuation — from now onward (present → future). Not てくる.

```
past ────────────────●──────────────→ future
                  present
勉強してきた →●  (studied and arrived at now)
                  ●→ 勉強していく  (will keep studying from now)
```

## Examples

- 日本語(にほんご)を勉強(べんきょう)してきた - I have been studying Japanese (up to now).
- 日本語を勉強する人(ひと)が増(ふ)えてきた - The number of people studying Japanese has been increasing.
- これから日本語(にほんご)を勉強(べんきょう)していきます - I will keep studying Japanese from now on.
- 雨(あめ)が降(ふ)ってきた - It has started raining.
- コンビニで飲(の)み物(もの)を買(か)ってきます - I'll go buy a drink (and come back).

## Use Cases

Sentence-meaning reference:

| Sentence                 | Meaning                                                     |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| 日本語(にほんご)を勉強(べんきょう)してきた。 | I have been studying Japanese (up to now).                  |
| 日本語を勉強する人(ひと)が増(ふ)えてきた。  | The number of people studying Japanese has been increasing. |
| これから日本語(にほんご)を勉強(べんきょう)していきます。 | I will keep studying Japanese from now on.                  |
| 雨(あめ)が降(ふ)ってきた。 | It has started raining. (non-literal てくる — change emerging now) |
| コンビニで飲(の)み物(もの)を買(か)ってきます。 | I'll go buy a drink (and come back). (literal てくる — round trip) |

This is **not** physical movement — くる/きた/いく here mark the time direction of an accumulating change, not literal coming/going. Compare:
- ている — describes a current state or ongoing action (snapshot)
- てきた — the action/change built up *over time*, leading to now (past → present)
- ていく — the action/change will keep building up *from now onward* (present → future)
- てくる (non-literal use) — a change becoming apparent as it nears now, very close to てきた in meaning; the everyday literal use is a round trip toward "here" (行ってきます — go and come back). てくる does **not** express future continuation — that's ていく's job.

## Register

Register: neutral
The てきた／てくる／ていく aspectual markers are register-neutral; politeness is carried entirely by the verb's ます／plain conjugation (compare していきます, polite, vs していく, plain) rather than by the construction itself.

## Often confused with

- [1. V「dict」ところです - about to start](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/tokoro-desu-dict-form) — test: is the action about to begin as a single imminent moment (ところです), or will it keep continuing/building from now onward (ていく)?
- [3. V「た形」ところです - action has just finished](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/tokoro-desu-ta-form) — test: did the action just finish this instant as a single completion point (たところです), or has it been gradually building up to reach this point (てきた)?

# Vて形＋くださいませんか politer form of くれませんか - could you please (do smth for me)

> A more polite version of くれませんか for asking someone to do something for you

## Structure

- て形 ＋ くださいませんか - could you please
    - ケーキの作(つく)り方を教(おし)えてくださいませんか - Could you please teach me how to bake a cake?
- て形＋いただけませんか - could you please (humble-request variant)

## Examples

展覧会(てんらんかい)の準備(じゅんび)を手伝(てつだ)ってくださいませんか - Could you please help me prepare for the exhibition?
おいしい お店(みせ)を紹介(しょうかい)してくださいませんか - Could you please introduce me to some delicious stores?
お金(かね)を少(すこ)し貸(か)してくださいませんか - Would you lend me some money?

## Register
Register: polite-context typical
More polite/indirect than くれませんか — appropriate toward superiors, customers, and in service or business contexts.

## Often confused with
- [お/ご V「ます形」ください - Respectful request form (尊敬語)](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/o-go-v-masukudasai-respectful-request-form) — test: General verb, everyday-to-moderately-formal request (→ Vて形＋くださいませんか), or Group1/2/3 ます-stem in a formal/service-industry/signage-style request (→ お/ご V「ます形」ください)?
- [Vて形 + いただく（謙譲語）・くださる（尊敬語） - describing a favor received from a superior](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/te-form-itadaku-kudasaru-favor-received) — test: Asking someone to do something for you (→ くださいませんか request), or describing a favor already received/given (→ いただく／くださる statement)?
- [ようにします - working hard on / making an effort to](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/you-ni-shimasu) — test: A direct one-time polite request, or asking someone to keep an ongoing goal in mind?
- [Would you please let me do...](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/would-you-please-let-me-do) — test: Asking the other person to do something for you (plain て形＋くださいませんか), or asking for permission to do something yourself (causative＋て形＋ください／もらえませんか／いただけませんか, "let me do X")?
- [V「て」もらえませんか, V「て」くれませんか](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/v-te-moraemasenka-v-te-kuremasenka) — test: Is this the politer くださいませんか escalation for superiors/customers, or the neutral-polite もらえませんか／くれませんか used in everyday requests?
- [Vて いただけませんか - polite requests. Politer than てください](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/itadakemasenka-polite-request) — test: Is this a respectful request elevating the listener (→ てくださいませんか), or a humble request lowering the speaker (→ ていただけませんか)?

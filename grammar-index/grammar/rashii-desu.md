# 。。。らしいです - it seems

> Reports something you heard from others, or describes an impression that fits the typical image of something, used when the information is less certain than そうです.

## Structure

### Verb
- Plain: V「plain」+ らしいです → 高校(こうこう)からあの二人(ふたり)は付(つ)き合(あ)っている**らしいです**よ。 - It seems that those two people have been going out since high school.
- Past: V「た」+ らしいです → (no dedicated example in source)

### い-adjective
- い-adj + らしいです → (no dedicated example in source)

### な-adjective
- な-adj + らしいです → 友達(ともだち)から聞(き)いたんですが、富士山(ふじさん)に登(のぼ)るのは大変(たいへん)だった**らしいです**。 - I heard from my friend that climbing Mt. Fuji was really difficult.

### Noun
- Noun + らしいです → 彼(かれ)は学生(がくせい)**らしいです** - I heard he's a student.

## Examples

- 友達(ともだち)から聞(き)いたんですが、富士山(ふじさん)に登(のぼ)るのは大変(たいへん)だった**らしいです**。 - I heard from my friend that climbing Mt. Fuji was really difficult.
- 高校(こうこう)からあの二人(ふたり)は付(つ)き合(あ)っている**らしいです**よ。 - It seems that those two people have been going out since high school.
- その話(はなし)は本当(ほんとう)**らしいね**。 - The story seems to be true.
- 彼(かれ)は学生(がくせい)**らしいです** - I heard he's a student.
- ここはアメリカらしいです - Here feels like America

## Use Cases

Two related uses: (1) reporting hearsay, similar to そうです but used when the information is less certain; (2) describing a typical/stereotypical impression with Noun + らしいです — ここはアメリカらしいです (Here feels like America). らしいです is the polite form; dropping です gives the plain/casual equivalent らしい (see その話は本当らしいね above).

## Register

Register: tiered (polite: らしいです; plain/casual: らしい)
らしいです is used in polite conversation; dropping です to plain らしい is the casual register, used with friends/family or in casual narration.

## Often confused with

- [V「plain」そうです / そうだ - It is said that / I heard](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/sou-desu-hearsay) — test: Is the speaker fairly confident in this reported information (そうです), or treating it as less certain (らしいです)?
- [...ようです](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/you-desu) — test: Is this based on hearsay or a typical/stereotypical impression (らしいです), or the speaker's own direct inference from evidence (ようです)?
- [言っていました - relay ideas](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/tteimashita-relay-ideas) — test: Is this hearsay/impression reported without naming a specific source, and with less certainty (らしいです), or attributed to a specific person who said it (言っていました)?

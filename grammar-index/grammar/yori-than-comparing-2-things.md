# より than - comparing 2 things

> Compare two things or people: N1 is more Adj than N2.

## Structure

- N1 は N2 より Adj です — N1 is more Adj than N2

## Examples

- 山田さんはすずきさんより背が高いです（せ、たか) - yamada is taller than suzuki
- 今日は昨日より寒いです（きょう、きのう、さむ) - Today is colder than yesterday
- このパンはそのパンより美味(おい)しいです - this bread is more delicious than that bread
- 黒いくつは青いくつよりきれいです（くろ、あお) - the black shoes are more beautiful than the blue ones

## Use Cases

より itself has no politeness of its own — the register comes from the adjective/copula ending (plain だ vs polite です), same as in any other sentence: 今日は昨日より寒い (plain/casual) vs 今日は昨日より寒いです (polite) — same particle, either register.

## Register

Register: neutral
より carries no politeness of its own — formality comes from the adjective/copula ending that follows (plain だ vs です).

## Often confused with

- [comparsion どちら・ の方が (のほうが)](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/comparsion-dochira-nohouga) — test: Are you directly stating the comparison (N1はN2よりAdjです), or asking/answering which of two things is more Adj (どちらが／のほうが)?

# Vて形 + くれます - Someone does something to me

> Someone does an action for the speaker (or someone close to the speaker), as a favor.

## Structure

- Aは + Bに + Vて形 + くれます - A does V for B
  - A - giver
  - B - receiver
    - You or someone close to you
    - often omitted

## Examples

- ラジさんは私の引っ越しを手伝ってくれます（ひっこ、てつだ）- Raj will help me with the moving
- 安藤さんは日本語を教えてくれました。(あんどう・にほんご・おし) - Miss Ando taught me Japanese.
- 母は私の部屋のごみを捨ててくれました。(はは・わたし・へや・す) - My mom threw out the trash in my room for me.
- 手伝(てつだ)ってくれて、ありがとうございました。 - Thank you for helping me.

## Register
Register: tiered (plain: くれる; respectful: くださる)
くれる is neutral/plain. くださる is the sonkeigo (respectful) counterpart used when the giver (A) is a social superior.

## Often confused with
- [Aは + Bに + Vて形 + あげます - I do something for someone](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/vte-agemasu) — test: Does someone act toward the speaker/someone close (→ くれます), or does the giver act toward someone else (→ あげます)?
- [Vて形 + もらいます - somebody helped you with something that you asked to do](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/vte-moraimasu) — test: Is the giver the grammatical subject, unprompted (→ くれます), or is the receiver the subject, having asked for the favor (→ もらいます)?
- [Vて形 + いただく（謙譲語）・くださる（尊敬語） - describing a favor received from a superior](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/te-form-itadaku-kudasaru-favor-received) — test: Is this the plain-register くれる statement, or the sonkeigo くださる statement used for a superior?

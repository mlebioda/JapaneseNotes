# 。。。ても。。。- even if, even though

> A concedes a situation while B holds true regardless — "even if / even though A, B."

## Structure

### Verb
- Negative: V-ない + くても → まだ時間（じかん）があるから、急（いそ）がな**くても** 電車（でんしゃ）に間に合います（まにあいます）- Because we still have time left, we can catch the train **even if** we are not in a hurry

### い-adjective
- Present: Adj + くても → 寒（さむ）**くても**泳（およ）ぎます - I will swim even if it is cold

### な-adjective
- Present: Adj + でも → いや**でも**、宿題（しゅくだい）をやらなくちゃ - I still have to do homework **even though** I hate it

### Noun
- Present: N + でも → **たとえ**雨（あめ）**でも** 自転車（じてんしゃ）で学校（がっこう）に行きます（い）- I go to school by bicycle **even if** it rains

## Examples

- まだ時間（じかん）があるから、急（いそ）がなく**ても** 電車（でんしゃ）に間に合います（まにあいます）- Because we still have time left, we can catch the train **even if** we are not in a hurry
- 寒（さむ）**くても**泳（およ）ぎます - I will swim even if it is cold
- いや**でも**、宿題（しゅくだい）をやらなくちゃ - I still have to do homework **even though** I hate it
- **たとえ**雨（あめ）**でも** 自転車（じてんしゃ）で学校（がっこう）に行きます（い）- **Even if** it rains, I go to school by bicycle (emphasize no matter what)

## Use Cases

たとえ...ても pairs たとえ with ても for extra emphasis — "even if [X], no matter what."

- A ても B - B happens regardless of the situation of A. Often used together with たとえ or もし for extra emphasis.

## Register
Register: neutral
Used broadly across casual and polite speech — the concessive meaning itself carries no formality weight.

## Often confused with
- [。。。のに - even though](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/noni-even-though) — test: Does B hold true regardless of a hypothetical or general A (ても), or is B a factual complaint about an A that actually happened (のに)?

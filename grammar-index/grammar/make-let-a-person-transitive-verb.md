# Make/let a person transitive verb

> Making or letting a person do a transitive action to something.

## Structure

### Verb
- N0 は/が N1 に N2 を Vt「causative」→ 先生(せんせい)は生徒(せいと)に小説(しょうせつ)を読(よ)ませた。(The teacher made the students read a novel.)

**Roles:** N0 - causer / N1 - person who acts / N2 - thing / V - transitive

## Examples

- 子供(こども)の頃(ころ)、母(はは)は私(わたし)にピアノを習(なら)わせました。 - My mom made me learn piano when I was a child.
- 後輩(こうはい)に学園祭(がくえんさい)の準備(じゅんび)を手伝(てつだ)わせます。 - I will let my junior help me with the school festival preparations.
- 会議(かいぎ)で、部長(ぶちょう)はみなさんに自由(じゆう)に意見(いけん)を言(い)わせました。 - In the meeting, the manager let everybody say their opinions freely.

## Register

Register: tiered
- Plain causative — top-down only; used when an authority figure (parent, teacher, boss) causes or allows a subordinate to do something to an object; rude toward superiors, not used in that direction.

## Often confused with

- [Make/let a person intransitive verb](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/make-let-a-person-intransitive-verb) — test: Is there a separate object the causee acts upon (transitive verb → causee marked に, object marked を), or does the causee just act with no object (intransitive verb → causee marked を/に directly)?

# 本（ほん）- long items

> How to count long, thin, cylindrical items using 本, one through ten.

## Structure

Number + 本（ほん／ぼん／ぽん）

- １本（いっぽん）- one (long cylindrical object)
- ２本（にほん）- two (long cylindrical objects)
- ３本（さんぼん）- three (long cylindrical objects)
- ４本（よんほん）- four (long cylindrical objects)
- ５本（ごほん）- five (long cylindrical objects)
- ６本（ろっぽん）- six (long cylindrical objects)
- ７本（ななほん）- seven (long cylindrical objects)
- ８本（はっぽん）- eight (long cylindrical objects)
- ９本（きゅうほん）- nine (long cylindrical objects)
- １０本（じゅっぽん）- ten (long cylindrical objects)
- 何本（なんぼん）- how many (long cylindrical objects)

## Examples

- 水（みず）を３本（さんぼん）買（か）いました。- I bought three bottles of water.

## Register

Register: neutral
Counter readings themselves don't carry politeness — they attach identically in polite and plain sentences; only the surrounding verb/copula changes with register.

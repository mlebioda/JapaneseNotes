# へ / に - direction of movement, travel (destination)

> Marks the destination of movement/travel — へ can be replaced with に in this function.

## Structure

- Place + へ／に + V → 会社へ戻(もど)ります (i go back to the company)

## Examples

- 会社へ戻(もど)ります - i go back to the company
- 大学(だいがく)に行(い)きます - I go to university

## Use Cases

Travel destination, reaching point; へ can be replaced with に in this function.

## Register

Register: neutral

へ and に carry no politeness of their own — register comes from the verb ending.

## Often confused with

- [Go Somewhere for a certain objective](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/go-somewhere-for-a-certain-objective) — test: is に/へ marking a purpose clause attached right before 行く／来る／帰る (V-stem/N + に + 行きます), or simply marking the plain destination of a motion verb (Place + へ／に + V)?

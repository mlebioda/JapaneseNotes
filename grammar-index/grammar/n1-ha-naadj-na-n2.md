# 4. N1は + なadj + な N2

> N1 is described as an N2 that has the quality of a な-adjective — the adjective modifies N2 directly, and the whole phrase becomes the predicate of N1.

## Structure

- なadj + な + Noun
	- 山田さんは静か **な** 人です（しずか） - Mr. Yamada is a quiet person

## Examples

- 山田さんは静か **な** 人です（しずか） - Mr. Yamada is a quiet person
- あれは丈夫(じょうぶ)な建物(たてもの)です - that is a solid building

## Register
Register: neutral
The な attachment before N2 is register-neutral machinery — it applies the same whether the sentence ends in です (polite) or だ (plain).

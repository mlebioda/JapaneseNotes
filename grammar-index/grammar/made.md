# まで

> まで marks a point up to which an action or state continues (duration/extent, in time or space), contrasted with までに, a deadline.

## Structure

まで marks a point up to which an action or state continues (duration/extent), contrasted with までに, which marks a deadline for a one-time completed action:
- までに = deadline — a one-time action completed no later than N (could happen earlier)
- まで = duration/extent — the action continues right up until N

With a place instead of a time, まで marks the spatial endpoint an action/movement extends up to:
- N(place) + まで + V

## Examples

- 土曜日までに戻ります - I'll be back by Saturday (possibly sooner)
- 土曜日まで働きます - I'll work through/until Saturday
- 駅(えき)まで歩(ある)きます - I'll walk as far as the station

## Register
Register: neutral
まで and までに are neutral particles usable in any speech style, from casual conversation to formal writing.

## Often confused with
- [間（あいだ）- between, during, among](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/aida-between-during-among) — test: Does the action/state continue right up until the endpoint (まで), or does something specific happen at just one point within a bounded span (間に)?
- [を 過ぎる（す）- to pass somewhere and further go ahead/ time](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/wo-sugiru-to-pass-time) — test: Does the action stop at/extend up to the given point (まで), or does it cross past that point and continue beyond (を過ぎる)?

# V「dict」 こと ＋ に　＋ する - I decided (on my own will)

> Expresses a firm, one-time decision made by the speaker's own will.

## Structure

### Verb
- Present: V-plain + ことにする → 大学院（だいがくいん）へ行く（い）ことにしました - I decided to go to graduate school
- Negative: V-ない + ことにする → スキーに行(い)かないことにしました。 - I decided not to go skiing.

## Examples

- 卒業（そつぎょう）したら 大学院（だいがくいん）へ行く（い）こ**とにしました** - When I graduate (undergraduate), **I have decided** to go to graduate school
- わたしは卒業(そつぎょう)したら、帰国(きこく)する**ことにしました**。 - I have **decided** to go back to my home country after graduation.
- 風邪(かぜ)がひどいので、スキーに行(い)かない**ことにしました**。 - Because I caught a bad cold, I **decided** not to go skiing.
- 仕事後(しごとご)に、必ず(かならず)2キロ歩(ある)くこと**にしました**。 - I **decided** to make sure to walk 2 kilometers after work.

## Use Cases

にする = I decided; になる = it was decided (by something outside me); になっている = it is already set (rule/schedule/arrangement).

## Notes

A common extension is ことにしている, which describes an established habit or routine rather than a single decision — 毎朝(まいあさ)走(はし)ることにしている - "I make it a habit to run every morning."

## Register
Register: neutral
Used across casual and formal speech alike — politeness comes from the sentence-final copula (する／します), not from the ことにする construction itself.

## Often confused with

- [ようにします - working hard on / making an effort to](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/you-ni-shimasu) — test: Are you stating a firm, one-time decision, or an ongoing effort/resolution you keep making?
- [V「dict」 こと ＋ に + なります - It was decided](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/v-dict-koto-ni-narimasu-it-was-decided) — test: Did the speaker decide this themselves, or was it decided by someone/something else?
- [V「dict」 こと ＋ に + なっています - It is supposed to](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/v-dict-koto-ni-natteimasu-it-is-supposed-to) — test: Is this a firm decision the speaker just made, or an already-established rule or arrangement?
- [Volitional form + と思っています - I'm thinking of](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/volitional-form-totteimasu-im-thinking-of) — test: Is this an ongoing consideration, or a firm one-time decision the speaker has just made? (と思っています = considering; ことにする = firm decision)
- [V「plain」つもりです - conviction of doing something planned by speaker](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/vplaintsumoridesu-conviction-of-doing-something-planned-by-speaker) — test: Is this an ongoing personal resolution/plan (つもりです), or a firm, one-time decision the speaker has just made (ことにする)?
- [た形 + ことがあります - have you ever](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/ta-kotogaarimasu-have-you-ever) — test: does this announce a one-time decision you made (辞書形＋ことにする), or report whether you've ever done something (た形＋ことがあります)?
- [行ったことがありました - I once did ~ (recalling a single past experience)](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/ttakotogaarimashita-i-once-did-recalling-a-single-past-experience) — test: does this announce a decision the speaker made at that time (辞書形＋ことにする), or recall a specific past experience (た形＋ことがありました)?
- [N＋に＋します - speaker says what he/she decided on](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/n-ni-shimasu-decision) — test: Is the decision about which verb/action to take (ことにする), or which noun/option to choose (にする)?

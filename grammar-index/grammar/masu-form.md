# ます形 - masu form

> The polite, neutral-formal conjugated form of a verb — used with strangers, colleagues, and in most everyday situations.

## Structure

- Present affirmative: V-stem + ます → 食べます
- Present negative: V-stem + ません → 食べません
- Past affirmative: V-stem + ました → 食べました
- Past negative: V-stem + ませんでした → 食べませんでした

## Examples

- 食べます - (I) eat / will eat
- 食べません - (I) do not eat / will not eat
- 食べました - (I) ate
- 食べませんでした - (I) did not eat

## Notes

Often confused with 辞書形 — test: are you speaking casually with friends/family (辞書形), or in a neutral-polite context like strangers, colleagues, or most everyday situations (ます形)? The two are not interchangeable mid-register without deliberate code-switching.

## Register

Register: polite-context typical

ます形 (teineigo) is the neutral-polite baseline — appropriate with strangers, colleagues, and in most everyday situations, without the status-based framing of sonkeigo／謙譲語.

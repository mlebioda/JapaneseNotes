# て形 - several events happening back to back - order

> Chains several verbs together to describe events happening one after another, in order.

## Structure

### Verb
- V1 て形, V2 て形, ... Vn (final form) → 昼(ひる)ご飯(はん)を食(た)べて、映画(えいが)を見(み)て、カラオケに行(い)きました (ate lunch, watched a movie, and went to karaoke)

Only the last verb in the chain carries the tense/politeness marking (ます／た／plain); every earlier verb stays in plain て形 regardless of the sentence's overall tense.

## Examples

- お菓子を持って行きます（おかし、も）- I will bring some sweets
- 毎日九時に会社に行って、１２じに昼ごはんを食べて午後六時に家へ戻って（まいにち、ひる、ごご、いえ、もど）- Every day I go to the company at 9 AM, eat lunch at 12 and go back home at 6 PM
- 土曜日に友達といっしょに昼ご飯を食べて、映画を見て、カラオケに行きました（どようび、ともだち、えいが、み）- I ate lunch with my friend, watched a movie, and went to karaoke

## Notes

て形 is not only for sequencing: it is also used to give a reason/cause (e.g. 熱があって、休みました — "I had a fever, so I rested"), which learners easily conflate with the "and then" sequencing use taught here.

## Register

Register: neutral
Not politeness-restricted itself — works the same way whether the final verb is polite or plain, since only that final form carries the register.

# Go Somewhere for a certain objective

> P + へ + V(masu-stem)/N + に + 行きます/来ます/帰ります — go/come/return somewhere in order to do something.

## Structure

- Verb: ます形 without ます (買います→買い, 食べます→食べ); for する verbs (勉強する) use only the Noun.
- P + へ + V-ます形(no masu) + に + 行きます/来ます/帰ります
  - 秋葉原　へ　漫画を買いに行きます（あきはばら、まんが、か）- I will go to Akihabara to buy manga
- P + へ + N + に + 行きます/来ます/帰ります
  - 日本　へ　日本語を勉強に行きます（べんきょう）- I will go to Japan to study Japanese
- Negative: 行きません/来ません/帰りません - not going/coming/returning (for this purpose)
- Past-negative: 行きませんでした/来ませんでした/帰りませんでした - didn't go/come/return (for this purpose)

へ highlights the direction/route toward the destination; に treats the destination as a fixed point of arrival. In casual modern speech に is often used interchangeably with へ here; へ tends to sound slightly more formal/literary.

## Examples

- 秋葉原　へ　漫画を買いに行きます（あきはばら、まんが、か）- I will go to Akihabara to buy manga
- 日本　へ　日本語を勉強に行きます（べんきょう）- I will go to Japan to study Japanese
- 本屋　へ　本を買いに行きます (ほんや) - I'm going to the bookstore to buy a book
- アメリカ　へ　英語を勉強に来ました (えい、き) - I came to America to study English
- うち　へ　母の料理を食べに帰りました (はは、りょうり, かえ) - I came back home to eat my mother's cooking

## Notes

A frequent mistake at this level: the に before 行きます/来ます/帰ります is required — it marks the purpose, not を. Don't drop it (漫画を買い行きます✗) or replace it with を (漫画を買いを行きます✗); only the object of the purpose verb (漫画を買い) takes を, the purpose clause itself always attaches with に.

## Register
Register: neutral
The pattern itself carries no inherent formality — it appears with the polite ます forms shown here, but works equally with plain-form verbs (行く／来る／帰る) in casual speech; formality is carried by the verb ending, not by the pattern.

## Often confused with
- [ために - for (purpose of)](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/tameni-for-purpose-of) — test: Is the purpose clause built specifically on 行く／来る／帰る (→ V-stem+に+行きます/来ます/帰ります), or on any other verb needing a general purpose clause (→ ために)?

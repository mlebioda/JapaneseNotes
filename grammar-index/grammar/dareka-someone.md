# だれか - someone

> だれか = "someone/anybody" — an indefinite pronoun for people, used in affirmative statements/questions.

## Structure

- だれか + (が) + V

## Examples

- だれか(が)来(き)ましたか。 - Did anybody come?

## Notes

In polite or formal contexts (customer service, formal invitations), どなたか is used instead of だれか.

## Register
Register: neutral
だれか itself is neutral/casual; in polite or formal contexts (customer service, formal invitations), どなたか is substituted for it — formality comes from swapping the word, not from conjugating だれか.

## Often confused with

- [だれも - nobodu](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/daremo-nobodu) — test: Is the sentence affirmative (→ だれか), or negative (→ だれも)?

# ～てみる - to try

> Do something to see what happens, or to try doing something.

## Structure

### Verb
- V「て形」＋みる - to try → いい温泉を探してみます - I will try to look for a good onsen
- V「て形」＋みたい - want to try → お弁当を作ってみたいですね - I want to try to make a boxed lunch

## Examples

- いい温泉(おんせん)を探(さが)してみます - I will try to look for a good onsen
- これは私がつくったケーキです。食べてみてください - This is the cake I made. Please try it
- お弁当(おべんと)を作(つく)ってみたいですね - I want to try to make a boxed lunch (obento)
- 駅前に新しいデパートができました。いっしょに行ってみませんか？（えきまえ、あたら) - A new department store was built around the station. Why don't we try going there?
- 私は着物を着てみたいです（き）- I want to try on a kimono
- 京都に行ったことがありません。京都に行ってみたいですね - I haven't been to Kyoto. I want to try and go to Kyoto

## Use Cases

Register: neutral — works casual to polite; てみてください is the polite request form.

## Notes

- Confusable: ～ようとする (to attempt/make an effort to do — regardless of outcome) vs ～てみる (to try doing something to see the result)

## Register

Register: neutral
Used across casual and polite speech; てみてください (asking someone else to try) is itself the polite request form, not a separate formality tier.

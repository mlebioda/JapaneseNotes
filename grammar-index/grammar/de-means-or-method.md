# で - means or method

> Marks the means or method by which an action is done.

## Structure

- N + で + V
- Register: neutral — で itself carries no politeness; register comes from the verb (行きます = polite, 行く = plain)
- Exception — verbs of manner of movement (歩く, 走る) don't take で; use the て-form + 行く/来る instead

## Examples

- 電車で行きます（でんしゃ）- I'm going by train
- 新橋駅まで歩いて行きました（しんばし、ある) - I walked to shinbashi on foot
- 駅まで**走って**行きました（えき、はし）- I ran to the station

## Register

Register: neutral
で itself carries no politeness — formality comes entirely from the verb that follows (行きます is polite, 行く is plain).

## Often confused with

- [Subject + で + V - who performs action](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/subject-de-v-who-performs-action) — test: Does で mark the tool/method used to do the action (で-means), or who/how many people are performing it together (Subject + で)?
- [で - where the action will take place](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/de-where-the-action-will-take-place) — test: Does で mark the tool/method used (で-means), or the place where the action happens (で-location)?

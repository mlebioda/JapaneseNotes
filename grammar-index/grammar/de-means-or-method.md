# で - means or method

> Uses で to indicate the means, tool, or method by which an action is performed.

## Structure

N + で + V

## Examples

- 電車で行きます — I'm going by train
- バスで来ました — I came by bus

## Notes

When the means of transport is itself a motion verb (walking, running), use V-て form instead of N+で:
- 新橋駅まで歩いて行きました — I walked to Shinbashi on foot (歩いて, not 歩きで)
- 駅まで走って行きました — I ran to the station (走って, not 走りで)

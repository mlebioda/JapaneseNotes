# PにNがあります・います - there is something somewhere

> Expresses the existence of an object or person at a given place: "There is [N] at [Place]."

## Use Cases

Use があります for inanimate objects; use がいます for animate beings (people, animals).

## Structure

- Place に Noun があります・います

## Examples

- あそこに椅子と机があります（いす、つくえ）— There is a chair and desk over there.
- エレベーターのとなりにトイレがあります — There is a restroom next to the elevator.
- 会議室にだれがいますか（かいぎしつ）— Who is in the meeting room?
- 会議室に部長がいます（かいぎしつ、ぶちょう）— The general manager is in the meeting room.

# 皿（さら) - dishes

> The counter for plates/dishes, using 枚.

## Structure

| Number | Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 1枚 | いちまい | 1 plate |
| 2枚 | にまい | 2 plates |
| 3枚 | さんまい | 3 plates |
| 4枚 | よんまい | 4 plates |
| 5枚 | ごまい | 5 plates |

枚（まい）is the general counter for flat/thin objects (plates, paper, shirts, etc.), not just dishes. Its count sequence is fully regular — no irregular readings, unlike つ or 本.

## Examples

- １枚(いちまい)のお皿(さら) - 1 plate
- ３枚(さんまい)のお皿(さら) - 3 plates

## Register

Register: neutral
枚 as a counter is a plain measurement word with no politeness association — used identically in casual conversation and formal writing.

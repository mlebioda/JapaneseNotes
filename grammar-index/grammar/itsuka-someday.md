# いつか - someday

> いつか = "someday" — an indefinite pronoun for time, used in affirmative statements.

## Structure

- いつか + V

## Examples

- いつか日本(にほん)へ行(い)きたいです。 - I want to go to Japan someday.

## Notes

いつか completes the 何か／だれか／どこか／いつか "something/someone/somewhere/sometime" series. Unlike どこも・だれも・何も, however, いつも does NOT mean "never" — it means "always" (a frequency adverb, not a negative quantifier). "Never" is expressed differently, e.g. 一度も〜ない (not once) or 全然〜ない.

## Register
Register: neutral
Formality is carried entirely by the verb that follows; いつか itself does not change with politeness level.

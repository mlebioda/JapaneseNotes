# ね - seeking approval

> Sentence-final particle used to seek agreement, confirm shared knowledge, or soften a statement — equivalent to "right?", "isn't it?", "don't you think?".

## Structure

[Sentence] + ね

## Examples

- 明日(あした)の仕事(しごと)のしりょうですね — These are tomorrow's work materials, right?
- インドから来(き)ましたね？ — You're from India, aren't you?
- KIMさんは韓国人(かんこくじん)ですね — Ms. Kim is Korean, isn't she?

## Notes

ね seeks shared feeling or confirms something both speaker and listener know. Contrast with よ, which asserts new information to the listener.

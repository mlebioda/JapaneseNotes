# Degree adverbs (少し・あまり・全然)

> 少し (a little) pairs with positive predicates; あまり and 全然 (not very / not at all) mark degrees of negation.

## Structure

- S + V (positive) — 少し(すこ)
  - すこし分かる - understand a little
- S + V negation — あまり / 全然
  - あまり - not very / not much (milder negation; needs a negative predicate)
  - 全然（ぜんぜん）- not at all (stronger negation; needs a negative predicate in standard usage — casually also used with a positive meaning, e.g. 全然大丈夫 - totally fine)
- S + adj
  - インドは全然寒くないです（ぜんぜん、さむ) - India is not cold at all

Summary:
- 少し - a little (used with positive predicates)
- あまり - not very / not much (needs a negative predicate; milder than 全然)
- 全然（ぜんぜん）- not at all (needs a negative predicate; strongest of the three)

## Examples

- 全然分(ぜんぜん)かりません - I can't understand at all
- お金(かね)が全然(ぜんぜん)ありません - I don't have money at all
- このコーヒーはあまり甘(あま)くないです - this coffee isn't very sweet
- インドは全然寒くないです（ぜんぜん、さむ) - India is not cold at all

## Register
Register: neutral
少し, あまり, and 全然 are all neutral and usable in any speech style; the one exception is 全然 with a positive meaning (全然大丈夫), which is a casual colloquialism not used in formal writing.

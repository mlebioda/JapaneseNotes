# How many days

> Duration counter for expressing a span of days; uses the same irregular native readings as calendar day numbers, plus optional 間（かん）.

## Structure

- Native day reading + (間)
- Can combine with months: か月 + 日間

## Examples

- いちにち — one day
- ふつか（かん）— two days
- みっか（かん）— three days
- よっか（かん）— four days
- いつか（かん）— five days
- むいか（かん）— six days
- なのか（かん）— seven days
- ようか（かん）— eight days
- ここのか（かん）— nine days
- とおか（かん）— ten days
- 一か月二日間（いっかげつふつかかん）— 1 month and 2 days

# いっしょ  - together, with, the same (cf. 同じ)

> Expresses doing something together with someone, or that two things/people are the same.

## 1. Together with (accompaniment)

### Structure

- N（person）+ と + いっしょに + V → いっしょにいきましょう (let's go together)

### Examples

- いっしょにいきましょう - let's go together
- 友達(ともだち)といっしょに映画(えいが)を見(み)ました - I watched a movie together with a friend

## 2. Same (identity)

### Structure

- A は B と いっしょです → 私の学校は弟の学校といっしょです (my and my younger brother's school is the same)

### Examples

- 私の学校 は 弟の学校　と　いっしょです - my and my younger brother's school is the same

## Notes

Often confused with 同じ（おなじ）— test: are you saying two things are directly identical/matching (同じ), or that you're doing something together as company (いっしょに)? 私の傘は彼のと同じです (my umbrella is the same as his) vs 彼といっしょに帰りました (I went home together with him).

Note the に is obligatory for the "together" sense (と + いっしょに + V) but absent for the "same" sense (と + いっしょです) — a common point of confusion since the two structures otherwise look alike.

## Register

Register: neutral

いっしょ(に) itself carries no register of its own — used in both polite and casual speech. Politeness comes from the verb that follows (いっしょに行きましょう is polite, いっしょに行こう is casual).

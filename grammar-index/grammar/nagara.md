# ながら - While - 2 actions occuring at the same time

> While doing V1, the same subject simultaneously does V2 — V2 is the main action, V1＋ながら is the background action.

## Structure

V1 and V2 must be done by the same subject, at the same time. V2 (main clause) is the primary action and carries the sentence's tense; V1＋ながら is the secondary/background action.

### Verb
- V-ます形 + ながら + V2 → 母は、台所で歌いながら、料理を作っています - My mother is cooking in the kitchen while singing a song

## Examples

- 母(はは)は、台所(だいどころ)で**歌(うた)いながら** 料理(りょうり)を作(つく)っています - My mother is cooking in the kitchen while singing a song
- 留学生はいつもアルバイトしながら、大学で勉強します (りゅうがくせい・だいがく・べんきょう) - The international students always do a part-time job while studying at university.
- 山田さんはコーヒーを飲みながら、アニメを見ています (やまだ・の・み) - Mr. Yamada is watching an anime while drinking coffee.
- 私は歌いながら、運転しました (わたし・うた・うんてん) - I drove my car while singing.
- きのうの晩、わたしはカラオケで歌いながら、ダンスしました (ばん・うた) - Last night, I danced while singing at Karaoke.
- 彼はいつもご飯を食べながら、ゲームをします (かれ・はん・た) - He is always playing a game while eating dinner.
- わたしは富士山を見ながら、富士山を描きたいです (ふじさん・み・ふじさん・か) - I want to draw Mt. Fuji while looking at it.

## Notes

Often confused with:
- つつ - same "while" meaning, formal/written register, also covers concessive "even though"
- 間(あいだ)に - "during the interval," doesn't require the same subject, usually implies a change happens
- とき - general "when," no simultaneity or same-subject requirement

## Register

Register: neutral
Used in speech and writing, formality carried by V2's conjugation. A more literary/formal equivalent is つつ, which additionally covers a concessive "even though" sense that ながら doesn't comfortably carry.

## Often confused with

- [間（あいだ）- between, during, among](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/aida-between-during-among) — test: Does the event happen at some point within a time span, without requiring the same subject or exact simultaneity (間に), or must both actions happen at the same time by the same subject (ながら)?
- [A....とき...B - while A, B](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/toki-non-past) — test: Must both actions happen simultaneously by the same subject (ながら), or is this a general "when" with no simultaneity or same-subject requirement (とき)?

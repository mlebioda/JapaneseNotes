# N＋だけ - only N

> Restricts a statement to a single item or amount — "only N".

## Structure

N + だけ

- 一人だけ - only 1 person

## Examples

- 一人だけ - only 1 person
- 先生だけ - only sensei
- 肉(にく)だけ - only meat
- クラスに中国人（ちゅうごくじん）の学生（がくせい）が１０人（にん）います。アメリカ人（じん）は１人（ひとり）だけいます。- There are ten Chinese students in the class. There is only one American.
- 私（わたし）の休（やす）みは日曜日（にちようび）だけです。- My day off is only on Sunday.
- 母（はは）が作（つく）る料理（りょうり）だけを食（た）べたいです。- I only want to eat the food my mother cooks.

## Register

Register: neutral
Works across all politeness levels, in both spoken and written Japanese.

## Often confused with

- [しか、だけ - only](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/shika-dake-only) — test: Does the sentence use a negative verb (only しか works — 一人しかいません) or an affirmative verb (only だけ works — 一人だけいます)?

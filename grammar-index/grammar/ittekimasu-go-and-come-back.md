# 行ってきます - i go and i'll come back

> "I'm going and will come back" — used when leaving home or the office with the intention of returning.

## Structure

### Verb
- 場所＋へ／に＋行ってきます → 銀行へ行ってきます - I'm going to the bank (and will come back)
- Response: 「いってらっしゃい」("Go and come back safely")

## Examples

- 銀行へ行ってきます(ぎんこ）- I'm going to the bank (and will come back).
- 図書館に行ってきます （としょかん） - I'm going to the library (and will come back).

## Use Cases

On returning, the companion exchange is「ただいま」("I'm back") answered with「おかえりなさい」("Welcome back") — the counterpart to the いってきます／いってらっしゃい exchange used when leaving.

## Register

Register: tiered (casual/neutral, 謙譲語)
行ってきます is casual/neutral, used with family, friends, and coworkers of equal standing. Its 謙譲語 (humble) counterpart 行って参ります is used when leaving in front of a superior, client, or in a formal workplace setting.

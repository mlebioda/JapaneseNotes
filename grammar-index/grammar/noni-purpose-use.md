# のに。。。- for, to (to indicate the application of purpose)

> Purpose of use.

## Structure

### Verb
- Dictionary: V「dict」+ のに → スマートフォンは道(みち)を調(しら)べるのにとても便利(べんり)です。 - Smart phones are very convenient for looking up directions. (V + の nominalizes the verb, since a verb isn't already nominal)

### Noun
- N + に (no の needed — a noun is already nominal) → お酒(さけ)を飲(の)みすぎると、体(からだ)によくないです。 - If you drink too much alcohol, it is not good for your health.

Often used with: 使う（つか）、役に立つ（やくにたつ）、便利（べんり）、いい、悪い（わる）。

## Examples

- スマートフォンは道(みち)を調(しら)べるのにとても便利(べんり)です。 - Smart phones are very convenient for looking up directions.
- お酒(さけ)を飲(の)みすぎると、体(からだ)によくないです。 - If you drink too much alcohol, it is not good for your health. (N + に, no の)
- この写真(しゃしん)はレポートを作(つく)るのに使(つか)いたいです。 - I want to use this picture for (make) my report.

## Register

Register: neutral
Used across all speech styles and registers; formality is carried by the surrounding verb/copula, not by のに itself.

## Often confused with

- [ために - for (purpose of)](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/tameni-for-purpose-of) — test: Is this describing something as useful/convenient for a purpose (のに, only before 使う／役に立つ／便利／いい／悪い) or stating an active goal someone works toward (ために, freely productive with volitional verbs)?
- [。。。のに - even though](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/noni-even-though) — test: Same spelling, different pattern — does のに mean "even though" (contrastive, unexpected mismatch) or "useful for / for the purpose of ~ing" (only before 使う／役に立つ／便利／いい／悪い)?

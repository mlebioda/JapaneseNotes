# Make/let a person intransitive verb

> Making or letting a person do an intransitive action.

## Structure

### Verb
- (N1 は/が) N2 を Vi「causative」→ 先生(せんせい)は学生(がくせい)をうちへ帰(かえ)らせました。(The teacher let the students go back home.)

**Roles:** N1 - causer / N2 - person who acts / V - intransitive

## Examples

- 先生（せんせい）は 学生（がくせい）を うちへ 帰(かえ)らせました - The teacher let the students go back home
- 私(わたし)は、両親(りょうしん)を困(こま)らせます。 - I cause my parents trouble.
- 昨日(きのう)友達(ともだち)とけんかして、親(おや)は私(わたし)を友達(ともだち)に謝(あやま)らせた。 - Yesterday I had a fight with my friend, so my parents made me apologize to him.
- (あなたを)待(ま)たせて、ごめんなさい。 - Sorry to keep you waiting.(abbreviated)

## Notes

The causee particle can alternate between を and に, shifting the nuance from forceful to permissive:
- 子供(こども)を歩(ある)かせる — make the child walk (forceful/coercive)
- 子供(こども)に歩(ある)かせる — let the child walk (permissive)

## Register

Register: tiered
- Plain causative — top-down only; used when an authority figure (parent, teacher, boss) causes or allows a subordinate to act; rude toward superiors, not used in that direction.

## Often confused with

- [Make/let a person transitive verb](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/make-let-a-person-transitive-verb) — test: Is the causee just acting with no separate object (intransitive verb → causee marked を/に directly), or also acting upon a separate thing (transitive verb → causee marked に, object marked を)?

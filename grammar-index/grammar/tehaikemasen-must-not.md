# て形＋はいけません - You must not do

> States that an action must not be done — a direct prohibition.

## Structure

### Verb
- て形＋はいけません → ここで泳(およ)いではいけません (you can't swim here)

## Examples

- この部屋(へや)でタバコを吸(す)ってはいけません - You cannot smoke in this room
- ここで泳(およ)いではいけません - you can't swim here

## Register

Register: tiered (blunt/direct はいけません vs. casual てはだめです vs. softer 〜ないでください)
Strong, direct prohibition — natural from parents/teachers, official rules, warning signs. Too blunt/commanding toward someone of higher status (a superior, someone you should be polite to). Softer everyday alternative for asking someone not to do something: 〜ないでください ("please don't..."). Casual synonym: 〜てはだめです (だめ = "no good").

## Often confused with

- [て形＋もいいです/か - You can do/ Can I?](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/temoiidesuka-permission) — test: is the action forbidden (てはいけません) or allowed (てもいいです)?
- [V「なくて」はいけません - must to do something](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/v-nakute-ha-ikemasen-must) — test: is the subject forbidden from doing something (てはいけません) or obligated to do it (なくてはいけません)?

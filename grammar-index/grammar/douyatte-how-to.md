# どうやって - how to

> Asks about the method, process, or route for doing something.

## Structure

どうやって + V — どう (how) + やって (て-form of やる, "to do"); literally "doing how", used to ask about a method, process, or route. Register: neutral — works with both plain and polite verb forms (行った / 行きましたか).

## Examples

- 秋葉原までどうやって行きましたか - how did you get to Akihabara?
- 上野駅まで　歩いて、地下鉄に乗って、二駅で着きます（うえの、ある、ちかてつ、ふたえき、つ）- Walk to ueno station, and take the subway for two stations, then you will arrive
- 東京からどうやって京都まで行きますか - How do I get to Kyoto from Tokyo?

## Notes

どうやって leans toward asking about a method or process (e.g. giving directions); compare with plain どう ("how is it / how about"), which is more general.

二駅で着きます above uses a third sense of で not covered elsewhere in this lesson — marking the quantity/extent within which something happens ("in/within two stops"), distinct from で-means and で-location.

## Register

Register: neutral
どうやって works equally with plain and polite verb forms (行った／行きましたか), so it carries no register of its own.

## Often confused with

- [～方（かた） - a way of doing](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/kata-a-way-of-doing) — test: Is this asking a direct question about method (どうやって + V), or naming "the way of doing X" as a noun (V-stem + 方)?

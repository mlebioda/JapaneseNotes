# Nouns, adjectives, adverbs

> The お／ご honorific prefix, attached to nouns, adjectives, adverbs, and some verbs to show respect

## Structure

- ご・お + Noun/Verb/Adverb/adjective
    - お国
    - お忙しい
    - おひま
    - ご十分
- お - precedes words in Japanese reading
    - お名前
    - お仕事
    - お話
- ご - precedes words in phonetic reading
    - ご利用（りよう）
    - ご熱心（ねっしん）

## Examples

ご家族はいらっしゃいましたか - Did your family come?
おきれいな方(かた)ですね - You are so beautiful
ご自由(じゆう)にご意見(いけん)をおっしゃってください - please say your opinions freely

## Notes

Exceptions:
- お電話
- お食事
- Words already starting with お・ご
    - 美味(おい)しい
    - 大(おお)きい
- Words in katakana
    - スーツ
- Words with long moras
    - かっこいい
    - 暖(あたた)かい
    - 新(あたら)しい

## Register
Register: polite-context typical
Marks nouns, adjectives, and some verbs as belonging to or describing the listener or a respected third party — used in polite/business speech toward superiors and customers, not with casual peers.

# Subject + で + V - who performs action

> Marks who performs an action, alone or as a group, using a person/group-count noun + で.

## Structure

Person-count noun / group noun + で + Verb

- 一人で行く - to go alone

## Examples

- 一人で行く - to go by oneself
- みんなで 旅行(りょこう)する - to travel with everyone
- ３人（にん）で一つ（ひとつ）の部屋（へや）に泊（と）まります。- Three people will stay in one room.
- ２人（ふたり）で昼（ひる）ごはんを一緒（いっしょ）に食（た）べに行（い）きましょう。- Let's go to eat lunch together!
- クラスで京都（きょうと）へ行（い）きます。- We will go to Kyoto with the whole class.

## Notes

This is a different use of で from the instrument/means marker (電車で行く - "go by train"). Here で attaches to a person/group count or a group noun (一人で, みんなで, クラスで) to mark who performs the action together/alone, not what is used to perform it.

## Register

Register: neutral
Works across all politeness levels.

## Often confused with

- [で - means or method](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/de-means-or-method) — test: Does で mark who/how many people are performing the action together (Subject + で), or the tool/method used to do it (で-means)?

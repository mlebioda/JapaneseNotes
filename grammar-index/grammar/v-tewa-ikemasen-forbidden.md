# V「て形」はいけません - is forbidden to do something

> Directly forbids someone from doing something.

## Structure

### Verb

- Present: V「て形」＋はいけません → 店の入り口の前に駐車してはいけません。(みせ・い・ぐち・まえ・ちゅうしゃ) - It is forbidden to park in front of the store.

## Examples

- 店(みせ)の入(い)り口(ぐち)の前(まえ)に駐車(ちゅうしゃ)し**てはいけません**。 - It is forbidden to park in front of the store's entrance.
- あなたはお酒(さけ)を飲(の)んだから、運転(うんてん)し**てはいけない**よ。 - You mustn't drive, because you drank alcohol.
- この博物館(はくぶつかん)では、写真(しゃしん)を撮(と)っ**てはいけません**。 - It is forbidden to take pictures in this museum.

## Use Cases

Natural on signs, from a parent to a child, or between close equals.

## Notes

Often confused with 禁止されています (formal/written, impersonal — used on official signs/notices, e.g. 撮影は禁止されています "photography is prohibited"; cites a rule rather than personally forbidding someone).

## Register

Register: tiered
Direct prohibition; can sound blunt/commanding toward a superior, where V-ないでください or ご遠慮ください is preferred instead.

## Often confused with

- [V「dict」＋な - Don't do...](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/vdictna-dont-do) — test: Is this a moderate, still-somewhat-negotiable prohibition (てはいけません), or the bluntest direct command form (辞書形＋な)?
- [Vないでください - please don't do it](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/vnaidekudasai-please-dont-do-it) — test: Is this a blunter, rule-like prohibition (てはいけません), or a polite request asking someone not to do something (Vないでください)?
- [Vない＋なければなりません- I have to do](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/vnainakerebanarimasen-i-have-to-do) — test: Is the subject forbidden from doing something (→ てはいけません), or obligated to do something (→ Vない＋なければなりません)?

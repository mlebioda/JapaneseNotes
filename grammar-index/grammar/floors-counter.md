# Floors

> The counter for building floors, first floor (一階) through tenth floor (十階), plus the question word 何階 ("which floor").

## Structure

一階（いっかい）- first floor
二階（にかい）- second floor
三階（さんがい）- third floor
四階（よんかい）- fourth floor
五階（ごかい）- fifth floor
六階（ろっかい）- sixth floor
七階（ななかい）- seventh floor
八階（はっかい）- eighth floor
九階（きゅうかい）- ninth floor
十階（じゅっかい）- tenth floor (alt. reading, older/formal: 十階（じっかい）)

何階（なんがい）- which floor

## Examples

- 本屋(ほんや)は三階(さんがい)です - The bookstore is on the third floor.

## Register
Register: neutral
Used identically in casual and polite speech — this everyday counter carries no formality association of its own.

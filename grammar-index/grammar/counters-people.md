# People counters

> Counting people in Japanese using special forms for 1–2 and regular に + ん for 3+.

## Structure

Number + 人（にん）— with irregular forms for 1 and 2:

- 一人（ひとり）— one person
- 二人（ふたり）— two people
- 三人（さんにん）— three people
- 四人（よにん）— four people
- 五人（ごにん）— five people
- 六人（ろくにん）— six people
- 七人（ななにん）— seven people
- 八人（はちにん）— eight people
- 九人（きゅうにん）— nine people
- 十人（じゅうにん）— ten people
- 何人（なんにん）— how many people

## Examples

- 全部で四人です — There are four people in total.

## Notes

一人（ひとり）and 二人（ふたり）are Yamato Japanese (native Japanese words), unlike the Sino-Japanese 〜にん used for three and above.

# Vます形＋ましょうか　- Shall I/We

> A euphemistic way to invite someone to let you do something, or to decide something together, showing respect to the listener.

## Structure

### Verb
- V ます形＋ましょうか → お菓子(かし)を持(も)っていきましょうか (Shall I bring some sweets?)

## Examples

- お菓子(かし)を持(も)っていきましょうか - Shall I bring some sweets?
- 傘(かさ)を貸(か)しましょうか - Shall I lend you my umbrella?
- そろそろ帰りましょうか - Shall we head back soon? (also usable as "Shall I...?" depending on context)

## Register

Register: tiered (polite ます-based vs. casual plain volitional + か)
Polite (ます-based); the most deferential of the three ませんか／ましょう／ましょうか since it explicitly offers to do something for the listener rather than proposing a shared action — common from hosts, service staff, or anyone offering a favor. Casual equivalent: plain volitional + か (行こうか).

## Often confused with

- [Vます形＋ませんか　Shall we? / Would you like? / Why don't you...?](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/masenka) — test: is the speaker offering to do something as a favor for the listener (ましょうか) or proposing a shared action open to refusal (ませんか)?
- [Vます形＋ましょう - Let's...](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/mashou) — test: is the speaker deferentially offering to do something for the listener (ましょうか) or proposing a shared action assuming agreement (ましょう)?

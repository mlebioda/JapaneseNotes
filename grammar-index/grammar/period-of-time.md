# Period of time

> Expressing a duration made of hours and/or minutes.

## Structure

1. Hour + 間
2. Minutes + 間
3. Hour + 間 + Minutes + 分

## Examples

- 十四時間三十四分（じゅうよじかん さんじゅうよんぷん）- 14 hours and 34 minutes

## Register
Register: neutral
Hour/minute duration expressions attach the same way regardless of formality.

## Often confused with

- [間（あいだ）- between, during, among](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/aida-between-during-among) — test: Is 間 a bound suffix forming a duration directly out of hours/minutes (時間、分), or a standalone word marking the interval between two named points (AとBの間)?

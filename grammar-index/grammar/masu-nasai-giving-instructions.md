# ます形 なさい - giving instructions

> Giving firm instructions from a position of authority — firmer than てください, softer than the plain imperative.

## Structure

### Verb
- ます形 + なさい → 食べる → 食べなさい (Eat!)

## Examples

- 練習問題(れんしゅうもんだい)に答(こた)えなさい。 - Answer the exercises.
- テレビを見(み)ないで、早(はや)く食(た)べなさい。 - Stop watching TV and eat quickly.
- ご飯(はん)を食(た)べる前(まえ)に、手(て)を洗(あら)いなさい。 - Wash your hands before the meal.

## Use Cases

Used by someone in a position of authority instructing someone lower in the hierarchy, like a teacher to students. なさい sits between てください and the plain imperative (しろ) — firmer than てください, softer than しろ; not used toward superiors or peers.

## Register

Register: tiered
- なさい — authority-based instruction; parent→child, teacher→student, or written/exam instructions; firmer than てください, softer than plain imperative (しろ); not used toward superiors or peers.
- てください — neutral-polite request, usable toward anyone regardless of hierarchy.

## Often confused with

- [命令形（めいれいけい）- imperative form](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/meireikei-imperative-form) — test: Is this a calm, authority-based instruction like teacher-to-student (なさい), or a blunt, urgent command (plain imperative)?
- [V「て」もらえませんか, V「て」くれませんか](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/v-te-moraemasenka-v-te-kuremasenka) — test: Is this a firm instruction from a position of authority, or a favor-asking request phrased as a question?

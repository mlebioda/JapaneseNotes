# Adjective to Adverb

> Turn an い-adjective or な-adjective into an adverb to modify a verb.

## Structure

### い-adjective
- Drop い, add く → はやい → はやく

### な-adjective
- Add に → 真面目（まじめ）→ 真面目に

## Examples

- はやく歩(ある)きます - I walk fast
- もうすぐ試験ですから、真面目に勉強してください（しけん、まじめ）- The exam is coming soon, please study hard.
- 晩御飯の後、私はお皿をきれいに洗いました（ばんごはん、あと、さら、あら）- I cleanly washed the plates after dinner.

## Use Cases

Register: neutral, no restriction — used in casual and polite speech alike.

## Register

Register: neutral
This is a morphological rule for turning an adjective into an adverb, with no politeness dimension of its own — it is used identically regardless of the speech level of the surrounding sentence.

# ～てくる, てきます - Action toward the speaker / returning to the starting point

> Do something and come back, or have something happen and reach the present.

## 1. Action toward the speaker / returning to the starting point

### Structure

#### Verb
- Vて＋くる／きます → コンビニでお弁当を買ってきます - I'll go to the convenience store to buy a bento (and come back here)

### Examples

- コンビニでお弁当を買ってきます（おべんとう）- I'll go to the convenience store to buy a bento (and come back here)
- 友達がケーキを持ってきた - A friend brought cake (to me).
- 家に入ってきてください（はい）- please come inside the house (speaker won't move - indicates motion toward speaker)

## 2. Change reaching the present

### Structure

#### Verb
- Vて＋くる／きます → 雨が降ってきました - It started to rain

### Examples

- 雨が降ってきました（ふ）- It started to rain (and it's raining now)

## Register

Register: neutral
Used identically across casual and polite speech in both senses.

## Often confused with

- [～ていく／～て行きます Action moves away from the speaker](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/teiku-action-away-from-speaker) — test: Does the action move toward the speaker or back to a starting point (てくる), or away from the speaker (ていく)?

# 言っていました - relay ideas

> Relays something someone else said or expressed that is still relevant now — contrasted with と言いました, which simply states a past fact.

## Structure

### Verb
- Present: V「plain」+ と言っていました → 社長(しゃちょう)は明日(あす)のミーティングがなくなると言(い)っていました。 - The company president said that tomorrow's meeting is cancelled.

## Examples

- 社長(しゃちょう)は明日(あす)のミーティングがなくなると言(い)っていました。 - The company president said that tomorrow's meeting is cancelled.

## Use Cases

- と言いました — quotes what someone said, as a fact in the past.
- と言っていました — relays something someone said that's still relevant (formal, spoken and written).

## Register

Register: tiered (polite: と言っていました; plain/casual: と言っていた)
と言っていました is the standard polite form, spoken and written; dropping ました gives the plain/casual equivalent と言っていた, used with friends/family or in casual narration. A further casual contraction, **って言っていました, is covered separately (see v-tte-itteimashita-informal).**

## Often confused with

- [V「plain」そうです / そうだ - It is said that / I heard](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/sou-desu-hearsay) — test: Does the sentence name a specific person who said it (言っていました), or report the information as general hearsay without necessarily naming a source (そうです)?
- [。。。らしいです - it seems](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/rashii-desu) — test: Does the sentence attribute the statement to a specific named speaker (言っていました), or report it as hearsay/impression without naming a source, and with less certainty (らしいです)?

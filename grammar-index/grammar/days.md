# Days

> The two readings of 日, and counting a duration in days.

## 1. ひ vs にち readings

### Structure

日 has two readings depending on use:
- ひ — special/named occasions (e.g. holidays, commemorative days)
- にち — calendar days (dates)

### Examples

- この日(ひ) - this day
- 母の日（ははのひ）- mother's day

## 2. How many days

### Structure

Date reading + (かん)

| Number | Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 一日 | いちにち | 1 day |
| 二日(間) | ふつか(かん) | 2 days |
| 三日(間) | みっか(かん) | 3 days |
| 四日(間) | よっか(かん) | 4 days |
| 五日(間) | いつか(かん) | 5 days |
| 六日(間) | むいか(かん) | 6 days |
| 七日(間) | なのか(かん) | 7 days |
| 八日(間) | ようか(かん) | 8 days |
| 九日(間) | ここのか(かん) | 9 days |
| 十日(間) | とおか(かん) | 10 days |

### Examples

- 一か月二日間（いっかげつ ふつかかん）- 1 month 2 days

## Register
Register: neutral
The ひ／にち reading choice depends on meaning (named occasion vs. calendar date), not formality, and the day-counting forms (ふつか、みっか…) attach the same way in casual and polite speech.

# Days

> The kanji 日 has two readings: ひ for named/special days, にち for calendar day numbers.

## Use Cases

- ひ — used for named days and special occasions (母の日, この日)
- にち — used when counting calendar dates (十四日, 何日)

## Examples

- この日（ひ）— this day
- 母の日（ははのひ）— Mother's Day
- 十四日（じゅうよっか）— the 14th

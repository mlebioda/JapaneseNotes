# N1 + は  + N2 + に + V「passive form」- **N1 was V by N2**

> The basic direct passive: N1 is the receiver of an action performed by N2, marked with に.

## Structure

- N1 + は + N2 + に + V「passive form」- **N1 was V by N2**
    - 私（わたし）は母（はは）**に**叱**られました**（しか） - I was scolded by my mom

## Examples

- 先生（せんせい）は学生（がくせい）を褒（ほ）める - The teacher praises students
- 学生（がくせい）は先生（せんせい）に褒め**られる**（ほ） - Students are praised by the teacher
- 私（わたし）は母（はは）に叱られました（しか） - I was scolded by my mom
- 安藤(あんどう)さんは部長(ぶちょう)にほめられました。 - Miss Ando was praised by the general manager.
- わたしとラジさんは社長(しゃちょう)にパーティーに招待(しょうたい)されました。 - Raj and I were invited to a party by the company president.
- さっき知(し)らない人(ひと)に道(みち)を聞(き)かれました。 - I was asked about the way by a stranger just now.

## Notes

Two に in one clause (agent + destination) is grammatical but can read ambiguously — から often replaces the agent instead: 社長(しゃちょう)からパーティーに招待(しょうたい)されました。

## Register
Register: neutral
Used in both casual and polite speech — the passive verb ending (plain or ます-form) tracks the surrounding register, not the pattern itself.

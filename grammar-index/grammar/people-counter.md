# People

> The native counter for people, one person (一人) through ten people (十人), plus the question word 何人 ("how many people").

## Structure

一人（ひとり）- one person
二人（ふたり）- two people
三人（さんにん）- three people
四人（よにん）- four people
五人（ごにん）- five people
六人（ろくにん）- six people
七人（ななにん）- seven people (alt. reading: 七人（しちにん）)
八人（はちにん）- eight people
九人（きゅうにん）- nine people
十人（じゅうにん）- ten people

何人（なんにん）- how many people

## Examples

- 教室(きょうしつ)に十人(じゅうにん)います - There are ten people in the classroom.

## Register
Register: neutral
Used identically in casual and polite speech — this everyday counter carries no formality association of its own.

## Often confused with

- [名 (めい) - counter for people (polite)](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/mei-counter-for-people-polite) — test: Are you speaking casually or with peers (→ 人 -にん), or in a formal/service context like a restaurant or reservation (→ 名 -めい)?

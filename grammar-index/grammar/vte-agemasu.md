# Aは + Bに + Vて形 + あげます - I do something for someone

> A does an action for B, as a favor — A is typically the speaker or someone close to the speaker, never used toward a superior.

## Structure

- Aは + Bに + Vて形 + あげます - A does V for B
  - A - giver
    - You, someone close to you
    - If giver is clear, can be omitted
  - B - receiver

## Examples

- 私は弟におもちゃを買ってあげました（おとうと、か) - I bought toy for my younger brother
- 宿題を手伝ってあげます。(しゅくだい・てつだ) - I will help you with the homework.
- 妹に弁当を作ってあげます。(いもうと・べんとう・つく) - I will make a boxed lunch for my younger sister.
- 山田さんに中国語の本を貸してあげます。(やまだ・ちゅうごくご・ほん・か) - I will lend Mr. Yamada a Chinese language book.

## Notes

**Don't use to superior**. The humble form さしあげる exists but is used cautiously — even it is often avoided toward superiors in practice, since it can still sound like you're bragging about doing them a favor, so it's usually better to just state the action plainly.

## Register
Register: tiered (plain: あげる; humble: さしあげる)
あげる is the neutral/plain form for doing a favor for someone close to you. The humble さしあげる exists for superiors but is used cautiously in practice.

## Often confused with
- [Vて形 + くれます - Someone does something to me](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/vte-kuremasu) — test: Does the giver act toward someone else (→ あげます), or does someone act toward the speaker/someone close (→ くれます)?
- [Vて形 + もらいます - somebody helped you with something that you asked to do](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/vte-moraimasu) — test: Is the giver the grammatical subject doing the action (→ あげます), or is the receiver the subject, having asked for the favor (→ もらいます)?

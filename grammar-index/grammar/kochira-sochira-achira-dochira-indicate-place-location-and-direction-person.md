# こちら、そちら、あちら、どちら indicate place, location and direction, person

> Polite equivalents of ここ／そこ／あそこ／どこ, used in business and customer-service settings; also usable for direction or to politely refer to a person.

## Structure

|              | proximity to the speaker | proximity to the listener | distance from both | indefinite pronoun |
| ------------ | ------------------------ | ------------------------- | ------------------ | ------------------ |
| Thing (pronoun) | これ | それ | あれ | どれ |
| Attributive (thing or person + noun) | この | その | あの | どの |
| Place | ここ | そこ | あそこ | どこ |
| Direction | こちら | そちら | あちら | どちら |

- こちら - this way / here (polite)
- そちら - that way / there, near you (polite)
- あちら - that way / over there (polite)
- どちら - where / which way (polite)

## Examples

- こちらに椅子(いす)があります - There is a chair here (polite)
- そちらに机(つくえ)があります - There is a desk there, near you (polite)
- あちらに自動販売機(じどうはんばいき)があります - There is a vending machine over there (polite)
- おてあらいはどちらですか - where is the toilet?
- 部長はどちらにいますか - where is the general manager?

## Register
Register: polite-context typical
Used in business and customer-service settings — directing a guest or customer, addressing a superior, or speaking with someone outside your in-group — rather than casual conversation among friends or family.

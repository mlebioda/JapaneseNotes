# こちら、そちら、あちら、どちら indicate place, location and direction, person

> Polite ko-so-a-do forms for indicating place, direction, and person — more formal than ここ/そこ/あそこ.

## Structure

- こちら — this way / here (polite)
- そちら — that way / there (polite, near listener)
- あちら — over there (polite, far from both)
- どちら — which way / where (polite question); also used to ask about people

## Examples

- おてあらいはどちらですか — Where is the toilet?
- 部長はどちらにいますか — Where is the general manager?

## Notes

どちら also functions as a binary choice question meaning "which of the two" (e.g. コーヒーとお茶とどちらがいいですか — Would you prefer coffee or tea?), not only as a direction/place pronoun.

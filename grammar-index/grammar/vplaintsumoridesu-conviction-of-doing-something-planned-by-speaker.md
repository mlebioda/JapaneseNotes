# V「plain」つもりです - conviction of doing something planned by speaker

> Expresses a stronger intention or resolution than volitional form + と思います.

## Structure

### Verb
- Present: V「plain」+ つもりです → 今年(ことし)、日本語能力試験(にほんごのうりょくしけん)を受(う)けるつもりです - This year, I intend to take the Japanese Language Proficiency Test
- Negative: V「ない」+ つもりです → あの人(ひと)もう会(あ)わないつもりです - I don't intend to meet that person again

## Examples

- 今年(ことし)、日本語能力試験(にほんごのうりょくしけん)を受(う)けるつもりです - This year, I intend to take the Japanese Language Proficiency Test
- あの人(ひと)もう会(あ)わないつもりです。- I don't intend to meet that person again
- 体(からだ)が丈夫(じょうぶ)になるように、今日(きょう)から毎日(まいにち)運動(うんどう)するつもりです。- To build a strong body, I intend to exercise every day from today.
- わたしは沖縄(おきなわ)で休(やす)みを過(す)ごすつもりです。- I intend to spend my holiday in Okinawa.
- 東京(とうきょう)の生活(せいかつ)に慣(な)れたので、田舎(いなか)に帰(かえ)らないつもりです。- Because I got used to the life in Tokyo, I don't intend to return to my countryside.

## Use Cases

つもりです is the polite form; dropping です gives the plain/casual equivalent つもりだ.

## Register
Register: tiered (polite / casual)
つもりです is the neutral-polite form used in most conversation and writing. Dropping the copula to つもりだ is the casual equivalent used with friends and family.

## Often confused with

- [Volitional form + と思っています - I'm thinking of](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/volitional-form-totteimasu-im-thinking-of) — test: Has the speaker already resolved to do it, or are they still weighing the idea? (つもりです = resolved intention; と思っています = still considering)
- [V「dict」 こと ＋ に　＋ する - I decided (on my own will)](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/v-dict-koto-ni-suru-i-decided-on-my-own-will) — test: Is this an ongoing personal resolution/plan (つもりです), or a firm, one-time decision the speaker has just made (ことにする)?
- [V「plain」予定です（よてい）- plan, scheduled - fixed schedule regardless of the speaker intention](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/yotei-desu-plan-scheduled) — test: Is this the speaker's own personal resolution (つもりです), or a fixed schedule/arrangement independent of what the speaker wants (予定です)?

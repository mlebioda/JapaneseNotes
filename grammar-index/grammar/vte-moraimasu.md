# Vて形 + もらいます - somebody helped you with something that you asked to do

> The speaker (or someone close) receives an action from someone else, having asked for it as a favor.

## Structure

- Bは + Aに + Vて形 + もらいます - A does V for B
  - A - giver (marked with に, or から for tangible items/organizations, e.g. 家族から手紙をもらいました)
  - B - receiver - usually speaker

## Examples

- 私はあんどうさん**に**本を貸してもらいました（か）- Miss Ando lent me a book (I asked her for it)
- このかばんは重すぎるので、運ぶ時、ラジさん**に**手伝ってもらいました。(おも・はこ・とき・てつだ) - Because the bag was too heavy, Raj helped me carry it. (I asked for it)
- 次の電車がいつ来るかを知りたかったので、駅員**に**教えてもらいました。(つぎ・でんしゃ・く・し・えきいん・おし) - Because I wanted to know when the next train was, the station staff told me. (I asked for it)
- いつも母に晩ご飯を作ってもらいます。(はは・ばん・はん・つく) - My mom always cooks dinner for me. (I asked for it)

## Register
Register: tiered (plain: もらう; humble: いただく)
もらう is neutral/plain. いただく is the kenjougo (humble) counterpart used when the giver (A) is a social superior.

## Often confused with
- [Aは + Bに + Vて形 + あげます - I do something for someone](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/vte-agemasu) — test: Is the receiver the subject, having asked for the favor (→ もらいます), or is the giver the subject doing the action (→ あげます)?
- [Vて形 + くれます - Someone does something to me](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/vte-kuremasu) — test: Is the receiver the subject, having asked for the favor (→ もらいます), or is the giver the subject, unprompted (→ くれます)?
- [Vて形 + いただく（謙譲語）・くださる（尊敬語） - describing a favor received from a superior](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/te-form-itadaku-kudasaru-favor-received) — test: Is this the plain-register もらう statement, or the kenjougo いただく statement used for a superior?

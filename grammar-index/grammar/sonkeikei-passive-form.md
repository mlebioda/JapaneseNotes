# 1. **動詞(どうし)の尊敬形(そんけいけい) - respectful forms of verbs**

> The least formal of the three sonkeigo verb patterns — built the same way as the passive form

## Structure

**動詞(どうし)の尊敬形(そんけいけい) = passive form**

- Group 1: final -u kana → -a kana + れる (う itself becomes われる, not あれる)
    - 作る -> 作られる（つくられる）
    - 読む -> 読まれる(よ)
    - 買う -> 買われる（か）
- Group 2: る -> られる
    - 出かける -> 出かけられる
    - 出る -> 出られる（でられる）
    - 助ける -> 助けられる（たすけられる）
- Group 3
    - 来る -> 来られる (こ)
    - する -> される
    - 出席する -> 出席される（しゅっせき）

## Examples

鈴木先生(すずきせんせい)、この本(ほん)使(つか)われますか - Do you use this book, Mr. Suzuki?
おタバコをやめられたんですか - Did you quit smoking?
昨日(きのう)の会議(かいぎ)で社長(しゃちょう)はどんなことを話(はな)されましたか - What did the CEO talk about in yesterday's meeting?

## Notes

Casual/everyday register, common in speech but best avoided when addressing superiors directly — for formal writing, prefer なさっている／しておられます over plain られる.

This form is identical in shape to the plain passive voice (作られる can mean either "is made" or "[you] make [it]" respectfully) — only context and subject reveal which reading is intended.

## Register
Register: tiered (lowest of the three sonkeigo verb-formation tiers)
Still respectful, but casual/everyday — appropriate for colleagues or quick, low-effort respect in speech. Best avoided when directly addressing superiors; prefer なさっている／しておられます or the special-verb/お＋ます形＋になる patterns in formal writing.

## Often confused with
- [**3. お + V「ます形」になります**](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/o-vmasu-ninarimasu) — test: Casual/quick respect with no special verb (→ this られる pattern), or a more careful お＋ます形＋になる construction toward a superior/customer (→ お＋ます形＋になる)?
- [**2. 特別な動詞（とくべつなどうし）- special verbs**](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/tokubetsunadoushi-special-verbs) — test: Does a dedicated special verb exist for this action (→ 特別な動詞), or not (→ this られる pattern)?
- [使役形（しえきけい) - causative form](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/shiekikei-causative-form) — test: Respectfully describing what a superior does using the passive-shaped honorific (られる), or making/letting someone perform an action (causative せる/させる)?
- [受身形（うけみけい）- passive form](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/ukemikei-passive-form) — test: Is the subject a superior whose own action is being described respectfully (honorific), or is the subject receiving an action performed by someone else (plain passive)?

# V「plain」予定です（よてい）- plan, scheduled - fixed schedule regardless of the speaker intention

> A fixed schedule or plan, regardless of the speaker's own intention.

## Structure

### Verb
- Present: V「plain」+ 予定(よてい)です → ３月(さんがつ)10日(とおか)にアメリカへ出張(しゅっちょう)する予定(よてい)です - I plan to go to America on business on March 10th.
- Negative: V「ない」+ 予定(よてい)です → 来月(らいげつ)は出張(しゅっちょう)しない予定(よてい)です - I'm not planning to go on a business trip next month.

### Noun
- Present: N + の + 予定(よてい)です → 明日(あした)は休(やす)みの予定(よてい)です - Tomorrow is scheduled to be a day off.

## Examples

- ３月(さんがつ)10日(とおか)にアメリカへ出張(しゅっちょう)する予定(よてい)です。- I plan to go to America on business on March 10th.
- 来月(らいげつ)は出張(しゅっちょう)しない予定(よてい)です。- I'm not planning to go on a business trip next month.
- 展覧会(てんらんかい)に参加(さんか)する予定(よてい)です。- I plan to attend an exhibition.
- 明日(あした)は休(やす)みの予定(よてい)です。- Tomorrow is scheduled to be a day off.

## Use Cases

予定です is the polite form; dropping です gives the plain/casual equivalent 予定だ.

## Register
Register: tiered (polite / casual)
予定です is the neutral-polite form used in most conversation and writing. Dropping the copula to 予定だ is the casual equivalent used with friends and family.

## Often confused with

- [V「plain」つもりです - conviction of doing something planned by speaker](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/vplaintsumoridesu-conviction-of-doing-something-planned-by-speaker) — test: Is this a fixed schedule/arrangement independent of what the speaker wants (予定です), or the speaker's own personal resolution (つもりです)?
- [V「dict」 こと ＋ に + なっています - It is supposed to](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/v-dict-koto-ni-natteimasu-it-is-supposed-to) — test: Is this a specific upcoming scheduled event (予定です), or a general established rule/routine arrangement (ことになっている)?

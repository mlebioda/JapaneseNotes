# People

> Indirect, polite ways to refer to people without pointing directly, plus honorific title suffixes

## Structure

- for persons (indirect/polite reference, not strictly sonkeigo)
    - こちら / そちら / あちら / どちら — polite indirect way to refer to a person instead of pointing directly (add 様 for more politeness: そちら様)
    - さん — everyday honorific title
    - さま — more formal honorific title (business letters, customers)

## Examples

こちらは　山田様(さま)です - This is Mr. Yamada
どちら様(さま)でしょうか - May I ask who you are?

## Register
Register: tiered (さん vs さま)
さん is the everyday honorific title, fine with peers and acquaintances in generally polite speech; さま is more formal, reserved for customers, business correspondence, and people of clearly higher status. こちら／そちら／あちら／どちら are polite but not strictly sonkeigo — safe in most formal-leaning contexts.

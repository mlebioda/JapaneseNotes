# Nは + なadj で - AND

> Connects two or more descriptions of the same subject: な-adjectives use their で-form (and い-adjectives their くて-form) to chain onto the next description, both positive or both negative.

## Structure

Both positive, both negative:
- Nは + なadj1 で + なadj2....
	- 真面目で立派な人です（まじめ、りっぱ）- He is a serious and respectable person
- N は いadj (くて-form) + なadj + で .....
	- この部屋は広くて しずかで安いです(へや、やす) - The room is spacious, quiet, and cheap
- Noun + で + ... → 学生（がくせい）→ 学生で、忙しいです - (I am) a student and busy

Negative: drop だ, add じゃなくて
- 静か（しずか）じゃなくて、うるさいです - it's not quiet, it's noisy

For connecting positive and negative use (BUT):
- .... が ....
	- うちは駅から遠いですが静かです(とおい、しず) - My house is far from the station but is quiet

## Examples

- 真面目で立派な人です（まじめ、りっぱ）- He is a serious and respectable person
- この部屋は広くて しずかで安いです(へや、やす) - The room is spacious, quiet, and cheap
- 学生（がくせい）→ 学生で、忙しいです - (I am) a student and busy
- 静か（しずか）じゃなくて、うるさいです - it's not quiet, it's noisy
- うちは駅から遠いですが静かです(とおい、しず) - My house is far from the station but is quiet

## Use Cases

な-adjectives chain with で (dropping だ/です) — the な-adjective equivalent of an い-adjective's くて-form. A common learner mix-up is reaching for て here instead, which only works for verbs and い-adjective stems.

## Register
Register: neutral
The で/くて chaining mechanics apply the same in casual and polite speech; only the final predicate's copula (です vs plain) carries the formality tier.

## Often confused with
- [い -> くて](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/i-kute) — test: Is the word being chained an い-adjective (→ くて), or a な-adjective/Noun (→ で)?
- [But  - ...  が ....](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/contrastive-ga-but) — test: Is だ dropped (→ で, meaning "and"), or kept before が (→ だが, meaning "but")?

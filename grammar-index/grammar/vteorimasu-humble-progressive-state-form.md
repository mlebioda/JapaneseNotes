# V「て形」おります - Humble progressive / state form

> Humble (謙譲語) equivalent of V「て形」います — おる replaces いる, stacked under the polite ます ending (same layering idea as other kenjougo + teineigo combos, e.g. 参ります, 申しております)

## Structure

V「て形」+ おります

- 待つ → 待って + おります → 待っております (I am waiting / we are waiting)
- 承知する → 承知して + おります → 承知しております (I understand / noted)

## Examples

おる - humble way to say いる
わたしはABCグローバル株式会社(かぶしきがいしゃ)に勤(つと)めております。 - I am working at ABC Global company.
ご連絡(れんらく)をお待(ま)ちしております。 - I look forward to hearing from you.
お世話(せわ)になっております。 - Thank you for your continued support.

## Use Cases

- Refers to your own actions/state, or your ウチ group's actions/state when speaking to ソト (e.g., describing what your boss or coworker is doing to an outside client) — not limited to strictly "your own condition"
- Covers the same range as ている: ongoing action (progressive) and resulting/current state
- おる alone (without ます) can sound rough or dialectal (especially in casual western-Japan speech) if used as a plain "to be." In standard business/formal keigo, it almost always appears as おります, not bare おる.

## Register
Register: polite-context typical
Business and formal keigo for describing your own or your in-group's ongoing actions or state to outsiders — standard in phone calls, email closings (お待ちしております), and customer service.

## Often confused with

- [V「て形」いらっしゃいます - Respectful (尊敬語) progressive/state form](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/vteirasshaimasu-respectful-progressive-state-form) — test: Is the subject the speaker or the speaker's in-group (→ おります), or someone being honored (→ いらっしゃいます)?
- [ている → てる / ていた → てた (casual contraction)](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/teiru-teru-teita-teta-casual-contraction) — test: is this humble/kenjougo speech about your own actions to a superior (おります), or casual speech about anyone (drop the い: てる)?

# やはり・やっぱり - as I thought, still

## 1. As I thought

### Structure

Adverb. やはり is the neutral/written form; やっぱり is the casual spoken contraction. Used to confirm that a state or fact turns out as expected/predicted.

### Examples

- あの人（ひと）はやはり弁護士（べんごし）です - As I thought, that person is a lawyer

## 2. After all (outcome despite alternatives)

### Structure

Marks that, despite weighing several options, the same conclusion or outcome holds in the end — "after all."

### Examples

- お土産（みやげ）はやっぱりカメラにします - I decided on a camera as a souvenir after all
- やはり日本製（にほんせい）の贈り物（おくりもの）の方（ほう）がいいと思（お）います - I think a present made in Japan is better, after all.

# ほとんど - almost (amount)

 > Meanings: roughly, about, almost, hardly

### Structure
- ほとんど....
	- 仕事がほとんど終(お)わった - i almost finished the work
- ほとんど + ない
	- お金はほとんど残(のこ)っていない - There is not much money left

#### Examples 
ほとんど（ほとんど） - almost, roughly, about , 
ほとんど +ない - hardly, barely

Positive
仕事がほとんど終(お)わった - i almost finished the work
病気(びょうき)はほとんど治(なお)った - i have almost recovered from my illness

Negative - ほとんど + ない
お金はほとんど残(のこ)っていない - There is not much (almost none) money left
あの人とほとんど話(はな)せなかったです - I could barely (almost none) talk to him

あの人とほとんど話せなかったです - I could barely talk to him

| Word     | Meaning                                  | Typical Use                       |
| -------- | ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| **ほとんど** | almost / nearly / mostly                 | Amount or degree                  |
| **なかなか** | quite (positive) / not easily (negative) | Difficulty or positive evaluation |

# ちょうど - perfect situation/ just

## Structure

Adverb ちょうど — "just / exactly"; describes something happening at precisely the right moment, amount, or fit, e.g. a perfect size or exact timing.
- ちょうど + Adj/V — a perfect/ideal fit or situation (e.g. サイズはちょうどいいです)
- ちょうど + N — an exact/precise amount or time (e.g. ちょうどバスが来ました)
- Neutral adverb, no formality restriction — works the same in casual and polite speech; politeness comes from whatever follows it.

## Examples

- サイズはちょうどいいです - the size is perfect
- ちょうどバスが来ました - bus just came
- ちょうど８時 - just 8 o'clock
- ちょうど 5000円 - just 5000 yen

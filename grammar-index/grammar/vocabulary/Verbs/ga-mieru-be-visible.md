# が + 見える - be visible

## Structure

Intransitive verb (ichidan), reading み. Takes the が particle for the thing that is visible. 見える describes something naturally coming into sight, naturally reaching one's eyes — no intention or effort involved. Contrast with 見られる, the potential form of 見る: that word implies a person intends to see something and is able to see it.

## Examples

- 富士山が見える - Mt. Fuji is visible
- 三番出口を出て、白い建物が見えます（三番でぐち、で、たてもの、み) - After you come out from exit no 3, you can see white building

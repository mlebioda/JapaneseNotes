# 取る（とる）- to carry/hold/take/pass

## Structure

Transitive verb (他動詞). N＋を＋取る - to take, carry, hold, or pass something by hand.

## Examples

- 本を取る（とる）- to take a book
- 辞書を取る（じしょう、と）- to carry a dictionary
- 財布を取る（さいふ、と）- to carry a wallet
- そのざっしを取ってください（と）- Please pass me that magazine

# 習う（なら）- to study/get knowledge

## Structure

習う (godan, transitive) — "to learn" (by practice, from somebody). Same に/から distinction as もらう/借りる: N1 に N2 を 習う (emphasis on who it's from — a specific person); N1 から N2 を 習う (emphasis on the source — often an organization/institution).

## Examples

- スミス先生に英語を習います（なら）- I learn English from Mr. Smith

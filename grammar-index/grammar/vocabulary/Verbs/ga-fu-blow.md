# が＋吹く（ふ）- blow

## Structure

吹く (ふく) — godan intransitive verb, "to blow" (used for wind).
- Collocation: X が吹く (風が吹く - the wind blows)
- Like 降る, commonly appears in て形＋いる (吹いています) to describe weather happening right now.

## Examples

- 風(かぜ)が吹(ふ)いています。 - The wind is blowing.

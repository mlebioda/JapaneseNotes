# もらう - to get

## Structure

もらう (godan, transitive) — "to receive." Marks the giver two ways: に (emphasis on who specifically gave it) or から (emphasis on the source, often an organization/institution). N1 に/から N2 を もらう.

## Examples

- 時計をもらう（とけい）- to get a watch
- 切手をもらう（きって）- to get a postal stamp
- 私は姉から切符をもらいました（あね、きっぷ）- I got a ticket from my older sister
- 明日ワンさんにDVDをもらいます - I'll get a DVD from Mr. Wang tomorrow (emphasis on who gave it)
- おっとに花をもらいました - I got flowers from my husband
- 郵便局から切手をもらいました（ゆうびんきょく、きって）- I got postal stamps from the post office

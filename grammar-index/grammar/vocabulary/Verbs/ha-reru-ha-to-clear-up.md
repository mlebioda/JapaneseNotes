# は + 晴れる (は) - to clear up

## Structure

晴れる (はれる) — ichidan intransitive verb, "to clear up" (of weather).
- The は in this heading is not a verb-specific collocation — it's just the general topic particle attached to 明日 ("tomorrow"). 晴れる's actual subject particle is が: 天気が晴れる／空が晴れる - "the weather/sky clears."

## Examples

- 明日(あした)は晴(は)れるでしょう。 - Tomorrow will be fine weather.

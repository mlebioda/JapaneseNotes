# 乗る・降りる - get on, get off

## 1. 乗る - get on

### Structure

(に)乗る（の）- to get on. Verb, reading の. Vehicle + に＋乗る — to get on (a vehicle); Place + で＋乗る — to get on at (a place). Register: neutral — に／で carry no politeness; register comes from the verb.

### Examples

- 新幹線に乗る（しんかんせん、の）- get on the shinkansen
- バスに乗る（の) - to get on the bus
- あきはばらで乗る（の）- to get on in Akihabara

## 2. 降りる - get off

### Structure

（から）降りる（お) - to get off. Verb, reading お. Vehicle + から＋降りる — to get off (a vehicle); Place + で＋降りる — to get off at (a place). Register: neutral — から／で carry no politeness; register comes from the verb.

### Examples

- 電車から降りる（でんしゃ、お) - to get off the train
- 飛行機から降りる（ひこうき、お）- to get off the plane
- 東京で降りる（お）- to get off in Tokyo

# 渡る（わたる）- to cross from one side to the other/ go overseas by plane or boat

## 1. To cross

### Structure

Verb, reading わた. Pattern: N + を + 渡る - to cross N.

### Examples

- 川を渡る（かわ、わた） - to cross a river
- 橋を渡る（はし、わた） - to cross a bridge

## 2. To go overseas by plane or boat

### Structure

Pattern: Place + へ/に + 渡る - to go to Place overseas by plane or boat.

### Examples

- 中国(へ/に)渡る（ちゅうごく、わた）- to go to China (by plane or boat)
- アメリカ(へ/に)渡る（わた）- to go to America (by plane or boat)

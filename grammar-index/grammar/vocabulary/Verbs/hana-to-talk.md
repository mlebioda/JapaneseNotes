# 話す（はな) - to talk

## 1. Talk to someone

### Structure

Verb, u-verb (group 1). N + に + 話す — to talk to N.

### Examples

- 父に話す（ちち）- talk to father
- 部長に話す（ぶちょう、はなす）- talk to general manager

## 2. Speak a language

### Structure

Language + を + 話す — to speak in Language.

### Examples

- 日本語を話す - speak in Japanese
- 中国語を話す（ちゅうごくご）- speak in Chinese

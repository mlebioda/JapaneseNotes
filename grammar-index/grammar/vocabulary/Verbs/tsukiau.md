# 付き合う (つきあう) - to associate with / to go out with

## 1. Companionship

### Structure

Verb (G1/G3, intransitive). Used between friends, colleagues, etc. to describe an ongoing relationship.

### Examples

- １０年（ねん）付き合った（つきあった）仲（なか）- a 10-year relationship

## 2. Romantic relationship

### Structure

Describes a boyfriend/girlfriend relationship.

### Examples

- 佐藤（さとう）くんは彼女（かのじょ）と大学（だいがく）から付き合っている（つきあって）- Mr. Sato has been together with his girlfriend since university

## 3. To accompany / go along with

### Structure

To do something together with someone, often as a request — 付き合ってくれる？

### Examples

- これから本屋（ほんや）に行く（い）んだけど、付き合って（つきあって）くれる？ - I will go to the book store now, would you like to go with me?

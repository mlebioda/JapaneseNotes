# 答える（こた）- to answer

## Structure

Verb, ru-verb (group 2). N + に + 答える — to answer N.

Compare: 応える（こたえる）- same reading, different kanji/meaning: "to respond to / live up to" (expectations, requests), e.g. 期待に応える (to meet expectations) — 答える is specifically "to answer" a question. 返事する（へんじする）- "to reply", a looser, more general reply to a letter, invitation, or call (RSVP-style), not specifically answering a question.

## Examples

- 質問に答える（しつもん、こた）- to answer a question

# 終わる (おわる) - to finish (by itself)

## Structure

Intransitive — …が + 終わる: happens by itself, no one causes it. Transitive counterpart: 終える (to finish something), which takes を instead of が. Classic 自動詞/他動詞 contrast pair.

## Examples

- 夏(なつ)が終(お)わる。 - Summer ends.

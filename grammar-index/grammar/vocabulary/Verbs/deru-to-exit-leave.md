# 出る - to exit/leave

## 1. を出る／から出る - to leave/exit (a place)

### Structure

Place を出る - to leave; Place から出る - to leave. Register: neutral — を／から carry no politeness; register comes from 出る itself.

### Examples

- 会社を出る(かいしゃをでる) - to leave the company
- 部屋から出る（へや、で）- to leave the room
- 大学を出る（だいがく）- to graduate from university

## 2. に出る - to come out into (a new place)

### Structure

Place に出る - when moving to another place. Register: neutral — に carries no politeness; register comes from 出る itself.

### Examples

- 店の外に出る（みせ、そと、で) - to go outside of the store

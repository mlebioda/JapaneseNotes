# が + 聞こえる - is heard

## Structure

Intransitive verb (ichidan), reading き. Takes the が particle for the thing that is heard. 聞こえる describes something naturally coming into sound, naturally reaching one's ears — no intention or effort involved. Contrast with 聞ける, the potential form of 聞く: that word implies a person intends to hear something and is able to hear it.

## Examples

- 赤ちゃんの声が聞こえます - i baby's voice is heard
- となりの部屋から音楽が聞こえます（へや、おんがく、き) - I can hear music from the next room

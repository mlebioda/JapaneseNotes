# あげる - to give

## Structure

あげる (ichidan, transitive) — "to give," direction away from the speaker (I/we give to someone else, or between two other parties). Never used for someone giving to me — that's もらう/くれる's job. N0 は N1(person) に N2 を あげる.

Register: giving to someone of clearly higher status (e.g. a teacher) normally calls for the humble さしあげる instead — plain あげる toward a superior can sound presumptuous.

## Examples

- アンドさんに仕事の資料をあげます（しりょう）- I gave Miss Ando the work materials
- ラジさんはランさんに誕生日のプレゼントをあげました(たんじょうび) - Raj gave Lan a birthday present
- 本をあげる - to give a book
- さとうさんはおくさんに花をあげました - Mr. Sato gave his wife flowers

# 住む - to live

## Structure

住む（すむ）- to live/reside. Place＋に＋住む — implies permanent/long-term residence, not a brief or temporary stay (easy to reach for [place]にいます instead, but that only describes presence, not residence). Polite: 住んでいます; plain: 住んでいる. Honorific form お住みになる — for respectfully referring to where someone else lives.

## Examples

- ポーランドに住んでいます - I live in Poland

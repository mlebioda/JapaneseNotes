# かける - multi-use verb (hang/pour, attach, lock, wear, sit, phone, trouble, time/money)

#### Use cases

##### put something like covering or pouring liquid or powder from the top

かける - put something on the top
足(あし)に布団(ふとん)をかける。 - to put a comforter over one's feet.
たこ焼(や)きにソースをかける。 - to put sauce on takoyaki.

##### to attach something to something
かける - attach something to something
服(ふく)をかける - to hang clothes (on a hanger)

##### put something in a fixed position so that it cannot move
かける - put something in a fixed position so that it cannot move
ドアにカギをかける - to lock the door
##### put something in a removable position
かける - put something in a removable position
めがねをかける - to wear glasses

##### to sit down on a chair
かける - to sit down on a chair (more formal/polite than 座る, e.g. どうぞおかけください - "please have a seat")
椅子(いす)にかける - to sit on a chair

##### to make a phone call
かける - to make a phone call
友達(ともだち)に電話(でんわ)をかける。 - to make a phone call to a friend.

##### to cause someone trouble / inconvenience
かける - to cause someone trouble / inconvenience
彼(かれ)は家族(かぞく)に迷惑(めいわく)をかけた。 - He caused trouble for his family.

Near-synonym: 人(ひと)を困(こま)らせる (causative of 困(こま)る - "to be troubled") — "to trouble/bother someone." More personal than 迷惑をかける, usually pointing at one person's specific behavior rather than a general inconvenience.
子供(こども)は親(おや)を困(こま)らせた。 - The child troubled his parents.

##### to spend time / money on something
かける - to spend time / money on something
日本語(にほんご)の勉強(べんきょう)に時間(じかん)をかけている。 - I'm spending time on studying Japanese.
旅行(りょこう)にお金(かね)をかける。 - to spend money on travel.

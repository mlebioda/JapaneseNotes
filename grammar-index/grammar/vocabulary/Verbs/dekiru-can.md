# できる／出来る - can, to be built/completed

## 1. Ability / possibility

### Structure

- Noun: N が＋できる/できない - can/can't N
- Verb: Vdict + こと + が＋できる - can V (nominalize the verb with こと first)
- Three ways to say "can" in Japanese, don't mix them up: Vることができる (nominalize the verb with こと, then ができる, e.g. 参加することができますか); potential form (可能形) of the verb itself (e.g. 食べられる can eat, 飲める can drink); Noun＋ができる, used with a skill/noun rather than a verb directly (e.g. 運転ができます can drive).

### Examples

- サッカー**が**できる - to be able to play soccer
- 卓球**が**できる（たっきゅう）- to be able to play ping pong
- 車の運転ができます。- I can drive a car.
- 車の運転**が**できない（くるま、うんてん）- to be unable to drive a car
- 泳ぐことができる（およ）- I can swim
- 妹は弁当を作ることができます（いもうと、べんとう、つく） - younger sister can make a lunch box
- ラジさんは日本語ができます (にほんご) - Raj can speak Japanese.
- わたしは運転ができません（うんてん） - I cannot drive.
- あにはサッカーができます - My elder brother can play soccer.
- 席はまだ空いていますから、わたしたちは予約ができます (せき・あ・よやく) - There are some seats available, so we can make a reservation.
- ランさんはベトナム語を話すことができます (ご・はな) - Lan can speak Vietnamese.
- 安藤さんはピアノを弾くことができます (あんどう・ひ) - Miss Ando can play piano.
- 鳥は空を飛ぶことができます (とり・そら・と) - Birds can fly in the sky.

## 2. Coming into existence (built/completed)

### Structure

N＋が＋出来る（できる）- something new is built, completed, or comes into existence. If what precedes 出来る is a physical thing / product / building (or something abstract like a friendship or free time), the meaning is "is built / finished / came about."

### Examples

- 新しい本屋が出来ました（ほんや）- a new book store was built (opened)
- 新しい店が出来ました（みせ）- a new shop was built/opened
- ケーキが出来ました - I finished making cake
- 料理が出来ました（りょうり）- I finished cooking
- 友達ができました（ともだち）- I made a friend (a relationship "comes into being," not a physical object)

## 3. Indicates ability is excellent

### Structure

Pattern: ...一番できる人 - "the most capable/best person," using できる to describe someone's skill level rather than a specific ability.

### Examples

- あの学生はクラスで一番できる人です（いちばん）- That student is the best student in the class
- 山田さんは仕事ができます（しごと） - Mr. Yamada is very good at working (can work)

## Notes

- 出来る (kanji) is the more common spelling for the "coming into existence / built" sense (section 2); the ability senses (sections 1 and 3) are usually written in kana as できる, though either spelling is grammatically acceptable for any sense.
- Section 2 was drilled in lesson UN4GL1; sections 1 and 3 in lesson UN5GL15.

# 分かる - understand

## Structure

分かる (godan verb) — "to understand / comprehend." The thing understood is marked with が, not を. Used for cognitive comprehension of a language or concept, contrasted with 知る (to know a fact). Collocates with degree adverbs: 少し分かります (understand a little), あまり分かりません (don't understand too well).

## Examples

- 英語が分かる（えいご、わ）- to understand English
- 彼はベトナム語が分かる - he understands Vietnamese
- あまり分かりません - don't understand too well
- 音楽が分かります (おんがく) - I understand music

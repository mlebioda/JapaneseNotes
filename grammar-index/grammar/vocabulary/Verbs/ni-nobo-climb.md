# に＋登る（のぼ - climb)

## Structure

登る (のぼる) — godan intransitive motion verb, "to climb."
- Collocation: X に登る - to climb X
- 登る is one of a small class of motion verbs (登る, 上る, 通る, etc.) that can take either に or を, with a nuance difference:
  - X に登る - focuses on the destination/goal reached (climb to reach X)
  - X を登る - focuses on the path/surface traversed (climb up along X)
  Both 山に登る and 山を登る are common and correct.

## Examples

- 山(やま)に登(のぼ)る。 - To climb a mountain.
- 木(き)に登(のぼ)る。 - To climb a tree.

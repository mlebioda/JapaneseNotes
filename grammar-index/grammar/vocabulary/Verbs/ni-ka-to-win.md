# に＋勝つ　（か）- to win

## Structure

勝つ (かつ) — godan verb, "to win / to defeat."
- Collocation: X に勝つ
- に can mark either the contest/event won or the opponent defeated:
  - 試合に勝つ - に marks the contest (win the match)
  - あの人に勝つ - に marks the opponent (defeat that person)
- 試合(しあい) - "game, match" (sport rivalry), the noun most commonly paired with this verb.

## Examples

- 試合(しあい)に勝(か)つ。 - To win the game.
- あの人(ひと)に勝(か)つ。 - To defeat that person.

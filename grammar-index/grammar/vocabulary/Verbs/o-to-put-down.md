# 置く（お）- to put down

## Structure

Verb, u-verb (group 1). N + を + P + に + 置く — to put N at/on location P.

Compare: 入れる（いれる）- "to put into" a container/enclosed space (かばんに入れる - put into a bag), vs 置く which is "to place on/at" a surface or location.

## Examples

- 資料を机の上に置きました（しりょう、つくえ、お）- I put materials on the desk
- 自転車を駅のとなりに置いてください（じてんしゃ、えき、お）- please park (put down) the bicycle near the station

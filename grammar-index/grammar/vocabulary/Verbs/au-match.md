# 合う（あ）

## Use cases

### 1. matches

#### Examples
話(はなし)が合う - to get along / to click (conversation and viewpoints align)
足(あし)に合う靴(くつ) - shoes that fit (one's feet)
ネクタイが服(ふく)の色(いろ)に合います。 - The necktie matches the color of the clothes. #teineigo
### 2. Do something interactively

#### Structure
- V「ます」合う（あ）

#### Examples
話(はな)し合う - to talk it over / discuss (with each other)
愛(あい)し合う - to love each other

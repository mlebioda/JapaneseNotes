# 泊まる（とまる）- to stay the night

## Structure

泊まる（とまる）- Group 1 (u-verb), intransitive. Used with ...に泊まる (to stay the night at ...). 泊まる implies an overnight stay (e.g. at a hotel or inn), as opposed to 住む (to live/reside somewhere long-term).

## Examples

- どこのホテルに泊まりますか（と）- Which hotel will you stay at?
- あきはばら駅に近いホテルに泊まります（えき、ちか、と) - I will stay at a hotel near Akihabara station

# かかる - to spend/cost (time, money, effort)

## Structure

かかる (godan, intransitive) — "to take/cost" (time, money). The thing costed is marked with が (N + が + かかる), or omitted with a bare number (Number + かかる, no が).

## Examples

- お金がかかる - to cost money
- 時間がかかる - to take time
- 三日間かかる（みっか）- it takes 3 days
- １０００円かかる - it costs 1000 yen
- この仕事は３か月かかる（しごと、さんかげつ） - this work takes 3 months
- 旅行はお金がたくさんかかります (りょこう) - traveling costs a lot of money

# 大変(たいへん)

## 1. な-adjective — difficult / hard (bad, serious)

### Structure

大変(な)（たいへん）- difficult / hard (bad, serious). As a な-adjective, attaches to a following noun with な, per the standard な-adjective + な + Noun pattern.

### Examples

- 大変なミス（たいへん）- big mistake
- 大変な生活（たいへん、せいかつ）- difficult/intensive life
- 大変な仕事（たいへん、しごと）- tough job

## 2. Adverb — very / extremely

### Structure

大変（たいへん）- very (adverb). More emphatic and formal than plain とても, and often used in negative or serious contexts. Attaches directly before an adjective.

### Examples

- 大変大きい（たいへん、おおきい) - very big
- 大変遠い（たいへん、とおい) - very far

# 全部で(ぜんぶ）in total

## Structure

Expression, reading ぜんぶで. Pattern: 全部（ぜんぶ）+ で + [number] + です → states a total count ("in total, there are..."). Distinct from bare 全部, which modifies an object directly (全部食べた - "ate all of it") rather than introducing a total-count statement.

## Examples

- 全部で四人です（ぜんぶ、よにん）- there are 4 people in total

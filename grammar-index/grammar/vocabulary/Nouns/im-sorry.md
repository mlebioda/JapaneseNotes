# I'm sorry

order of politeness
- 申し訳ありません・申し訳ございません (formal)
- すみません (common way to apologize, to people you don't know personally)
- ごめんなさい (close people or subordinates)
- ごめん (light apology, close people)


| Word     | Meaning                                  | Typical Use                       |
| -------- | ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| **ほとんど** | almost / nearly / mostly                 | Amount or degree                  |
| **なかなか** | quite (positive) / not easily (negative) | Difficulty or positive evaluation |

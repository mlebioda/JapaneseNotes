# 所（ところ）

## 1. Place (overall)

### Structure

所（ところ）- place, in a general/overall sense (physical or abstract).

### Examples

- 静かな所（しず、ところ）- quiet place

## 2. Specifying a certain point or part

### Structure

所（ところ）used to point to a specific trait or part of something/someone.

### Examples

- 山田さんの真面目な所（まじめ、ところ）- Mr. Yamada's serious side

## 3. Referring to someone's home/place (colloquial)

### Structure

所（ところ）used colloquially to mean someone's home or place.

### Examples

- おじいさんの所（ところ）- my grandfather's house (place)

## 4. Specifying timing

### Structure

所（ところ）used to specify a moment/timing.

### Examples

- いい所に来ました（ところ、き）- I came at a good time

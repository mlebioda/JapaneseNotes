# V「て形」いらっしゃいます - Respectful (尊敬語) progressive/state form

> Respectful (尊敬語) version of V「て形」＋います — いらっしゃる raises the subject's status (the listener, a customer, a superior, or a respected third party)

## Structure

- V「て形」+ いらっしゃいます → 持(も)っていらっしゃいます、飲(の)んでいらっしゃいます

## Examples

いらっしゃいます - respectful いる
お元気(げんき)でいらっしゃいますか。 - How are you?
社長(しゃちょう)は日本酒(にほんしゅ)を飲(の)んでいらっしゃいます。 - The company president is drinking sake.
チケットを持(も)っていらっしゃいますか。見(み)せていただけますか。 - Do you have a ticket? Could you show it to me?

## Use Cases

- Covers ongoing action or current state, like ている, but honors the subject.
- いらっしゃる／いらっしゃいます also functions as 尊敬語 for 行く／来る in dictionary form.

## Register
Register: polite-context typical
Honorific register for describing the ongoing actions or state of a customer, superior, or respected third party — typical in service and business contexts.

## Often confused with

- [V「て形」おります - Humble progressive / state form](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/vteorimasu-humble-progressive-state-form) — test: Is the subject someone you honor (→ いらっしゃいます), or yourself/your in-group (→ おります)?

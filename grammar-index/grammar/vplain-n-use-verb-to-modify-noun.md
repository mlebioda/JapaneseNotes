# Vplain + N - use verb to modify noun

> Using a plain-form verb (or adjective/noun + copula) directly before a noun to modify it, like a relative clause.

## Structure

### Noun
- N1 + の + N2 → 私の仕事 - my job

### い-adjective
- Adj + N → 白い建物 - white building

### な-adjective
- Adj + な + N → しずかな人 - quiet person

### Verb
- Present:         Vplain + N       → すしを食べる人 - person who eats sushi
- Negative:        Vない + N        → すしを食べない人 - a person who does not eat sushi
- Past:             Vた + N          → すしを食べた人 - person who ate sushi
- Past-negative:   Vなかった + N    → すしを食べなかった人 - person who didn't eat sushi

The subject of the modifying clause takes が (or の, in this construction) — it's the subject of the embedded verb phrase, not of the main sentence.

## Examples

- すしを食べる人 - person who eats sushi
- すしを食べない人 - a person who does not eat sushi
- すしを食べた人 - person who ate sushi
- すしを食べなかった人 - person who didn't eat sushi
- 授業で使う本（じゅぎょう、ほん) - book to use in class
- これは母(はは)（が/の) 作(つく)ったケーキです。- This is the cake my mom made. (2 particles can be used: が/の)
- もうすぐ、昼(ひる)ごはんを食(た)べる時間(じかん)になります。- It will be lunch time soon。
- 駅(えき)の近(ちか)くに自転車(じてんしゃ)を置(お)くところがあります。- Near the station, there is a place to park your bicycle
- 私はラジさん(が/の)買った本を借りました（か）- I borrowed a book that Raj bought (2 particles can be used)
- 昨日 飲んだ牛乳はとてもおいしかったです (きのう、ぎゅうにゅう) - The milk I drank yesterday was very delicious
- これは駅の前のスーパーで買った弁当です（えき、まえ、べんとう) - This is the lunch box i bought at the supermarket in front of the station

## Register

Register: neutral
The modifying verb always stays in plain form regardless of the sentence's overall politeness level (e.g. すしを食べる人です is correct, not すしを食べます人です).

# 助詞(じょし)＋も - list things

> The particle も used to list things, pair items, emphasize something unexpected, or (with an interrogative) express total affirmation or denial.

## 1. Listing things

### Structure
- N + に + も → 彼(かれ)は毎日(まいにち)野球(やきゅう)を練習(れんしゅう)しています。雨(あめ)の日(ひ)にも練習(れんしゅう)しています - He practices baseball every day. Even on rainy days, he practices.

### Examples
- 彼(かれ)は毎日(まいにち)野球(やきゅう)を練習(れんしゅう)しています。雨(あめ)の日(ひ)にも練習(れんしゅう)しています。- He practices baseball every day. Even on rainy days, he practices.

## 2. Both A and B

### Structure
- N も N も + affirmative → 犬(いぬ)も猫(ねこ)も好(す)きです - I like both dogs and cats.

### Examples
- 犬(いぬ)も猫(ねこ)も好(す)きです。- I like both dogs and cats.

## 3. Emphasizing something unexpected

### Structure
- N + にも → 忙(いそが)しい社長(しゃちょう)が、飲(の)み会(かい)にも参加(さんか)しました - Our busy company president still attended the drinking party. (feeling of surprise)

### Examples
- 忙(いそが)しい社長(しゃちょう)が、飲(の)み会(かい)にも参加(さんか)しました。- Our busy company president still attended the drinking party. (feeling of surprise)

## 4. Total affirmation or denial (with interrogative)

### Structure
- Interrogative + も + negative → 足(あし)を怪我(けが)したので、どこへも行(い)けません - Because I injured my foot, I can't go anywhere.

### Examples
- 足(あし)を怪我(けが)したので、どこへも行(い)けません。- Because I injured my foot, I can't go anywhere.

## Register
Register: neutral
The particle も carries no inherent politeness of its own — it attaches identically to plain or ます/です-form sentences at any speech level.

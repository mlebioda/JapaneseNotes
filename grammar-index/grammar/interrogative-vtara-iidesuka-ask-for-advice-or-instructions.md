# interrogative V「た」ら + いいですか - ask for advice or instructions

> Used to ask for advice or instructions — "what should I do?"

## Structure

### Verb
- Affirmative: Interrogative + V「た」ら + いい(です)か → どうしたらいいか、わかりません - I don't know what to do

## Examples

- どうしたらいいか、わかりません - I don't know what to do
- A: 明日(あした)日本語(にほんご)のスピーチで何(なに)を言(い)ったらいいですか。B: 日本(にほん)の留学生活(りゅうがくせいかつ)のことを話(はな)したほうがいいと思(おも)います。- A: What should I say in tomorrow's Japanese speech? B: I think you'd better talk about your life studying abroad in Japan.
- A: 秋葉原(あきはばら)に行(い)きたいんですが、何線(なにせん)に乗(の)ったらいいですか。B: 地下鉄(ちかてつ)のひびや線(せん)で行(い)けます。- A: I want to go to Akihabara, which line should I take? B: You can go there by the subway Hibiya line

## Register
Register: tiered (polite / casual)
いいですか is the neutral-polite form suitable for most conversations, including asking someone else for advice. Dropping to plain いいか (as in どうしたらいいか、わかりません) is used for self-directed musing or casual speech.

## Often confused with

- [Interrogative V conditional form + 　いいですか - ask for opinion or advice](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/v-ba-iidesuka) — test: Is this the ば-conditional version of "what should I do?" (ば), or the たら-conditional version, which is equally common and often more natural in speech (たら)?

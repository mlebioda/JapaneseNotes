# ～ていく／～て行きます Action moves away from the speaker

> Take something and go away from the speaker, or have a process keep going into the future.

## 1. Action moves away from the speaker

### Structure

#### Verb
- Vて＋いく／行きます → お弁当を買って行きます - I'll buy a bento and go

### Examples

- お弁当を買って行きます - I'll buy a bento and go (I won't come back here).

## 2. Change continues into the future

### Structure

#### Verb
- Vて＋いく／行きます → これからもっと寒くなって行きます - It will keep getting colder

### Examples

- これからもっと寒くなって行きます (さむ) - It will keep getting colder from now on.

## Register

Register: neutral
Used identically across casual and polite speech in both senses.

## Often confused with

- [～てくる, てきます - Action toward the speaker / returning to the starting point](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/tekuru-action-toward-speaker) — test: Does the action move away from the speaker (ていく), or toward the speaker / back to a starting point (てくる)?

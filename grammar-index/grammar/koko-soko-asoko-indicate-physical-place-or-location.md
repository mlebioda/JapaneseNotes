# ここ、そこ、あそこ - indicate physical place or location

> Location pronouns in the ko-so-a-do series: ここ (here), そこ (there, near listener), あそこ (over there), どこ (where).

## Structure

- ここ — here (near speaker)
- そこ — there (near listener)
- あそこ — over there (far from both)
- どこ — where (question)

## Examples

- 会議室はどこですか？（かいぎしつ）— Where is the meeting room?

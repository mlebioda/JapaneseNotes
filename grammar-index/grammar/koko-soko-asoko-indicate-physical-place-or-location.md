# ここ、そこ、あそこ - indicate physical place or location

> Plain, neutral words for "here," "there (near listener)," and "over there (far from both)."

## Structure

- ここ - here
- そこ - there (near listener)
- あそこ - over there (far from both)
- どこ - where

## Examples

- 会議室(かいぎしつ)はどこですか？ - where is the meeting room?
- ここに受付(うけつけ)があります - There is a reception desk here
- そこに自動販売機(じどうはんばいき)があります - There is a vending machine there (near you)
- あそこに椅子(いす)と机(つくえ)があります - There is a chair and desk over there

## Register
Register: neutral
Plain, everyday location words used in ordinary conversation; their polite equivalents (こちら／そちら／あちら／どちら) are used in more formal or customer-facing settings.

# 冊　（さつ) - number of books

> How to count books using 冊, one through ten.

## Structure

Number + 冊（さつ）

- １冊（いっさつ）- one (book)
- ２冊（にさつ）- two (books)
- ３冊（さんさつ）- three (books)
- ４冊（よんさつ）- four (books)
- ５冊（ごさつ）- five (books)
- ６冊（ろくさつ）- six (books)
- ７冊（ななさつ）- seven (books)
- ８冊（はっさつ）- eight (books)
- ９冊（きゅうさつ）- nine (books)
- １０冊（じゅっさつ）- ten (books)
- 何冊（なんさつ）- how many (books)

## Examples

- 本（ほん）を３冊（さんさつ）読（よ）みました。- I read three books.

## Register

Register: neutral
Counter readings themselves don't carry politeness — they attach identically in polite and plain sentences; only the surrounding verb/copula changes with register.

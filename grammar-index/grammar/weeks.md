# weeks

> Counting a duration in weeks using 週間.

## Structure

Number + 週間

| Number | Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 一週間 | いっしゅうかん | 1 week |
| 二週間 | にしゅうかん | 2 weeks |
| 三週間 | さんしゅうかん | 3 weeks |
| 四週間 | よんしゅうかん | 4 weeks |
| 五週間 | ごしゅうかん | 5 weeks |
| 六週間 | ろくしゅうかん | 6 weeks |
| 七週間 | ななしゅうかん | 7 weeks |
| 八週間 | はっしゅうかん | 8 weeks |
| 九週間 | きゅうしゅうかん | 9 weeks |
| 十週間 | じゅっしゅうかん | 10 weeks |

## Examples

## Register
Register: neutral
週間 attaches the same way regardless of formality — used identically in casual conversation and polite/formal speech.

## Often confused with

- [間（あいだ）- between, during, among](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/aida-between-during-among) — test: Is 間 a bound suffix directly counting how many weeks something lasts (週間), or a standalone word marking the span/interval between two named points (AとBの間)?

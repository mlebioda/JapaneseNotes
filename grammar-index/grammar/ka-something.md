# 何か - something

> 何か = "something" — an indefinite pronoun used in affirmative statements/questions.

## Structure

- 何か + (を) + V
- 何か + Adjective + の + V — "something + adjective"

## Examples

- 何(なに)かを食(た)べますか。 - What will you eat? (something)
- 何(なに)かおいしいものを買(か)いました。 - I bought something delicious.

## Register
Register: neutral
Formality is carried entirely by the verb that follows; 何か itself does not change with politeness level.

## Often confused with

- [何も - nothing](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/mo-nothing) — test: Is the sentence affirmative (→ 何か), or negative (→ 何も)?

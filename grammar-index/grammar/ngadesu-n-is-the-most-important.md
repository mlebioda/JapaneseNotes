# Nが一番です - N is the most important

> N is the most important / the best.

## Structure

### Noun
- N + が + 一番です → 健康(けんこう)が一番(いちばん)です。(Health is the most important.)

### Verb
- V「dict」+ の/こと + が + 一番です → 風邪(かぜ)を引(ひ)いた時(とき)は薬(くすり)を飲(の)んで寝(ね)るのが一番(いちばん)だ。(When you catch a cold, it is most important to take medicine and sleep.)

### い-adjective
- いadj + の + が + 一番です → 見(み)やすいのが一番(いちばん)です。(It is most important to see clearly.)

## Examples

- 健康(けんこう)が一番(いちばん)です。 - Health is the most important.
- 見(み)やすいのが一番(いちばん)です。 - It is most important to see clearly (easily).
- 風邪(かぜ)を引(ひ)いた時(とき)は薬(くすり)を飲(の)んで寝(ね)るのが一番(いちばん)だ。 - When you catch a cold, it is most important to take medicine and sleep.

## Register

Register: tiered
- 一番です — polite/neutral, spoken and written.
- 一番だ — plain/casual, friends/family or internal monologue/writing.

## Often confused with

- [N のほうが adj です - N is more adj](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/n-nohouga-adj-desu-n-is-more-adj) — test: Are you comparing more than two things and picking the single best/most extreme one (一番), or comparing exactly two specific things against each other (のほうが)?

# 辞書形（じしょ）- dictionary form

> The plain, dictionary-form citation of a verb — the casual/neutral form used between friends and family.

## Structure

- Dictionary form: V (plain, non-past affirmative) → 食べる, 行く, する

## Examples

- 明日だれと会うの？ - who will you meet tomorrow? (dict)

## Notes

Often confused with ます形 — test: are you speaking casually with friends/family (辞書形), or in a neutral-polite context like strangers, colleagues, or most everyday situations (ます形)? The two are not interchangeable mid-register without deliberate code-switching.

## Register

Register: tiered (plain/casual 辞書形 — neutral-polite ます形 — respectful/humble keigo above that)

辞書形 sits at the plain/casual base of the register system — used with friends, family, and as the neutral dictionary citation form. Moving up: ます形 (teineigo, neutral-polite) for strangers and colleagues, then sonkeigo／謙譲語 (respectful/humble) for status-sensitive contexts.

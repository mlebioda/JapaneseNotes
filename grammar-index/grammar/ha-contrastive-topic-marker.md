# は - marks a phrase (already carrying another particle) as a contrastive topic

> Marks a phrase that already carries another particle as a contrastive topic — "in/at/from/until THIS one, unlike others" — very common in negative/restrictive statements.

## Structure

- combines with (stacks after) other particles marking place/time/direction/extent:
  - には
  - では
  - のは
  - からは
  - までは
  - へは
- replaces (never combines with):
  - を
  - が

## Examples

- 病院(びょういん)では、携帯電話(けいたいでんわ)をかけてはいけません - It is forbidden to use the cellphone in the hospital (emphasize hospital)
- みんなには言(い)わないでください。 - Don't tell the other people. (emphasize others/everyone)
- 明日からは休みです。(あした・やす) - Starting tomorrow is a holiday.(emphasize from tomorrow)
- この部屋ではタバコを吸ってはいけません。(へや・す) - It is forbidden to smoke in this room. (emphasize this room)

## Notes

Often confused with: contrastive も, which stacks onto other particles the same way (には も, とも, でも) but means "also/even" rather than "this one, unlike others"

## Register
Register: neutral
Used identically in casual and polite speech — its function is scope/contrast, not politeness.

## Often confused with
- [Nなら - as for,  jesli o tym mowa](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/nnara-as-for) — test: Is this contrastively marking a phrase that already carries another particle (→ には／では／のは etc.), or picking up and commenting on a topic just mentioned (→ Nなら)?
- [N＋を　vs N＋は](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/n-wo-vs-n-ha) — test: Is は stacking onto a particle that marks place/time/direction (に／で／から／まで／へ), or replacing a bare subject/object marker (が／を) entirely?
- [N1 は N2 が ADJ です — N2 is a trait/feature of N1](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/n1-ha-n2-ga-adj-desu-n2-is-a-trait-feature-of-n1) — test: Is は stacking onto another particle or replacing が／を entirely, or does が still mark a second subject N2 alongside は marking N1 (→ N1は N2が ADJです double-subject construction)?

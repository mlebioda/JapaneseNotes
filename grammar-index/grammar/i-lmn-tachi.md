# I - lmn + Tachi

> First-person pronouns ("I") and their register/formality levels.

## Structure

- わたし - I (anyone can use it)
- わたくし - formal I, in public
- あたし - I (used by woman, non-formal)
- 僕（ぼく）ら・たち - I (used by men), from lower position, not formal — plural: 僕ら／僕たち
- 俺（おれ）ら・たち - I (used by men) — rough, blunt, masculine; casual only — plural: 俺ら／俺たち

Register (use / avoid):
- わたし - neutral polite; safe in almost all situations
- わたくし - very formal only (business, ceremonies, public speech)
- あたし - casual only; women, informal chat
- 僕 - casual-to-neutral; boys/young men; avoid in very formal ceremonial speech
- 俺 - casual only; close friends/family; avoid with strangers, superiors, formal settings

Plural: the rest add たち for plural forms — but あなたたち/あなたがた sounds unnatural for addressing groups, so natives prefer みなさん instead.

## Examples

## Register
Register: tiered (formal: わたくし; neutral: わたし; casual: あたし／僕; rough/casual: 俺)
わたくし is reserved for business, ceremonies, and public speech; わたし is the safe neutral default in almost any setting; あたし and 僕 are casual and gendered (あたし: women, 僕: men/boys), used with friends and family; 俺 is rough and masculine, appropriate only among close friends, never toward strangers or superiors.

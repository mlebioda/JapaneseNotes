# 台（だい) - machines and vehicles

> How to count machines and vehicles using 台, one through ten.

## Structure

Number + 台（だい）

- １台（いちだい）- one (machine/vehicle)
- ２台（にだい）- two (machines/vehicles)
- ３台（さんだい）- three (machines/vehicles)
- ４台（よんだい）- four (machines/vehicles)
- ５台（ごだい）- five (machines/vehicles)
- ６台（ろくだい）- six (machines/vehicles)
- ７台（ななだい）- seven (machines/vehicles)
- ８台（はちだい）- eight (machines/vehicles)
- ９台（きゅうだい）- nine (machines/vehicles)
- １０台（じゅうだい）- ten (machines/vehicles)
- 何台（なんだい）- how many (machines/vehicles)

## Examples

- 車（くるま）が２台（にだい）あります。- There are two cars.

## Register

Register: neutral
Counter readings themselves don't carry politeness — they attach identically in polite and plain sentences; only the surrounding verb/copula changes with register.

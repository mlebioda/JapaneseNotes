# 辞書けい（じしょ）- dictionary form

> The plain base form of a verb, used in dictionaries and in casual speech with friends or family.

## Use Cases

Used when looking up a word in a dictionary and when speaking informally with friends or family (as opposed to the polite masu form).

## Structure

Base verb form (plain/dictionary form)

## Examples

- 明日だれと会うの？ — Who will you meet tomorrow? (casual)

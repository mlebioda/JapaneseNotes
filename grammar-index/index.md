- [passive-causative](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/passive-causative) — 受身形／使役形 and the sentence patterns built on them.

- [decisions-plans-intentions](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/decisions-plans-intentions) — Deciding, planning, intending, wanting, and inviting someone along.

- [nominalization-relative-clauses](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/nominalization-relative-clauses) — の／こと-based nominalizers, and clauses that modify a noun.
- [speech-registers](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/speech-registers) — The politeness/formality system — teineigo, sonkeigo, kenjougo, keigo verb forms, and register-driven word/particle choices.

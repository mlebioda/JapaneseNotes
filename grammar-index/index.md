# Grammar Index

## Verbs

- [verb-te-form](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/verb-te-form) — Patterns built on the verb て-form — connecting, progressive/state, and auxiliary constructions.
- [communication-verbs](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/communication-verbs) — Verbs for informing, telling, reporting, and contacting others.
- [causative](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/causative) — Patterns for making or letting someone do something (使役形), including the humble causative-request forms used to ask permission from a superior.
- [passive-voice](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/passive-voice) — Patterns for the passive/receptive verb form (受身形) — direct passive, suffering passive, agentless informational passive, and material/ingredient passive.
- [verb-transitivity](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/verb-transitivity) — Patterns and vocabulary for the transitive/intransitive (他動詞／自動詞) verb pair system — which particle a verb takes and how paired verbs differ in meaning.
- [verb-volitional-form](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/verb-volitional-form) — Patterns built on the verb volitional form (行こう, 食べよう) — expressing the speaker's will, a suggestion, or an intention still being considered.
- [verb-potential-form](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/verb-potential-form) — The potential form (可能形) — conjugating a verb to express "can do X" — plus its group-by-group formation rules.
- [giving-receiving](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/giving-receiving) — Patterns for giving, receiving, or doing an action as a favor for someone — あげる／くれる／もらう and their て形 auxiliary forms.

## Particles

- [particles-restrictive](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/particles-restrictive) — Patterns for limiting a statement to a single item or option — "only" — しか (with negative form) and だけ (with affirmative form).
- [particles-mo](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/particles-mo) — Patterns built on the particle も — listing items, pairing "both A and B," emphasizing something unexpected, and (with an interrogative) expressing total affirmation or total denial.
- [particles-ga](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/particles-ga) — The particle が used to connect two clauses as "but," or to softly introduce a new topic.
- [particles-de](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/particles-de) — The particle で marking who performs an action, alone or as a group, distinct from its instrument/means use.
- [topic-marking](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/topic-marking) — Patterns and particles for marking or shifting a sentence's topic — responsive topic-picking なら and contrastive-stacking は — and related forms.

## Patterns

- [requests-commands](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/requests-commands) — Patterns for asking, requesting, and instructing — from plain requests to honorific service-industry forms.
- [decisions-plans](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/decisions-plans) — Patterns for stating a decision or an arrangement — a firm choice made by the speaker, a decision made by someone/something else, or an already-established rule or schedule: ことにする, ことになる, ことになっている.
- [nominalization](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/nominalization) — Patterns for turning a verb, adjective, or clause into a noun so it can act as a subject, object, or topic: の, こと, and related formal nouns.
- [conditionals](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/conditionals) — Patterns for expressing hypothetical or general "if/when" situations — たら, ば, なら, と, 場合, and related forms.
- [action-timing](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/action-timing) — Patterns marking the timing of an action relative to now — about to start, in progress, or just finished.
- [similarity-inference](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/similarity-inference) — Patterns for expressing resemblance, simile, or an inference based on indirect evidence — まるで, ようだ／ようです, みたい, contrasted with そう(様態).
- [change-of-state](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/change-of-state) — Patterns expressing a change of state over time — gaining or losing an ability, or starting/stopping a habit: ようになる, なくなる, and related forms.
- [effort-intention](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/effort-intention) — Patterns expressing a speaker's deliberate effort or resolution to do (or not do) something, including polite requests built on that framing: ようにする, ようにしている, ようにしてください.
- [desire-want](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/desire-want) — Patterns for expressing desire — wanting a thing, wanting to do something yourself, or wanting someone else to do something: が欲しい, たい, てほしい.
- [concession-contrast](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/concession-contrast) — Patterns expressing a mismatch between expectation and reality — despite/even though something is true, the opposite happens: のに, and related forms.
- [sensory-perception](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/sensory-perception) — Patterns for expressing a sensory quality being perceived — smell, taste, sound, or feeling — arising on its own: がする, and related forms.
- [comparison](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/comparison) — Patterns for comparing two things or ranking the most extreme case — which one is more X, or which one is the most X: のほうが, と比べて, 一番, and related forms.
- [purpose-reason](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/purpose-reason) — Patterns for stating a goal or purpose behind an action — ために, ように, and related forms.
- [obligation-necessity](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/obligation-necessity) — Patterns expressing obligation, prohibition, necessity, and permission — from strongest "must" to "don't have to" to "may" — plus はずです, the related epistemic pattern for logical expectation.
- [manner-accordance](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/manner-accordance) — Patterns for describing an action performed in accordance with a plan, instruction, or expectation — 通り／どおり, and related forms.
- [subordinate-clauses](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/subordinate-clauses) — Patterns describing how a subordinate clause carries its own が-marked subject, separate from the main clause's subject — とき, てから, まえに, たら, ても, and related connectors.
- [hearsay-reported-speech](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/hearsay-reported-speech) — Patterns for reporting information heard from someone else, rather than experienced directly — そうです（伝聞）, らしいです.
- [ease-difficulty](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/ease-difficulty) — Patterns for describing how easy or difficult an action is to do, attaching to a verb's ます-stem: やすい, にくい.
- [question-formation](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/question-formation) — Patterns for forming and embedding questions — indirect/embedded questions, question-word combinations, and related forms.
- [conjecture-guessing](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/conjecture-guessing) — Patterns for expressing a guess or confident assumption, and its confidence level — from confirming a view the speaker already holds (んじゃない) to a guess presented as likely (でしょう) to an unconfident possibility (かもしれません).
- [reason-listing](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/reason-listing) — Patterns for listing multiple reasons or descriptive traits about one topic, implying the list isn't exhaustive: し, and related forms.
- [advice-suggestions](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/advice-suggestions) — Patterns for giving advice or a suggestion to the listener: ほうがいい, and related forms.
- [reasons-causes](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/reasons-causes) — Patterns for stating a direct cause or reason for something — から, ので, and related forms.
- [listing-examples](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/listing-examples) — Patterns for giving example items from a set, implying there could be more like them — とか...とか, や...や, and related forms.

## Forms & Counters

- [counters-numbers](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/counters-numbers) — Number/counter reference tables — how to count different categories of objects.
- [temporal-adverbs](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/temporal-adverbs) — Adverbs marking the ongoing status of a state relative to expectation — still happening, no longer happening, already happened, not yet happened: まだ, もう.
- [range-interval](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/range-interval) — Patterns marking a range or span — a distance/time interval, a specific action occurring within a span, or a state holding across a group: 間.

## Other

- [humble-expressions](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/humble-expressions) — Kenjougo (謙譲語) — patterns for lowering your own actions, status, and possessions when speaking to or about people outside your group.
- [polite-expressions](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/polite-expressions) — Teineigo (丁寧語) — the neutral polite register (ます／です and their formal variants) showing formality and social distance, independent of relative rank.
- [respectful-expressions](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/respectful-expressions) — Sonkeigo (尊敬語) — patterns for raising the status of the listener, a customer, a superior, or a respected third party.
- [politeness-hierarchy](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/politeness-hierarchy) — Compares the three 尊敬語 verb-formation patterns by formality level, from highest to lowest: dedicated special verbs, お＋ます形＋になる, and the られる passive-form pattern.
- [relationships](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/relationships) — Vocabulary and expressions for describing interpersonal relationships — friendships, romantic relationships, and social bonds.

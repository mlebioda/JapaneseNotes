# Temporal Expressions

> とき／ながら／まで／ところ／ばかり and other time-relative clause, duration, and aspect-timing patterns.

### とき／ながら／とすぐ — Simultaneous & Sequential Clauses

> Clauses that link two actions by when they happen relative to each other.

- [ながら - While - 2 actions occuring at the same time](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/nagara) · N5
- [A....とき...B - while A, B](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/toki-non-past) · N5

### ところ — Precise Action Timing

> The three ところです forms pinpointing a moment relative to an action: about to start, in progress, just finished.

- [1. V「dict」ところです - about to start](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/tokoro-desu-dict-form) · N4
- [2. V「て形」いるところです - action in progress right now](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/tokoro-desu-teiru-form) · N4
- [3. V「た形」ところです - action has just finished](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/tokoro-desu-ta-form) · N4

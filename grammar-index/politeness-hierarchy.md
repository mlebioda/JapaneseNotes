# Politeness Hierarchy

> Compares the three 尊敬語 verb-formation patterns by formality level, from highest to lowest: dedicated special verbs, お＋ます形＋になる, and the られる passive-form pattern.

- [**3. お + V「ます形」になります**](/JapaneseNotes/grammar-index/grammar/o-vmasu-ninarimasu) · N4 — High: superiors/customers, no special verb, ます-stem ≥2 mora

# JLPT N4 Grammar — Master List

Compiled and cross-checked from multiple sources (there is no single official JLPT grammar list, so this list merges several study resources to avoid gaps):
- [JLPTsensei.com N4 Grammar List](https://jlptsensei.com/jlpt-n4-grammar-list/) (132 points)
- [Japanesetest4you.com N4 Grammar List](https://japanesetest4you.com/jlpt-n4-grammar-list/) (106 points)
- [Coto Japanese Academy — 20 Must-Know Grammar Points for the JLPT N4](https://cotoacademy.com/20-must-know-grammar-points-jlpt-n4/)

Organized by category for study purposes. Nothing from the source lists was intentionally omitted — overlapping entries across sources were merged into one line.

## grammar-index/ coverage check

**Result: 78 / 119 individual points covered (≈66%).** Real gaps (nothing in the vault at any level): plain こと／さ nominalizers, ということ, ことができる, はずがない, づらい, そうに／そうな, みたいだ／みたいな／みたいに, ように／ような in its resemblance sense (only the unrelated purpose sense "in order to" exists), と言われている／と聞いた／と言ってもいい, させられる (causative-passive), つづける, 出す, 始める (transitive), 頃／ごろ, さっき, やっと, かしら／かな／かい, のは～だ, から作る, に気がつく, に見える, causal て／で, それでも／それに／さすが／きっと／急に, は～が…は, また／または, まま, emphatic も, がする, がる／がっている, ことがある (N4 "sometimes" sense — only the N5 "have done before" sense exists), and たらどう／たらどうですか.

## 1. Conditionals

- 🟢 ば (ba) — "if... then" (conditional form)
- 🟢 たら (tara) — "if / when / after..." (conditional)
- 🟢 なら (nara) — "if it's the case that...", "if you're talking about..."
- 🟢 と (to) — "whenever [A] happens, [B] happens" (natural/automatic consequence)
- 🟢 場合は (baai wa) — "in the event of...", "in the case that..."
- 🔴 たらどう／たらどうですか (tara dou / tara dou desu ka) — "why don't you...?"
- 🟢 たらいいですか (tara ii desu ka) — "what should I do (if...)?"

## 2. Giving, Receiving, and Doing Favors (授受表現)

- 🟢 てあげる (te ageru) — to do something for someone (as a favor)
- 🟢 てくれる (te kureru) — someone does something for me/my in-group
- 🟢 てもらう (te morau) — to get someone to do something (receive the favor)
- 🔴 てやる (te yaru) — to do for (casual, used toward inferiors/animals)
- 🟢 てほしい (te hoshii) — "I want you to..."
- 🟢 ていただけませんか (te itadakemasen ka) — "could you please...?" (polite request)
- 🟢 させてください (sasete kudasai) — "please let me do..."

## 3. Passive, Causative, and Causative-Passive

- 🟢 られる (rareru) — potential form (ability to do something)
- 🟢 られる／受身形 (rareru / ukemi kei) — passive voice/form
- 🟢 させる (saseru) — causative form ("to make/let someone do")
- 🔴 させられる (saserareru) — causative-passive ("to be made to do") — not covered anywhere; only plain causative させる exists

## 4. Honorific and Polite Language (敬語)

- 🟢 でございます (de gozaimasu) — honorific "to be"
- 🟢 ございます (gozaimasu) — honorific "to be/exist"
- 🟢 いらっしゃる (irassharu) — honorific "to be/come/go"
- 🟢 いたす／いたします (itasu/itashimasu) — humble "to do"
- 🟢 なさる (nasaru) — honorific "to do"
- 🟢 なさい (nasai) — soft/firm command form
- 🟢 お～ください (o~kudasai) — honorific "please do"
- 🟢 お～になる (o~ni naru) — honorific "to do"

## 5. Nominalizers and Noun-Forming Patterns

- 🔴 こと (koto) — verb nominalizer (standalone) — only appears inside compound patterns like ことになる, not taught as its own point
- 🔴 さ (sa) — "-ness"; nominalizer for adjectives
- 🔴 ということ (to iu koto) — converts a phrase/sentence into a noun
- 🟢 という (to iu) — "called...", "that..." (covered via という意味です)
- 🟢 って (tte) — casual "named/called"; also casual quotation particle

## 6. Ability, Necessity, and Difficulty

- 🔴 ことができる (koto ga dekiru) — "can do", "able to do" — only the られる potential form is covered, not this construction
- 🟢 はずだ (hazu da) — "it must be", "it should be" (expectation)
- 🔴 はずがない (hazu ga nai) — "there's no way that...", "cannot be"
- 🟢 必要がある／必要（が必要） (hitsuyou ga aru / ga hitsuyou) — "need to", "is necessary"
- 🟢 なければいけない／なければならない (nakereba ikenai/naranai) — "must do", "have to do"
- 🟢 なくてはいけない／なくてはならない (nakute wa ikenai/naranai) — "must do", "have to do"
- 🟢 なくてもいい (nakute mo ii) — "don't have to"
- 🟢 にくい (nikui) — "difficult to do"
- 🟢 やすい (yasui) — "easy to do", "likely to"
- 🔴 づらい (zurai) — "difficult/awkward to do"

## 7. Hearsay, Appearance, and Conjecture

- 🟢 そうだ (sou da) — 1) hearsay: "I heard that...", "it is said that..."
- 🟢 そうだ (sou da) — 2) visual conjecture: "looks like...", "appears..."
- 🔴 そうに／そうな (sou ni/sou na) — only the そうです predicate form is covered, not the adverbial/attributive そうに／そうな
- 🔴 みたいだ／みたいな／みたいに (mitai da/na/ni) — "like...", "similar to..." (casual) — not covered anywhere
- 🟢 らしい (rashii) — "seems like...", "apparently", "typical of..."
- 🟢 ようだ (you da) — "it seems that...", "it appears that..."
- 🔴 ように／ような (you ni/you na) — resemblance sense not covered; only the unrelated purpose sense ("in order to", ように＝目的) exists in the vault
- 🟢 かもしれない (kamoshirenai) — "might", "perhaps" (possibility)
- 🟢 と思う (to omou) — "I think that..."
- 🔴 と言われている (to iwarete iru) — "it is said that..."
- 🔴 と聞いた (to kiita) — "I heard that..."
- 🔴 と言ってもいい (to ittemo ii) — "you could say...", "one might say..."

## 8. Change, Becoming, and Progressive Actions

- 🟢 ようになる (you ni naru) — "to reach the point that...", "to come to..."
- 🟢 ようにする (you ni suru) — "to try to...", "to make sure that..."
- 🟢 ようと思う (you to omou) — "I'm thinking of doing...", "planning to..."
- 🔴 つづける／続ける (tsuzukeru) — "to continue to..."
- 🟢 ていく (te iku) — "to go on doing", "to continue"
- 🟢 てくる (te kuru) — "to come to be", "to continue", "to start"
- 🟢 ておく (te oku) — "to do something in advance/preparation"
- 🟢 てしまう／ちゃう (te shimau/chau) — "to do by accident", "to finish completely"
- 🟢 てみる (te miru) — "to try doing"
- 🔴 出す (dasu) — "to suddenly begin to...", "to burst into..."
- 🔴 始める (hajimeru) — "to start/begin to..." (transitive) — only intransitive 始まる ("to begin, by itself") is covered
- 🟢 終わる (owaru) — "to finish/end..."

## 9. Time and Sequence Expressions

- 🟢 間 (aida) — "while", "during", "between"
- 🟢 間に (aida ni) — "while (something happens)", "during the interval of"
- 🟢 後で (ato de) — "after...", "later"
- 🟢 たばかり (ta bakari) — "just did...", "something just happened"
- 🟢 たところ (ta tokoro) — "just finished doing...", "was just doing..."
- 🟢 ているところ (te iru tokoro) — "in the process of doing..."
- 🟢 ていた (te ita) — "was doing..." (covered via the ている→てる casual-contraction table)
- 🔴 頃／ごろ (koro/goro) — "around", "about", "at the time of"
- 🟢 ながら (nagara) — "while", "during", "simultaneously"
- 🟢 までに (made ni) — "by...", "by the time..."
- 🔴 さっき (sakki) — "a short while ago", "just now"
- 🔴 やっと (yatto) — "at last", "finally", "barely"

## 10. Uncertainty and Sentence-Ending / Question Expressions

- 🟢 かどうか (ka dou ka) — "whether or not..."
- 🔴 かしら (kashira) — "I wonder..." (feminine/soft)
- 🔴 かな (kana) — "I wonder...", "should I...?"
- 🔴 かい (kai) — turns a sentence into a casual yes/no question
- 🟢 じゃないか／ではないか (janai ka/dewa nai ka) — covered via んじゃない (seeking agreement/confirmation)

## 11. Reason, Manner, and Purpose

- 🟢 のに (noni) — 1) "although", "in spite of", "even though"
- 🟢 のに (noni) — 2) "in order to..." (purpose, with a noun-forming verb)
- 🔴 のは～だ (nowa~da) — "the reason [A] is [B]"
- 🟢 かた／方 (kata) — "how to do...", "the way of doing"
- 🔴 から作る (kara tsukuru) — "made from...", "made with..."
- 🟢 にする (ni suru) — "to make/decide something into..."
- 🔴 に気がつく (ni ki ga tsuku) — "to notice", "to realize"
- 🔴 に見える (ni mieru) — "to look/seem/appear" — only the different construction が見える ("is visible") is covered
- 🔴 て／で (te/de) — conjunctive particle: "so", "because of"

## 12. Degree, Extent, and Emphasis

- 🟢 あまり～ない (amari~nai) — "not very", "not much"
- 🟢 なかなか～ない (nakanaka~nai) — "not easily", "struggling to"
- 🟢 全然～ない (zenzen~nai) — "(not) at all"
- 🟢 そんなに (sonna ni) — "so", "so much", "like that"
- 🔴 それでも (sore demo) — "but still", "and yet", "even so"
- 🔴 それに (sore ni) — "besides", "in addition", "moreover"
- 🔴 さすが (sasuga) — "as one would expect", "even [X] would..."
- 🟢 ぜひ (zehi) — "by all means", "certainly", "definitely"
- 🟢 すぎる (sugiru) — "too much" (excessive degree)
- 🔴 きっと (kitto) — "surely", "almost certainly"
- 🔴 急に (kyuu ni) — "suddenly", "quickly"

## 13. Comparison and Grouping

- 🟢 より (yori) — "than", "rather than"
- 🟢 の中で (no naka de) — "among...", "in..."
- 🔴 は～が…は (wa~ga...wa) — "[A] but [B]" (contrastive comparison)
- 🟢 とか～とか (toka~toka) — "things like A and B, among others"
- 🔴 また／または (mata/matawa) — "or", "otherwise" (choice between options)
- 🔴 まま (mama) — "as it is", "remaining unchanged"
- 🔴 も (mo) — "as many/much as...", "up to..." (emphatic quantity) — only indefinite-pronoun compounds exist, not this sense
- 🟢 し (shi) — "and", "and what's more" (listing reasons, emphasis)
- 🟢 しか～ない (shika~nai) — "only", "nothing but"

## 14. Volition, Intention, and Plans

- 🟢 意向形 (ikoukei) — volitional verb form ("let's do...", "I will...")
- 🟢 予定だ (yotei da) — "plan to", "scheduled to"

## 15. Other Verb-Related Patterns

- 🔴 がする (ga suru) — "to smell/hear/taste/feel [something]" — only individual sensory verbs (が聞こえる, が見える, が吹く, が降る) exist, not the general がする pattern
- 🔴 がる／がっている (garu/gatte iru) — shows a third person's feelings/appearance
- 🔴 ことがある (koto ga aru) — "there are times when..." (N4 sense) — only the N5 たことがある ("have done before") sense is covered
- 🟢 ことになる (koto ni naru) — "it has been decided that..."
- 🟢 ことにする (koto ni suru) — "to decide on..."
- 🟢 他動詞・自動詞 (tadoushi / jidoushi) — transitive & intransitive verb pairs

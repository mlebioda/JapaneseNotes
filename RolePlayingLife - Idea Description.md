## 1. Goals

### 1.1 Set up your life goals

Motivation comes and goes. Discipline and clear direction are what really move you forward.  
You might want to get fit, learn new skills, change careers, or improve your relationships — but without clear goals, effort often turns into frustration.

RolePlayingLife starts with one simple idea:  
**real progress begins with well-defined goals.**

Before you can start your adventure, you must define your quest.

And every good quest must be **SMART**.

---

#### 1.1.1 What is SMART?

SMART is a framework for setting clear, actionable, and achievable goals. Each goal should meet **all five** criteria:

- **S — Specific**: Clearly defined, focused, and unambiguous.  
- **M — Measurable**: Progress and completion can be tracked with numbers or clear indicators.  
- **A — Achievable**: Realistic given your current resources, skills, and time.  
- **R — Relevant**: Aligned with your long-term vision and priorities.  
- **T — Time-bound**: Has a clear deadline or timeframe.

SMART turns vague wishes into real missions.

---

#### 1.1.2 S — Specific

A quest must tell you exactly what to do.

Questions to ask:
- What exactly will I do?
- Why does this matter to me?
- What is included, and what is not?

Example:  
❌ *I want to improve my programming skills.*  
✅ *I will complete a C++20 concurrency course to improve my multithreading skills.*

---

#### 1.1.3 M — Measurable

Progress should be visible — just like an experience bar in a game.

Questions to ask:
- How much? How many?
- How will I know I’ve completed this quest?

Example:  
❌ *I will practice Japanese more.*  
✅ *I will learn and review 30 new JLPT N4 vocabulary words per week in Anki.*

---

#### 1.1.4 A — Achievable

Good quests are challenging, but not impossible.

Questions to ask:
- Do I have enough time and energy?
- Can I realistically do this alongside my other responsibilities?

Example:  
❌ *I will master all JLPT N2 grammar in two weeks.*  
✅ *I will finish 3 JLPT N4 grammar chapters per week.*

---

#### 1.1.5 R — Relevant

Every quest should move your story forward.

Questions to ask:
- Does this help me become the person I want to be?
- Does this support my career, health, or personal growth?

Example:  
❌ *Learn video editing while preparing for job interviews.*  
✅ *Practice technical interview questions to prepare for software engineering roles.*

---

#### 1.1.6 T — Time-bound

Deadlines create momentum. Without them, quests never truly start.

Questions to ask:
- By when will I complete this?
- What are the milestones along the way?

Example:  
❌ *I will build a portfolio project someday.*  
✅ *I will publish a Qt mobile app demo on GitHub by March 31.*

---

#### 1.1.7 SMART Goal Template

Use this template to create your own quests:

> **I will** [specific action]  
> **measured by** [clear metric]  
> **using** [realistic plan or resources]  
> **because** [why this matters to me]  
> **by** [deadline].

Example:

> I will complete 20 LeetCode medium problems,  
> measured by accepted submissions,  
> studying 4 problems per week,  
> because I want to be ready for technical interviews,  
> by the end of February.

---

#### 1.1.8 Checklist

Before accepting a quest, check:

- [ ] Is it clearly defined? (Specific)  
- [ ] Can I track my progress? (Measurable)  
- [ ] Is it realistic right now? (Achievable)  
- [ ] Does it support my long-term goals? (Relevant)  
- [ ] Does it have a deadline? (Time-bound)

When all boxes are checked, your quest is ready.

### 1.2 Realize what you need

Once your main goal is set, it’s time to plan your path.

In RolePlayingLife, you grow using **skill trees**, just like in RPG games.

- Break your goal into smaller skills.  
- Decide which skills unlock others.  
- Learn the basics first, then move to advanced levels.  
- Build multiple skill trees that grow different parts of your character.

Each tree affects your character stats: strength, intelligence, discipline, creativity, confidence, and more.

Example — **Gym Bro Class**:

- **Training Tree**  
  - Strength  
  - Endurance  
  - Mobility  

- **Knowledge Tree**  
  - Nutrition  
  - Exercise technique  
  - Recovery and injury prevention  

Both trees grow together.  
You learn correct technique before increasing weight.  
You improve diet to support harder training.

Smarter growth means faster progress and fewer setbacks.

And because life changes, your skill trees can always be updated.

---

### 1.4 Set up priorities

You cannot do everything at once — and you don’t need to.

RolePlayingLife helps you focus on what matters most right now.

- Main quests first.  
- Side quests when you have extra time.  
- Optional quests when you need variety and fun.

Progress comes from choosing the right battles.

---

### 1.5 Track your progress — stats and experience

Just like in games, progress should be visible.

- Experience points show effort.  
- Stats show long-term growth.  
- History shows how far you’ve come.

When progress slows down, you can see why and adjust your priorities.  
When you improve, you can clearly see the results of your work.

Nothing is more motivating than watching your character grow.

---

### 1.6 Discipline and consistency — powered by game mechanics

Big goals are achieved through small daily actions.

RolePlayingLife supports this with:
- Daily quests  
- Weekly challenges  
- Monthly and quarterly missions  

These systems turn discipline into habit and habit into progress.

But this is not about grinding forever.

Balance rules are part of the game:
- If you focus on your mind, take care of your body.  
- If you focus on your body, train your mind too.  
- Social and rest events are part of your character’s health.

A strong character needs balance, not burnout.

---

### 1.7 Share your journey with others

Adventures are better with companions.

- Share your progress with friends.  
- Compete in friendly challenges.  
- Support each other during difficult quests.

Looking for a job?  
Your progress log becomes proof of your discipline, skills, and consistency.

This is not a single-player game.

This is co-op mode for real life.
